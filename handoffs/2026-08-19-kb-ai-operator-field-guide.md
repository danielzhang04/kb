### Recommended content architecture

Use a project-based apprenticeship, approximately 12 weeks at 8–12 hours per week:

1. **Operator core:** workflow discovery, current-state map, baseline metric, deterministic-vs-AI-vs-agent decomposition, integration fundamentals.
2. **Revenue-ops rotation:** prospect intake/enrichment, qualification, CRM upsert/routing, controlled outreach, dashboard.
3. **Support-ops rotation:** intake/classification, context retrieval, response draft, escalation, SLA/quality measurement.
4. **Owned-media rotation:** use the YouTube workflow to practice research, production orchestration, verification, publishing, and analytics.
5. **Production and transfer:** schemas, idempotency, retries, permissions, human gates, logs, eval cases, incident/runbook, handoff.
6. **Career packaging:** one flagship real-user case study, two reference builds, short demos/teardowns, architecture and workflow maps, KPI evidence, resume bullets, and an interview narrative.

Keep the emphasis roughly **20% structured study, 60% building, 20% user validation and reflection**. Require one real external user or team whose workflow currently wastes at least several hours per week. Certificates are supporting material; shipped, measured work is the proof.

Recommended competency spine:

- Workflow diagnosis and business judgment
- APIs, webhooks, authentication, and systems of record
- SQL, data models, quality, and operational dashboards
- AI decomposition: deterministic code vs bounded LLM worker vs agent loop vs human decision
- Reliability, evaluation, security, and handoff
- Adoption, stakeholder communication, and measurable impact

De-emphasize model training, framework collecting, elaborate multi-agent graphs, and certificates without a real build.
