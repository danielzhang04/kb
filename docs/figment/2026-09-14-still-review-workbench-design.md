## One useful product slice

An authenticated operator opens one already-generated, already-graded persona gen plan, views its exact references and original images, records every required observation and deliberately applies one complete keep/cull ruling through existing authority. They can leave unfinished review without applying anything. Success reports **“Review applied: N kept, M culled”**; all-cull reports **“Review applied: all images culled.”** Neither implies checkpoint, batch, final-delivery or publication approval.

Support one server-configured published `creator-001` gen plan/revision, one gen run and at most 16 graded PNGs, on local Windows CPython. Exceeding capacity is an explicit unavailable state, not truncation. No actual eligible media gives an honest empty state. Synthetic implementation testing can proceed before media is eligible. No generation, grading/model invocation, checkpoint selection, video, renderer, assignment action or publication belongs in this slice; retain existing separate navigation links only.
