## Summary (15 lines)

1. The verdict is REJECT: the document is directionally strong but is not safe to build and spend from tonight.
2. Counts are 4 blockers, 13 high, 8 medium, and 1 low finding.
3. The fully automated N-account end goal remains visible in §1, the dashboard, and the account model; it is not yet carried through the build phases or acceptance graph.
4. P3 violates the explicit research-before-expansion rule while all three r15b video reports remain absent.
5. P3 also lacks the T2 operator approval that must precede every live pod create.
6. The 18-cell arithmetic is correct: `900 + 18×120 + 300 = 3,360 s`, leaving 240 s under 60 minutes.
7. At `$0.80/h`, 60 minutes costs `$0.80`; `--max-usd 0.90` is sufficient, and four invocation ceilings total `$3.60`.
8. The listed arc ceilings sum exactly to `$12.20`, but that total and the `$50` enforcement omit recurring network-volume storage.
9. The first eye gate is unsafe because the reused rulings schema cannot persist adult-read, garment-integrity, or real-person-resemblance verdicts.
10. `review_status`, lifecycle `state`, and human gate decisions need separate single writers and hash-bound durable records before resumability is credible.
11. The 72-cell plan needs a concrete 40-stratum coverage/allocation table, and its batch schema must represent four pod runs rather than one.
12. P0 fixes and every scorer used by P3 require fresh different-model review at the exact executed SHA before live use.
13. The mandate-complete LoRA still lacks full-body coverage and an operator-only owned-hardware path for the explicit-tier training portion.
14. Safe tonight parallelism is P0 ∥ P1 ∥ isolated P4b; P2 waits for P0+P1+QA review, P4 account/scorer work waits on its schemas, and P3 is last after research and approval gates.
15. Later work also needs corrected cadence authorization, training timeout/security fixes, disclosure preflight, and an explicit two-account isolation proof.

## Verdict

**REJECT.** Do not run P3 or treat P0-P4 as an executable build plan until findings 1-5 are resolved and re-reviewed. The first resubmission should: close the r15b research gate; add the pre-spend T2 gate; remove or fully ledger/delete the network volume; extend and independently review the safety QA schema; and split/resequence P0-P4 with explicit files, tests, reviewers, and dependency edges.
