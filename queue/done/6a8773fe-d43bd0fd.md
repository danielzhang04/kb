\## 5. Recommended mechanism change

Change the existing criterion in place at `visual-kit/visual-grammar.md:127-130,147`.

Old: “Only a genuine rearward mass beat uses the simplified crowd rig.”

New: “A crowd is authored in the primary scene clause as exactly N anonymous figures—or a narrow numeric range—X deep or single-file inside a named capacity-limiting boundary (behind a pane, between rails, through a doorway, along a pavement, on a far bank), with the near zone explicitly assigned empty; qualitative words such as ‘small,’ ‘restrained,’ or ‘rear-zone mass’ do not supply quantity.”

This is one construction criterion where crowds already live, not a new when/when-not rule.

Change the exemplar too: retain 5–6 legible rig figures, but arrange them as a two-deep bounded cluster in perspective behind glass/rails with dominant negative space and period-neutral clothing. Remove the edge-to-edge frontal lineup, top hat, and bonnet. The prompt should own count; the seed should demonstrate distribution, scale, and rig.

Lint can require, whenever `figures.crowd=true`: a numeral/range, a depth or queue term, a concrete boundary noun with a spatial relation, and an assigned empty near zone. The render critic can measure visible-body count versus declaration, boundary leakage, row depth, largest crowd-figure height, and whether figures form an edge-to-edge frontal wall.
