2. [lib/helpers/backend/startup_tasks.dart:120](/C:/Users/danie/AI-Operator-Program/pb-11/lib/helpers/backend/startup_tasks.dart:120) — Notification and intent listeners are deferred without the required readiness queue.

This creates observable event-loss windows:

- `IntentsService.init` does not subscribe until after reading the initial intent.
- `NotificationsService.init` establishes its database watcher later; its initial event only establishes `currentCount`, so messages arriving before subscription do not produce notifications.

Minimal fix: restore `await notif.init()` and `await intents.init()` before the frame until the owning services provide pre-frame capture plus post-frame draining. Add tests injecting an intent and notification during the readiness gap.

3. [lib/helpers/backend/startup_tasks.dart:132](/C:/Users/danie/AI-Operator-Program/pb-11/lib/helpers/backend/startup_tasks.dart:132) — The `updateChecks` task actually runs all of `StartupTasks.onStartup()`.

Minimal fix: split `onStartup` into correctly named operations. Preserve the timing of chat/socket, server-detail, and FCM work; defer only the update checks, with their failures routed through `DeferredStartupIssue`.
