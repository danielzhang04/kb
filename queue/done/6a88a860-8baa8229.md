\## The question
The owner fears "some images aren't matching the script". Sit in the viewer's seat: the narrator says `vo_text` (and the sentence around it in `script.md`) while this frame is on screen for ~2 s. Does the picture belong to the words? Judge each shot on:

Be strict on LOOSE vs MATCH: "the object appears somewhere" is not a match if the frame's dominant subject is something else.
