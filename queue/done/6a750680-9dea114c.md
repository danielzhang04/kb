\## The three worth Daniel’s attention

**Prefect** is the least awkward technical replacement. It could take over scheduling, retries and durable run state from [execution.ts](/C:/Users/danie/kb/dashboard/server/control/execution.ts), while the current Claude/Codex adapters remain shell-backed tasks. [queueBridge.ts](/C:/Users/danie/kb/dashboard/server/control/queueBridge.ts), [policy.ts](/C:/Users/danie/kb/dashboard/server/control/policy.ts) and [launch.ts](/C:/Users/danie/kb/dashboard/server/control/launch.ts) would still need to remain authoritative. Integration cost is medium-high: a Python orchestration boundary, Prefect DB reconciliation, JSONL-to-task-event projection and a second UI. The main losses would be git-native state, exact dashboard/terminal ownership, content-bound WebAuthn approvals, T1–T3 risk floors and credential/spend refusal unless kb reimplemented every one around Prefect. That makes Prefect a recovery-semantics benchmark, not an import.

**Temporal** is the durability ceiling. Its Workflows could replace the attempt/session/retry state machine in [execution.ts](/C:/Users/danie/kb/dashboard/server/control/execution.ts); Activities would wrap [claudeWorkerAdapter.ts](/C:/Users/danie/kb/dashboard/server/control/claudeWorkerAdapter.ts) and [agent_runner.ps1](/C:/Users/danie/kb/scripts/agent_runner.ps1), while Signals carried approval decisions. Cost is high: deterministic workflow/versioning discipline, another worker service and database, custom approval projection, and synchronization back to queue cards. kb would lose its single intelligible git audit trail and purpose-built agent cockpit without eliminating the compiler, policy or WebAuthn layers. Temporal becomes justified only if multi-day crash recovery and scale become real operational problems.

**Windmill** is the closest UI/HITL analogue. Its flows could replace much of [defs.ts](/C:/Users/danie/kb/dashboard/server/workflows/defs.ts), launch/execution plumbing and part of the dashboard, with Bash steps calling the subscription CLIs. That is still a medium-high migration plus secure mounting of Claude/Codex credential stores into workers. More importantly, Postgres would supersede cards as truth, and kb would lose server-owned action classification, immutable approval content, WebAuthn presence, the inert Evidence boundary and exact double-execution protections. Community approval links are also weaker than kb’s latch, while stronger approver controls pull toward paid enterprise features.

\## Steal-the-pattern

- Prefect’s typed approval/input schemas and resume forms for richer `HumanRequest` payloads.
- Temporal’s append-only event history, replay discipline and explicit activity idempotency.
- Windmill’s “release the worker while suspended,” non-self-approval and multi-approver semantics—implemented behind kb’s WebAuthn latch.
- Trigger.dev/Inngest wait tokens: correlated, expiring, one-use resume capabilities.
- Conductor’s assignee/group routing, approval SLAs and escalation timers.
- GitHub Actions’ compact waiting-job visualization and direct graph-to-log navigation.
- Claude Agent Teams’ local mailbox/shared-task primitives inside a single governed stage, without promoting them to platform authority.
