## Result

The regression is real, but it is not a general “prompts got shorter” failure. Three mechanisms line up with Daniel’s complaints:

1. scene-wide environment/style anchors were replaced by locally minted, sometimes unseeded first-place plates;
2. crowds and people were systematically replaced with empty evidence tableaux;
3. a forced-perspective workaround remains embedded in ten shot prompts after its doctrine was reverted.

\## 4. Review gate

Style-bible §3 itself is principally “**The rig checklist — channel invariants**”: head shape, nose/ears, hands, outline/render, identity, proportion, expression, and lettering; it explicitly says camera framing varies [style-bible.md](/C:/Users/danie/kb/orgs/faceless-youtube/channels/the-second-take/visual-kit/style-bible.md:106). The formal working-tree review gate is broader: it mandates three blocking axes—identity/rig, fidelity, and “**Style/taste**,” including palette, fore/mid/background depth, atmosphere, edge-to-edge richness, and rejection of generic/thin/sparse work [SKILL.md](/C:/Users/danie/kb/orgs/faceless-youtube/.claude/skills/image-generation/SKILL.md:262). `stamp_review.py` mechanically recognizes `fidelity`, `style`, and `rig`, and any non-clean axis parks the shot [stamp_review.py](/C:/Users/danie/kb/orgs/faceless-youtube/.claude/skills/image-generation/scripts/stamp_review.py:35). However, the round-2/3 lane logs record only “§3 rig verdict” plus authored-fact flags, repeatedly state that outputs are staging-only/unreviewed, and do not apply the fresh-eyes three-axis stamp. So a real style/composition gate exists, but these rounds had not passed through it; their logged “PASS” is not evidence of style or composition quality.

\## 5. Style-bible linkage

Current generation still has runtime style-bible linkage:

- Forge opens `style-bible.md`, extracts the locked, style-only, rig-hold, crowd, and base-rig descriptors, and prepends the appropriate descriptor to each request [forge.py](/C:/Users/danie/kb/orgs/faceless-youtube/.claude/skills/image-generation/scripts/forge.py:323).
- `shots.json` carries a `global_prompt_suffix`: “clean flat cel-shaded cartoon style … even medium-thick … outline …”
- VPW intentionally does not restate art style. Its rule is: “**Never art style, texture, or line weight — the global_prompt_suffix and the style bible’s forge descriptors inject those on every gen**” [VPW SKILL.md](/C:/Users/danie/kb/orgs/faceless-youtube/.claude/skills/visual-prompt-writer/SKILL.md:64).

Therefore the generic prose linkage was not lost.

What was lost was visual-reference linkage for scene-wide style. Before `6735796d`, image-generation’s seed law preferred:

> “target plate or prior frame in a chain > a `refs/env/` anchor matched by REGISTER … > an approved on-style scene.”

Afterward it says no cross-video ENV register exists and prefers the video’s own plate/prior frame or a STEP-1 figure. That linkage lived in image-generation’s seed law and Forge, not VPW. Only lettering/stamp hand exemplars remain as cross-video style references.

\## Ranked root causes

1. **Unseeded first-place plates replaced human-verified environment/style anchors.**  
   Strongest evidence: direct ENV seeds fell from **0.429/slate to 0/62**, while L55/L70/L94/L97/L100 explicitly ran with no image anchor and their children inherited them.

2. **Crowd/interaction authoring was converted into empty evidence tableaux.**  
   Strongest evidence: crowd declarations fell **13→1** across the 27 rewrites; 17 rewritten shots remove a human group.

3. **The reverted foreground-props/forced-perspective recipe remains in current prompts.**  
   Strongest evidence: ten current shots retain it, while their own notes still call it a “proven scale scaffold” after the decision log says the recipe was removed.

4. **Four-seed pressure makes style the negotiable slot when props/register assets are tagged.**  
   Strongest evidence: all tagged prop/environment assets enter the same capped array; prior run data records seven mandatory anchors lost through scratch-level truncation. Current Forge now fails instead of truncating, so this is secondary to the plate-first exception.

5. **The round logs’ PASS labels did not exercise the formal style/composition gate.**  
   Strongest evidence: the logs contain only “§3 rig verdict” and staged/unreviewed outcomes, whereas the actual blocking gate requires clean fidelity, style, and rig rulings.
