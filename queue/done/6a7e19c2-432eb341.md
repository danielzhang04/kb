**Severity: High**  
**Location:** [forge_codex.py:1077](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/orgs/faceless-youtube/.claude/skills/image-generation/scripts/forge_codex.py:1077), [p7_matched.py:279](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/scratch-codex-image-engine/p7_matched.py:279)

**Mechanism:** `run_item` scans `item.payload` before calling `compose_fn`. Only the built-in composer scans its composed output. There is no common post-compose validation for:

\### 2. `seed_cap_override` converts the reserved fifth slot into arbitrary content capacity

**Severity: High**  
**Location:** [forge_codex.py:1057](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/orgs/faceless-youtube/.claude/skills/image-generation/scripts/forge_codex.py:1057), [forge_codex.py:1079](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/orgs/faceless-youtube/.claude/skills/image-generation/scripts/forge_codex.py:1079), [forge_codex.py:1114](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/orgs/faceless-youtube/.claude/skills/image-generation/scripts/forge_codex.py:1114)

**Mechanism:** `seed_cap_override=5` feeds five arbitrary caller-supplied seeds directly into `prepare_seeds`; it does not require the fifth seed to be a register/style anchor or to have been added by `with_register_seed`. Because `tile_added` is false, the log also records `added_by: null`.

Thus the transport ceiling remains intact, but the doctrine cap of four content seeds does not.

**Demonstrated repro:**

- Default/`None`: five content seeds rejected at cap four.
- Override five: the same five content seeds accepted.
- Six at cap five: rejected.

- The arbitrary fifth seed receives no study-policy provenance.

The production CLI has no override flag, which limits reachability, but any Python caller of the production engine—including the P6/P7 drivers—can use this path.

**Suggested fix:** Remove the generic cap override; derive the fifth-slot allowance only from a validated, controlled style-anchor append operation and always log its provenance.

\### 5. Resumed reports can relabel old outputs with a new anchor/reference plan

**Severity: Medium**  
**Location:** [study_run.py:98](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/scratch-codex-image-engine/study_run.py:98), [p7_matched.py:330](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/scratch-codex-image-engine/p7_matched.py:330), [p7_matched.py:367](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/scratch-codex-image-engine/p7_matched.py:367)

**Mechanism:** On resume, completed result keys skip `generate_fn`, leaving `stats["refs_used"]` and `stats["ref_trims"]` empty. `build_report` then reconstructs refs, trims, and anchors from the current register and Forge derivation—not from what generated the stored PNG.

Changing the register or seed derivation between invocations therefore rewrites the report’s claimed provenance for already-generated results. The engine row’s actual `seed_sha256` paths are available but ignored for this purpose.

**Suggested fix:** Persist anchor, ordered roles/paths, and trims atomically with each result row, then compare them against engine-log seed evidence on resume.

\### 6. A qualifying dark accent can be discarded as “ink”

**Severity: Medium**  
**Location:** [p7_register.py:56](/C:/Users/danie/kb-worktrees/boss-codex-image-engine/scratch-codex-image-engine/p7_register.py:56)

**Mechanism:** The cluster nearest the darkest-3%-pixel mean is removed before the saturation/coverage accent predicate runs. A dark material accent contributing to that 3% can therefore be classified as ink and become ineligible despite meeting both accent thresholds.

**Demonstrated repro:** A synthetic frame containing:

- 1% black ink;
- 2% dark blue `#001e5a`, saturation 1.0;
- eight neutral fill clusters;

produced darkest-3% mean `#00143c`. The blue 2% cluster was selected as ink and removed, leaving no accent entry, despite coverage 2% ≥ 0.3%.

**Suggested fix:** Make ink and accent classification non-exclusive unless the removed cluster is independently established as line ink.
