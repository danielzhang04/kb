## Work order

[LOW] P4-B finding: on a cold load, the terminal client shows "Terminal unavailable" for 2-4
seconds before self-resolving — a mount/fetch race in `dashboard/src/App.tsx:244-260` where the
unavailable state renders before the fetch that would clear it has returned. Separately, a
reloaded live tab needs a manual click to reattach to its session instead of reattaching
automatically.

Fix: gate the "Terminal unavailable" render on the fetch having actually completed (loading
state distinct from unavailable state), and make a reloaded live tab reattach automatically
without requiring a click.
