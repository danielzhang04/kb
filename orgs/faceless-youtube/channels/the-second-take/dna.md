## Doctrine (universal.md §1a — one lever per channel, never per video)

- **Locked emotional lever:** **Vindication / forbidden-knowledge** — "here's what *really* happened / how
  they pulled it off / the part they don't tell you." Reward is epistemic superiority: the viewer performs
  the "aha." Curiosity-forward (the untold story) with vindication as the edge; Crayon's exposé register.
  Low-grade dread / schadenfreude only as *flavor* on collapse/scam stories — never the driver.
- **Named refusals:** No get-rich / hype / "one weird trick." No specific investment/tax/legal *advice*.
  **No finance lectures / teaching-forward "how X works" explainers — we tell the STORY and teach through
  it.** No doom-porn or ragebait. No cloning a rival's format or literal crayon look. No breathless
  trailer-voice (§1d-R). No story with no money in it — every video touches money.

## Format

- **Primary format:** long-form is the earner; Shorts are a **first-class** viral engine (our edge over
  the model channels — each Short is a self-contained money-mechanism payload, §11-0, not a teaser).
- **Target length:** **hard 7:30–9:30** pre-render estimate (VO words ÷ the voice's measured wpm;
  ruled 2026-07-30, hard floor AND ceiling, lint-enforced — supersedes the 2026-07-28 8–10 min
  ruling); **avoid the 16–24 min death zone**.
- **Shorts per long-form:** 3–5 (virality-forward; one surprising money fact each, closed in-short).
- **Cadence:** 1 long-form/week + shorts staggered every 2–3 days after (long-form ships first).
- **Recurring structure:** curiosity-gap cold-open (the surprising claim/number) → the setup (who/what/
  when in 15s) → the STORY walked beat by beat with real numbers + named characters (the con landing, the
  turn, the fallout); any "how it worked" made legible fast, in service of the story → mid-video re-arm
  (the twist that reframes it) → the withheld "what really happened" in the final 20% → dry close (a
  realization, never "and that's why you should…").

- **Script/voice register (locked — storytelling-first, register by gravity):** **plain-concrete-specific

  narrator (Crayon Capital / HeyHistorically register *as storytellers*), **not** dry-serious.
  **More comedic than any pure finance-or-history channel, without becoming a comedy channel** — good
  storytelling is the goal; the wit rides on it. Vindication delivered as *"here's what actually happened,"*
  with the joke riding the fact. No trailer-voice (§1d-R) — playful ≠ portent. **Casual, like telling a
  friend; every joke is a plain modern analogy the viewer instantly gets ("a startup with no product"),
  never literary flourish ("the load-bearing wall of the con") or a coinage nobody's heard ("a bug in him")
  — the humor bar in `storytelling-grammar.md §6`.**
- **Narrator persona (locked):** **the razor-smart storyteller** — a fast, wry, hyper-literate narrator
  who makes real money-history *fun*, **roasts the fools** (the con-man, the market, the institution —
  never the mark or the audience), and tells it like a story, not a lecture. Register = Crayon Capital +
  HeyHistorically *as storytellers*. Dramatized beats default to the narrator's **reported speech**
  ("he reportedly told them the vault was on a time lock"), characterized with attitude. Do
  NOT import funny character-voices, pure-bit jokes with no fact, or cringe/meme silliness that dents
  credibility. Full macro-architecture + register grammar: `storytelling-grammar.md`. Writes toward this
  person on every line (§1d-V/§5d).
- **Humor dial (locked — register set by topic gravity):** the comedic rate is **a DIAL set by the topic's
  gravity, NOT a fixed rate** (owned here). Money-absurdity / systemic farce runs **hot**
  (dense wit); human-villainy / ruin runs **wry and sparse**. Slide the
  dial to the beat you're on. The default register is **warm, irreverent, and loaded with pop
  culture** (a narrator plainly enjoying the story). Across the channel this lands **more comedic than
  any pure finance-or-history channel, without becoming a comedy channel** (storytelling first, the wit
  riding on it). Toolbox: **anachronistic analogy that *teaches*** ("jazz became the
  Wi-Fi of the 20s"), deadpan undercut, ironic re-label ("exorbitant privilege = legalized robbery"), comic
  false precision, absurd escalation capped by a pun, bathos, narrator-described personification. **Every joke
  carries a fact** (delete-test — lose the joke, lose the info). No solemn essay, no paper-conclusion
  close (grammar §3.5). **Franchise and meme references with staying power are licensed** (Thanos,
  Doctor Strange's sanctums, the idiot sandwich, a Peloton, Ocean's Eleven is the grade of reference to
  reach for); the bar is instant recognition and the joke has to HIT, never how recently it was coined.
  **Profanity is in bounds at the "ass"/"shit" grade and stops there; the f-word is not the channel.**
  Punch up at power and absurdity, never at the mark or the audience. **TTS-safe:** keep jokes that live
  in *fact + framing*; convert any needing performance timing/voices to narrator-reported or hand to
  visuals. Rules in §1d-V; full grammar in `storytelling-grammar.md`.

## Branding

- **Thumbnail style:** our own flat-cel 2.5D look — a **cast member or a personified money object as
  the hero** (faces are allowed and preferred), the one red `#d7402b` accent, and a persistent corner
  wordmark. The full rule set — one loud emotion, one big thing legible at 168px, punchline/verdict
  overlay text, the pointing accent, the 168px half-second test — lives in `visual-prompt-writer`
  Step 4, which derives every concept from `script.md` plus this file.
- **Banner / profile:** locked 2–3 color palette + accent; the illustrated wordmark.
- **Naming conventions for titles:** curiosity-gap **story** declaratives — "The [person] Who [did the
  impossible-sounding thing]" / "The [famous thing] That [hidden twist]" / "The [scam/collapse] That
  [outcome]". A promise the video keeps; no numbered lists, no question marks, no hype. **Never a
  "how X works" explainer title** (that's the lecture framing we rejected). **Familiarity anchor is
  mandatory (universal.md §3a):** lead with the biggest thing the audience already knows the story
  touches — a famous name/era/product/place or a universally graspable premise — and let the obscure
  specifics be the payoff (title against *Warren Buffett* or *the Backstreet Boys* or *"the richest
  country on Earth,"* never against an unknown proper noun like a company/person nobody's heard of).
  Weight this hardest while the channel is small.

## Guardrails specific to this channel (YMYL — not optional)

- **Education, not advice.** Explain what happens and why; never tell the viewer what to *do* with their
  money. No projections as certainty. Include the not-financial-advice disclosure line.
- **Two reputable sources** on any hard claim (rates, timelines, legal/financial mechanics); cite at the
  bottom of `script.md`; mechanics vary by year/state/institution — hedge and date them.
- No defamation of real named people/institutions; describe mechanisms, tell documented stories, don't
  accuse.
