[B-ROLL: a debit-card PIN pad, four zeros pressed one after another]
Here is how a bank employee opened an account a customer never asked for. [BEAT]

[B-ROLL: a new account form filled in under a real customer's name; a trickle of money moving into it]
First they opened it in that person's name. To make it look real and active, they moved a little of the customer's own money into it. Then they set the PIN on the new debit card to four zeros, so they kept control of it.

[B-ROLL: a fake email address typed into an online-banking signup; an alert quietly routed to nowhere]
And they invented an email address, sometimes their own, to sign the customer up for online banking, so any alert that might have tipped anyone off went somewhere nobody would ever see. Inside the bank, the move even had a nickname. Pinning. And it all happened because a teller needed one more product to hit the day's quota.
