[B-ROLL: a hand picking up a phone under a poster that reads ETHICS LINE]
Some Wells Fargo employees refused to go along with it. They called the company's ethics line, the number employees are supposed to call, to report accounts being opened in customers' names without consent. [PAUSE]

[B-ROLL: a termination notice; a personnel form following someone from one job door to the next]
In cases that later came to light, the people who called got fired. And on the way out, the bank sometimes filed a standard industry termination form in a way that followed them and made them hard to hire anywhere else.

[B-ROLL: a Labor Department order; a reinstatement and a dollar figure]
Years later, the Labor Department ordered the bank to reinstate one banker it had fired, and to pay about five point four million dollars, the largest award the agency had ever ordered for a single whistleblower. The line meant to catch the fraud had been catching the people who reported it.
