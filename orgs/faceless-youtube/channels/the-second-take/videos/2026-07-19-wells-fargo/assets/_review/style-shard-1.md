# STYLE/TASTE review — shard 1 (L01–L30)

Judge: fresh-eyes style/taste subagent. Bar = §6 committed visual recipe + the "gold" Poyais scenes.

### L01  (beat: hook | class: physicalized-imbalance)
READS AS BEAT: yes — bank teller counter with a paper/card tower over one small customer reads as scale-as-argument, though the customer is drawn large enough that the "dwarfed" imbalance lands softer than authored.
RECIPE: off — the entire paper tower and the card stacks are drawn in THIN PALE GREY linework with hairline ruled details, not the even medium-thick `#241a12` outline the figure and counter carry; two line registers in one frame.
RICHNESS: rich — teal counter run, cream panelling, fore/mid/background reads, filled edge-to-edge.
PALETTE: ok — teal/cream committed, single red on the OVERDUE slip, used semantically.
EXPRESSION REGISTER: fit — flat lidded deadpan on an ordinary opening beat is the correct restrained default.
VERDICT: FLAG
FINDINGS:
- [MEDIUM] The "impossibly tall leaning TOWER of account-opening forms" is rendered in thin pale grey outlines with hairline internal ruling, a visibly different line weight and ink from the `#241a12` figure/counter in the same frame.
- [LOW] The customer wears the base template's default brown hoodie + trousers — the one costume §1 reserves for the never-on-screen template — instead of a distinguishing "plain modern customer" outfit.

**Weaknesses first.** The batch does not hold one look. Three frames (L02, L03, L18) are staged in locations that belong to a different video entirely — an exterior park, a tropical river valley with palms and mountains, a fog-bound mangrove swamp — which reads as an unrelated style/environment anchor leaking into the seed stack and taking the palette with it; these are the most damaging defects here because the viewer is looking at Poyais while hearing about an American bank. Second, the lettering register is the batch's most systematic failure: the story's single recurring symbol, the '8', is drawn five mutually incompatible ways across L07, L08, L19, L20, L22, L23, L24 and L25 — bevelled drop-shadowed digital type, clean stencil, heavy shadowed display face, genuine marker — and only L19 and L25 are anywhere near the locked relaxed marker capitals. Third, richness is bimodal rather than uniform: L05, L09, L11–L14, L21, L26 and L27 are properly built and filled, while L04, L07, L08, L15, L16, L20, L23, L24 and L29 are objects floating on dead flat fields, with L16 the worst offender — clipart icons with drop shadows and an empty box on white, palette running to six unrelated hues. Fourth, the accent rule is broken in two opposite directions: scattered decoratively across the whole L11–L14 house chain (flowers, a picture dot, red-brown label ink, none of it semantic) and inflated into large red masses in L06 and L29. Fifth, character rendering is inconsistent shot to shot — L10, L15, L21 and L28 put nosed, haired, realistically proportioned figures on screen while L17, L27 and L30 hold the actual rig, so the same story roles are drawn as two different casts. Sixth, expression lands at both failure poles: uniform ecstatic grinning on ordinary beats (L10, L21), and completely absent faces on beats that need one (L11–L14 mouthless lead, L29 mouthless googly-eyed banker, L30 lead with no face at all). L15 also breaks its own four-frame chain by swapping the customer for a different person.

What is working: L26 and L27 are the two frames that show what the recipe looks like when it lands — committed muted palettes, flat-cel weathering and light, filled depth, a restrained legible face in L27's case. L09 is close behind on build quality. L05's boardroom is the richest environment in the shard. L25's tag-hung punchline is the sharpest editorial idea. Those are the reference points the rest of the batch should be brought back toward — but note that even the PASS frames carry LOW findings, and the bar for this channel is the human's reference grade, not this shard's internal best.
