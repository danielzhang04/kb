### L31  (beat: mid-arm | class: physicalized-imbalance)
READS AS BEAT: no — the prompt's whole argument is "a boulder marked '8' rolls DOWNHILL past executives onto a teller", and there is **no boulder, no '8', and nothing rolling** in the frame; three executives simply stand on a grey ramp.
RECIPE: on — flat cel, even medium-thick `#241a12` outline, built-but-flat boardroom (windows, curtains, skyline, chairs, counter).
RICHNESS: thin — the bare slate ramp is one enormous empty diagonal wedge eating roughly the lower-left 40% of the frame, exactly the "large flat empty wedge / dead air" failure.
PALETTE: ok — cool slate + cream + a warm teal counter is a disciplined 2–3 colour read; the only red is a tiny till element (semantic-ish, near-invisible).
EXPRESSION REGISTER: flat — all three executives wear the same heavy-lidded bored look while "stepping aside" from a threat that isn't drawn; nothing reads as pressure or evasion.
VERDICT: FLAG
FINDINGS:
- [HIGH] The shot's entire physicalized-imbalance premise is missing — "a heavy round boulder marked with the scorecard number '8' rolls DOWNHILL" is absent, so the frame reads as men walking down a ramp, not pressure rolling downhill.
- [MEDIUM] The empty slate ramp occupies a huge dead wedge with no furniture, texture, or depth incident — sparse against the §6 "no dead air / filled edge-to-edge" bar.
- [MEDIUM] The scorecard at top-left is a blank ruled grid with no marker lettering and no '8', so the causal chain (scorecard → boulder → teller) has no visible link.
- [LOW] All four visible faces carry the identical drooping-lid expression, flattening the register on a beat that wants at least evasion below and menace above.
