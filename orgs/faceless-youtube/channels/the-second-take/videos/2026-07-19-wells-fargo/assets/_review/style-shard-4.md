Judge: fresh-eyes style/taste subagent. Law: style-bible §6 (committed recipe), §4 (palette), §2 (locked descriptor).
Out of lane: rig invariants (noses/ears/digits/proportion) and lettering spelling — noted only as CROSS-LANE.

### L91  (beat: body | class: number-glued-to-object)
READS AS BEAT: yes — a wedge visibly carved off the big stack and slid toward the investor group reads "a slice set aside for investors" instantly.
RECIPE: on — flat cel, even `#241a12`-family outline, no bokeh/airbrush; the only softness is a mild ground vignette under the stack.
RICHNESS: thin — the top ~45% of the frame is undifferentiated cream with no environment, no fore/mid/background: a lone prop plus a group on dead air, exactly the failure §6 names, and it contradicts the prompt's own "filled edge-to-edge".
PALETTE: ok — slate/cream two-colour scene with the red confined to the carved cut face, semantic and single.
EXPRESSION REGISTER: fit — the investor group is on the crowd tier with flat neutral mouths, correct for a dry informational beat.
VERDICT: FLAG
FINDINGS:
- [MEDIUM] "no environment above the horizon" — the banknote stack and the investor cluster float on a blank cream field with no room, floor line, or backdrop, so the frame reads as flat pixel-art rather than a built scene.
- [LOW] the cut-face red is applied as three separate stripes on the wedge and the notch, which slightly dilutes the "one semantic red" read into decoration.

**Weaknesses first.** The dominant failure in this shard is **sparseness**: a large majority of frames are one or two props on a blank or two-tone field with no room, no light and no depth ladder — L91, L98, L99, L101, L102, L103, L104, L107, L108, L111, L113 and L114 all fail the §6 "rich, not sparse, no dead air" bar, and several of them run consecutively, so the sequence reads as a slideshow of stickers rather than a built world. The second failure is **lettering register**, which has come apart: L111 renders the sentence card in beveled drop-shadowed bubble letters (the exact extruded-lettering failure §6 forbids), L108 and L104 letter in BLUE ink in a display sans, L101 and L102 use a clean digital rounded font, and L92 uses a wet calligraphic brush — five distinct hands, none of them the locked marker capitals, and at least three in the wrong ink colour. Third, **all four stamp cutouts are off-register**: every one carries 3D beveled glyphs with an offset instead of the thin hugging `#241a12` contour, and the two FIRED FOR CAUSE / BANNED FOR LIFE marks additionally sit on a magenta field inside a baked red glow — flat matte, no glow and no gradient are all violated.

Beyond that: **two frames contradict their own script** — L107 stages the "exactly one person was charged, Carrie Tolstedt" peak on a generic bald man, and L108 writes in "GROSS MISREPRESENTATION" where the video's payoff is obstruction; **the recurring number motif breaks** at L106 (the 8 becomes 100) and the 8 glyph itself changes shape across L105/L117/L119; **continuity fails across four adjacent pairs** — L92/L93 (boardroom becomes a swamp), L93/L95 (the same swamp reused as a retirement send-off), L98/L99 (a talon becomes a human fist and the ground colours invert), L103/L104 (the same SEC drawn two ways); and **faces vanish** in L102 and L110, where figures render as blank featureless heads. Expression register lands weak overall: the one genuine peak (L107) is flat, and the close (L116) is over.

The frames that hold the recipe are L96, L105 and L118, with L115 and L119 close behind — all of them share the thing the rest lack: a committed scene palette, a built environment with a real depth ladder, and the red spent on exactly one thing.
