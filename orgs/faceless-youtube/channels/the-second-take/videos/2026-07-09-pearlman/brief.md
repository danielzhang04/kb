### ST-004 — The Backstreet Boys Were Built to Hide a Fraud
- **Format:** long-form (~12–15 min). *(Shorts skipped for this production batch.)*
- **Provisional angle:** Lou Pearlman manufactured the Backstreet Boys and NSYNC while simultaneously running one of the longest Ponzi schemes in US history — selling stock in a fake airline and an "employee savings" program built on forged financials and a fake German accounting firm. Lead with the *audit* mechanism (how a fake airline passed as investable for ~20 years), not the celebrity gossip everyone else leads with.
- **Payload promise:** The viewer learns the pop empire of a generation was partly financed by fraud, and *how* a business with fabricated books stayed "investable" for two decades (fake auditor, forged statements, affinity trust).
- **Lever:** vindication / forbidden-knowledge.
- **Key questions the video must answer (→ researcher's seed):**
  1. How did the "Trans Continental" airline / Employee Investment Savings Account program actually take money, and how much (verify $300M+)?
  2. The fake German accounting firm and forged statements — how were they used to reassure banks and investors?
  3. How did the boy-band cashflow interact with the Ponzi — was it feeding it, laundering it, or separate?
  4. What finally exposed it, and what was the sentence/recovery?
  5. The overlooked victims: who were the retirees/investors, vs. the famous band members' lawsuits?
- **Title options:** 1) The Man Who Built the Backstreet Boys to Hide a Fraud 2) He Made NSYNC. He Also Ran a Fake Airline You Could Invest In. 3) The Boy-Band Mogul's Half-Billion-Dollar Secret
- **Why original:** Big nostalgia/name-recognition demand for discovery; the fresh cut is the audit-mechanism ("how did no one check for 20 years?"), which the Netflix-style gossip takes skip.
