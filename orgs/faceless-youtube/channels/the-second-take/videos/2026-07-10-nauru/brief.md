### ST-008 — The Richest Country on Earth Ran on Bird Droppings
- **Format:** long-form (~12–16 min). *(Shorts skipped for this production batch.)*
- **Provisional angle:** Nauru, an 8-sq-mile Pacific island, sat on the world's purest phosphate (fossilized seabird guano), briefly became the richest place per capita on Earth, strip-mined itself into a wasteland, and blew the sovereign trust fund on catastrophic bets — including funding a flop London musical about Leonardo da Vinci. Do the forensic follow-the-money on the trust fund that the "bird poop irony" explainers all skip.
- **Payload promise:** The viewer learns how a one-resource sovereign fortune (~$1.7B trust) was quietly bled by bad advisors and vanity projects, and how the nation later rented itself out (detention camp, passport-selling) to survive — a case study in resource-curse + sovereign-wealth mismanagement, told as a national tragedy.
- **Lever:** vindication / forbidden-knowledge — but register skews tragedy/curiosity more than scam; keep vindication in the "where did the money actually go" thread. Comedy hot on the absurdity (bird-poop economy, the flop musical), OFF on the human cost (the island destroyed, the nation later renting itself out).
- **Key questions the video must answer (→ researcher's seed):**
  1. Where did the trust-fund money actually go — Air Nauru, phantom real estate, the da Vinci musical, other bets? Quantify the biggest losses.
  2. Who were the advisors/decision-makers, and how were the disastrous investments approved?
  3. The physical toll: how much of the island was mined out, and what did that do to habitability?
  4. The survival pivots (Australian detention centre payments, citizenship/passport schemes) — dates and dollar figures.
  5. What's the honest "lesson" without lecturing — resource curse vs. specific mismanagement?
- **Title options:** 1) The Richest Country on Earth Ran on Bird Droppings 2) They Had the Highest Income on Earth. Then the Island Ran Out. 3) A Nation Got Rich on Guano and Spent It on a Flop Musical
- **Why original:** The forensic follow-the-money on the trust fund is genuinely untold (others stop at the irony); whole-nation protagonist + bottomless "wait, what?"; viral-friendly premise.
