# Raw-versus-leashed preservation findings

1. **Raw:** “The company grew into the center of a system that handled more than 80 percent of the world's rough diamonds, meaning stones before they were cut and polished, for most of the twentieth century.”  
   **Leashed:** “The company grew into the center of a system that controlled more than 80 percent of worldwide rough-diamond supply for most of the twentieth century. Rough diamonds are the stones before they're cut and polished.”  
   **Supporting ledger:** [F-15] supports “controlled” and “worldwide rough-diamond supply.”  
   **Smallest correction available:** “The company grew into the center of a system that controlled more than 80 percent of worldwide rough-diamond supply, meaning stones before they were cut and polished, for most of the twentieth century.”  
   **Unrelated voice loss:** The correction split the raw narrator's running clarification into a separate glossary sentence, losing the conversational “meaning” joint and its one-breath buildup into the nightclub joke.

2. **Raw:** “Historical jewelers used markups of 100 to 200 percent on the diamond and setting, so the checkout price included a whole retail machine the second buyer wasn't paying for.”  
   **Leashed:** “Historical keystone markup added 100 percent to the diamond and setting, so the checkout price included a whole retail machine the second buyer wasn't paying for.”  
   **Supporting ledger:** [F-19] supports a historical retail keystone markup on the diamond and setting, but its 100%–200% range must be resolved to one flat figure.  
   **Smallest correction available:** “Historical jewelers used a 100 percent markup on the diamond and setting, so the checkout price included a whole retail machine the second buyer wasn't paying for.”  
   **Unrelated voice loss:** Replacing people who “used markups” with the abstract trade label “keystone markup added” makes the sentence more technical and removes the raw's conversational agent-and-action rhythm; resolving the range did not require that rewrite.
