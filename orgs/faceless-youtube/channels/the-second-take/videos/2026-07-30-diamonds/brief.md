### ST-013 — Diamonds Are Worthless. One Ad Made Them Forever.
- **Format:** long-form (~12–16 min) + shorts bench (provisional; shorts-writer finalizes from the researched long-form)
- **Provisional angle:** Diamonds are not rare and have almost no intrinsic resale value; the entire idea that a diamond is precious, and that a man should spend two or three months' salary on an engagement ring, was manufactured by De Beers, which hoarded global supply to fake scarcity and, in 1947, had an ad agency (N.W. Ayer, copywriter Frances Gerety) invent the slogan "A Diamond Is Forever." Do the forensic build of the con: supply control + demand invention, the single most successful marketing campaign in history.
- **Payload promise:** The viewer learns that diamond scarcity is engineered, not natural; that the two-months'-salary rule and the engagement-ring tradition were literally ad campaigns; and why you can't resell a diamond for near what you paid.
- **Lever:** vindication / forbidden-knowledge — "the thing on your finger is the most successful con in history." Register runs HOT (money-absurdity), universal appeal.
- **Key questions the video must answer (→ researcher's seed):**
  1. How did De Beers control global diamond *supply* to manufacture scarcity (the Central Selling Organisation / stockpiling), and roughly what share of the market did it hold at peak?
  2. Where did the engagement-ring-and-"two months' salary" tradition actually come from, and when (the N.W. Ayer / Frances Gerety campaign, 1938–1947)?
  3. What is the real resale value of a retail diamond vs. its purchase price, and why (the wholesale/retail markup + no liquid resale market)?
  4. How does the "blood diamond" era and the rise of lab-grown diamonds change the scarcity story now?
  5. The honest nuance: what part is genuine geology/cutting cost vs. pure manufactured value?
- **Title options:** 1) Diamonds Are Worthless. One Ad Made Them Forever. 2) The Most Successful Con in History Is on Your Finger 3) How One Company Convinced the World a Rock Was Priceless
- **Why original:** Backlog is all named-con stories; this is a *manufactured-value* mechanism con with universal reach, and the forensic supply-plus-slogan build differentiates it from the usual "fun fact" diamond videos.
- **Score:** 94/100 (payload 19, hook 15, lever 10, demand 14, monetization 14, diff 13, fit 5, feasibility 4)
