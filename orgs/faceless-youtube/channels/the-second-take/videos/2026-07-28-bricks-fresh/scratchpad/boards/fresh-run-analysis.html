Crowd recurrence and the remaining register gap

L07–L08, L16–L17, and L22 repeat the same mechanism behind parked L09 at lower severity: near-camera crowds acquire varied hair, smiles, eyebrows, or fuller faces. It is a subclinical L09 defect, so the appropriate response is a foreground-crowd rig mechanism fix rather than isolated retries. The larger gap is render register: LIKED consistently has paper/canvas grain, volumetric dust-beam light, painterly gradient shading on terrain and materials, desaturated mood, and metal/glass/gold sheen. FRESH has uniform fills and thin linework, with only L10–L12’s lamp wash, L18–L20’s vignette, and L23–L25’s sunbeams as partial atmospheric gestures.

How prompts changed (including Acts 2–4 / L26+)

Register delta. Liked prompts are directed scene briefs: a complete world, one readable relationship, a decisive camera, then the visible payload. They are not shorter—median 73 words overall and 87 on non-deltas versus fresh 61 and 73. Liked begins with scene framing on 150/214 shots and names a camera/vantage on 152/214; fresh is more cast/chain led and names vantage on only 63/245.

Suffix smoking gun. The liked full-run forge did not append its stored suffix: authored scene content closed the assembled prompt except where figure/rig clauses applied. Current forge appends an 80-word recipe after every payload. That makes the generic flat-cel, warm-bias, semantic-red, exclusion, and aspect recipe the provider-weighted tail—and the cleanest unisolated engine-input difference.
