## Findings

### 1. Rig defects
**None found.** Zoomed crops on L23 (shrug) and L19 (rake grip) both confirm 4-digit hands, no
5-finger or malformed-hand instances. All rig faces (pc-boxy, rival-pc, drive-maker, brick-foreman,
crowd) show no nose/ears, correct head proportions on-model against `base.png`/`pc-boxy.png`/
`rival-pc.png`. `rival-pc` vs `pc-boxy` differentiation at L16/L17 is clean — distinct silhouette
(tower vs monitor), distinct color (slate-grey vs cream), distinct vent-slot vs floppy-slot detail,
matching the canonical mint exactly. Named-cast costumes (drive-maker's cap+coat, brick-foreman's
shirt+tie) hold consistently across their multi-shot runs.

**Rig-clean: 25/25.**

### 2. Script-fit misses
**None outright.** Every shot states its VO beat as one readable idea, matching the L09/L11 standard.
One caveat, not a miss: L25's stencilled label sits on the box actually receiving the brick (open,
flaps up) rather than on a taped-shut box as the prompt literally specifies ("the carton in front of
him is taped shut and its top face now carries the stencilled words") — see Lettering finding below.
Visually it still reads the line correctly; flagged as a prompt-fidelity note, not a comprehension
failure.

**Script-fit: 25/25 HIT** (1 shot carries a fidelity caveat: L25).

### 3. Detail verdicts
No shot reads as bland/empty or noisy/cluttered. Figureless beats (L04, L13, L22) are deliberately
restrained per their own notes and still carry enough environmental storytelling (car+awning;
folders/tin/cassettes; the blank shutter row itself) to avoid feeling empty. Mass/crowd beats (L02,
L03, L08, L09, L15, L20, L21) run dense by design — that's the payload, not clutter.

One minor over-detail note: **L07** has a second beige monitor+keyboard in the foreground corner not
named in the prompt (which specifies a single "prop-beige-pc unit front panel turned to the far
side"). Reads as plausible shop-counter furniture (a till/display), not a rig or continuity error, but
it is an unauthored second beige-computer object sharing the frame with the named unit — worth a
sweep if a stricter one-object-per-shot standard is wanted.

**Too-bland shots: 0. Noisy/cluttered shots: 0. Minor unauthored-prop note: 1 (L07).**

prompt-instruction deviation, not a spelling or unrequested-text defect.
