## 2. Cross-variant ranking

**Per axis:**
1. Scale/composition: **vb > vc > va** — vb has zero un-parked scale failures and the most consistent execution; vc has the single best individual frames but also the two worst crowd-density frames in the whole set; va sits in the middle but its one failure (L09) was never caught.
2. Palette: **tie, vb marginal edge** — all three deliver legitimate warm/cool/mixed spread; va's edge is dulled by the L05 parked residual sitting on an otherwise warm palette frame.
3. Characters/crowds: **vb > vc > va** — vb is the only variant with a correct named-cast-in-front-of-simplified-crowd frame (L09) and no crowd defect anywhere; vc's crowd instinct is right but execution failed twice (caught, parked); va's crowd failed once and was **not** caught.
4. Depiction mix: **vc > vb > va** — vc's non-literal vocabulary (irony, physicalized imbalance, number-object, aftermath) is the richest and most story-led; vb and va lean more conventionally on plates/personification/crowd.
5. Chains: **va = vb > vc** — both va and vb have one legible chain; vc has none in this batch (N/A, not a fail).
6. Render register: **tie-ish, vb slight edge** — all three are substantively flat-cel and equally fail the liked bar; vb and vc each carry a little more tonal softness than va, but none touches painterly grain or atmospheric light-beam haze.
7. Lettering: **vb > vc > va** — vb has zero lettering-adjacent issues; vc is clean on this axis specifically; va carries the one parked red-beacon residual.
8. Review integrity: **vc (most self-critical) ≈ va (honest but missed one) > vb (cleanest but least tested)** — vc's 3 diagnosed parks show the hardest scrutiny; va's 1 park is honest but its crowd frame slipped through; vb's zero parks is either genuinely clean or a sign its harder calls (L02/L09 crowd individuation) weren't pressure-tested as hard.

**Overall: vb > vc > va.**
vb wins on consistency — no un-parked failures anywhere in its 12, the correct crowd pattern, the cleanest lettering, and marginally the least flat render. vc has higher individual highs (its best 4 frames are arguably the best scale/space frames in the whole 36) but its crowd instinct twice ran into a wall it had to park rather than solve, dragging its board average down. va is the weakest: comparable content to vb but denser/more object-cluttered on average, with one crowd failure that escaped review — the only true integrity gap found across all 36 frames.

## 3. Best and worst frames across all 36

**3 best:**
1. **vb/L06** — tiny computer at one end, tiny sprouting-circuit object at the other, of a single huge workbench under receding teal roof trusses. The single closest frame in the set to the liked-set "small subject in a vast structured world" gestalt (compare liked L24's warehouse corridor).
2. **vc/L06** — one computer alone on a cart in a cavernous basement store with receding columns and empty display alcoves; ~65% open air with genuine architectural depth.
3. **vc/L03** — a cracked trophy on a pedestal in a long curving, empty mall corridor; symbolic/irony class depiction with excellent depth and space, no figure needed to carry the beat.

**3 worst:**
1. **vc/L07** — the densest, most individuated crowd of all 36 frames (many distinct hair/hat silhouettes, wall-to-wall figures); correctly parked.
2. **vc/L01** — same defect class as L07, one register down in severity; correctly parked.
3. **va/L09** — a crowd of individuated, expressive front-row faces filling most of the frame width; the same class of defect as vc's two parked frames, but this one was **not** caught or parked — the single clearest review-integrity gap in the whole review.

## 4. Closest to the liked feel, and what all three still miss

**Closest overall: vb.** Its board is the most consistently airy and structured (L06 through L08, L11 in particular), its crowd/cast handling is the one instance across all 36 frames that matches the doctrine's intended named-cast-plus-simplified-background-crowd pattern, and it carries zero unresolved defects. vc's best single frames (L03, L06) arguably match or exceed vb's best, but a whole-board judgment has to weight in its two severely failed, if honestly parked, crowd frames — so on balance vb is the safer, more even recovery of the liked feel; vc is the higher-ceiling, higher-variance bet.

**What all three still miss, identically:** the render-register axis. Every one of the 36 frames read is flat-cel — hard uniform outlines, flat color fills, no visible paper/canvas grain, no soft gradient material shading, and no atmospheric haze or dust-mote light beams. The liked reference frames (L05, L09, L11, L24 in particular) all show a soft radial light-beam with visible dust particles, gentle gradient falloff on shadowed surfaces, and a slightly painterly (not poster-flat) tonal transition. None of the three variants touch this — they differ from each other only in content, staging, palette choice and crowd handling, never in render technique. This confirms the taste-ground-truth's 2026-08-19 finding that render register remains the dominant, still-unclosed gap: no variant may claim a full visual recovery on scale/palette/crowd wins alone.

## 5. Honest differentiators

**A vs B (the suffix is the declared only dispatch difference):** va and vb do not share shot content — they are different beats/compositions, not the same shot rendered with and without the suffix — so no clean pixel-identical A/B pair exists in this batch. Judged in aggregate, though, the suffix's presence/absence produces a real but second-order visual difference: vb frames consistently read slightly airier (more consistently generous open space) and carry marginally softer tonal falloff (TV glow in L01, roof-truss shading in L06, window wash in L11) than va's harder, more uniformly flat, more object-dense frames. This is consistent with the suffix acting as a mild "graphic poster" tightening force that, removed, lets a touch more natural gradation and spacing through. It is **not** a dramatic register change — vb is still solidly flat-cel and does not recover the liked painterly bar — but it is a visible, consistent lean in the intended direction. The suffix's removal also correlates with vb's cleaner crowd handling and zero lettering/content residuals, though this may be coincidental to which specific shots were drawn rather than a suffix effect per se.

**A/B vs C:** vc is a different doctrine track ("current doctrine + surgical fixes," not a restoration), and it shows in the depiction mix: vc reaches for irony, physicalized imbalance, number-object and aftermath classes that neither va nor vb attempt in this batch, which sit closer to the liked set's non-literal richness (compare liked L23's ironic "hard drive" box containing a literal brick). This is vc's real strength. Its real weakness, also distinctive to it, is that its crowd beats (L01, L07) are the most severely over-individuated and over-dense of any frames in the whole 36-frame review — worse than va's single crowd miss and further from the crowd-rig discipline than vb's crowd frames — suggesting vc's current-doctrine crowd handling is measurably weaker than the restoration track's, even though vc's non-crowd depiction range is stronger.
