## Q1 — Crowd staging (R1/R2)

### What the LIKED-era documents actually said

At the exact archived baseline, crowd distance/staging lived in both the authoring skill and the channel grammar:

- VPW `SKILL.md` Step 2 lines 68–72 `@30d2b7e8` requires a crowd's **positive rear zone** in the primary clause—far side of furniture, behind a divider, through a doorway—says never to rename a co-planar gathering “background-scale,” and says non-identity people are staged at **crowd scale**. Crediting the LIKED pixels to that short-lived sentence would therefore be a **guess**.
