Worker: R1. Date: 2026-08-06. Scope: bricks-fresh era doctrine, grey-desaturation drift.
Daniel's ruling this answers: **plates may render COOL, but must not render GRAYSCALE.**

effectively achromatic. `pct_chromatic` = share with S ≥ 0.15. Hue bands: warm = 330–65°,
cool = 165–280°, measured only over chromatic pixels.

### 1b. Resolution confound RULED OUT

The priors were 2K, the offenders 1K, so both priors were LANCZOS-downsampled to the offenders'
exact 1376x768 and re-measured. The gap survives essentially intact:

| file | mean_S | med_S | pct_S<0.08 | pct_chromatic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L28-prior downsampled to 1K | 0.205 | 0.181 | 18.5% | 53.6% |
| L03-prior downsampled to 1K | 0.302 | 0.299 | 3.8% | 65.3% |
| *(vs L28-retry1 fresh)* | *0.154* | *0.089* | *44.9%* | *30.7%* |
| *(vs L03 fresh)* | *0.202* | *0.091* | *37.0%* | *39.8%* |

**1K is not the cause.** Median saturation is halved-to-thirded (0.089 vs 0.181; 0.091 vs 0.299)
and the achromatic share is 2.4x–10x larger at matched resolution.

| file | pct_S<0.15 (neutral mass) | neutral-mass mean R−B (×255) | neutral mean S | pure-neutral share (S<0.05) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L28-retry1 (fresh) | **69.3%** | −11.2 (cool lean) | 0.075 | 12.9% |
| L28 prior @1K | **46.4%** | +10.6 (warm lean) | 0.087 | 12.3% |
| L03 (fresh) | **60.2%** | −2.0 (neutral) | 0.067 | 33.2% |
| L03 prior @1K | **34.7%** | −14.6 (cool lean) | 0.108 | 9.6% |
| scene-style-tile (exemplar) | **16.1%** | +6.6 | 0.089 | 13.7% |

**The L03 prior is the proof case for Daniel's ruling.** It is strongly COOL (41.8% of its
chromatic pixels in the cool band, neutral mass leaning −14.6 toward blue) and still holds
mean_S 0.302 / med_S 0.299 with only 3.3% achromatic. Cool and chromatic are not in tension —
the era proved it on this exact shot. What changed is not hue, it is **how much of the frame is
given over to near-zero-chroma pixels**: 34.7% → 60.2% on L03, 46.4% → 69.3% on L28.

So the defect is a missing **chroma FLOOR**, not missing warmth. Fixing warmth would fight
Daniel's ruling; fixing the floor honours it.

## 2. Mechanism — every style voice in the prompt argues for LESS colour; none sets a floor

The assembled prompt for a cast-free plate (verified by `forge.py gen --dry-run` on L28) is five
blocks, in this order:

1. **§2b STYLE-ONLY descriptor (head voice)** — "…**simple flat colours** with gentle soft cel
   shading, rounded friendly shapes, no realistic detail."
2. **§2c RIG-HOLD** — form only, no colour content.
3. **SEED-ROLES preamble** (generated by `forge.py`) — "The SECOND image is the channel's SCENE
   STYLE TILE — a register sample ONLY. It pins LINE WEIGHT, the outline colour (#241a12), the
   FLAT-CEL RENDER, and **PALETTE DISCIPLINE**, and nothing else. Take **NOTHING else** from it…"
4. **Authored payload** — L28: "Palette: beige, **steel grey**, **cool white** tube light."
   (Two of the three palette tokens are literally achromatic. Legitimate per Daniel; not to be
   re-authored.)
5. **`global_prompt_suffix` (tail voice — the position this provider weights hardest)** —
   "…**locked 2-3 colour scene palette** plus the single red accent #d7402b **used only
   semantically**…"

Read as one instruction set, the prompt says: use *simple* colours, take only *discipline* from
the exemplar, honour a palette that is 2/3 grey words, cap the whole scene at 2-3 colours, and
the one saturated hue named anywhere (#d7402b) is **forbidden except as a small semantic mark**.
Nothing anywhere states a minimum chroma. Grey is the unique solution that satisfies every
constraint simultaneously — the model is not drifting, it is complying.

Two specific faults:

- **The exemplar's chroma is explicitly not requested.** The style tile is the register's own
  colour authority and measures mean_S 0.407 with only 16.1% neutral mass — it carries exactly the
  chroma level the priors had. But the seed-role text tells the model to take "PALETTE
  DISCIPLINE" and "NOTHING else". *Discipline* is a restraint word: it transfers "few colours,
  no rainbow", the opposite of what the tile is being shipped to supply. The one chromatic anchor
  in the request arrives with its chroma opted out.
- **The doctrine's anti-grey rule exists but never reaches the provider.** `style-bible.md` §4
  already states, in the channel's own words, "**Neutral-grey-only is not a palette.**" That
  sentence lives in the human-facing palette table and is injected into no prompt. The rule is
  written; it just is not wired to either style voice.

This also explains why the era never needed the rule: era shots were ~93% authored-warm, so the
authored payload supplied the chroma the doctrine omitted. The fresh file authors cool — which is
now legitimate — and with the payload no longer carrying chroma, the omission became visible.
The register has no floor of its own.

## 3. The edit

Two rewordings of EXISTING doctrine sentences. No new rule, clause, lint, or checking layer was
added; no `shots.json`, no `registry.json`, no re-authored `Palette:` line. Both edits give the
register a chroma floor **without mentioning warmth**, so an authored-cool scene stays cool.

### Edit 1 — `visual-kit/style-bible.md` §2b STYLE-ONLY descriptor (the HEAD voice)

The word "simple" was pulling toward restraint in the one sentence that describes colour. It now
carries the floor that §4 already states in prose ("Neutral-grey-only is not a palette") but never
delivered to a prompt.

**Before**
> …outline on everything, **simple flat colours with gentle soft cel shading**, rounded friendly
> shapes, no realistic detail. No text, no words, no labels.

**After**
> …outline on everything, **flat colours laid down at FULL cel strength — every fill a real colour,
> and any grey or neutral clearly TINTED warm or cool, so a cold scene reads COLD-COLOURED and never
> drains to greyscale — with gentle soft cel shading**, rounded friendly shapes, no realistic detail.
> No text, no words, no labels.

Greys stay legal (L28's authored "steel grey" survives); they just have to be *tinted* greys rather
than neutral ones, which is what the priors actually did (§1c: prior neutral mass leans R−B +10.6
on L28, −14.6 on L03 — tinted in both directions).

### Edit 2 — what the style tile GRANTS (one rule, in its two homes)

`visual-kit/style-bible.md` §5:

**Before** — "it contributes **line register and palette discipline ONLY**, never content, layout,
or the place it depicts"
**After** — "it contributes **line register and palette SATURATION (the strength its flat colours
are laid down at, which the new frame matches in its own hues) ONLY**, never content, layout, or
the place it depicts"

`.claude/skills/image-generation/scripts/forge.py`, the `STYLE_ANCHOR_ROLE` branch of
`seed_roles_text()` (~line 1166) — the string derived from that doctrine sentence:

**Before** — "It pins LINE WEIGHT, the outline colour (#241a12), the FLAT-CEL RENDER, and **PALETTE
DISCIPLINE, and nothing else.** Take NOTHING else from it…"
**After** — "It pins LINE WEIGHT, the outline colour (#241a12), the FLAT-CEL RENDER, and **PALETTE
SATURATION — match how strongly its flat fills are coloured and lay THIS frame's own hues, warm or
cool, down at that same strength; a frame drained to neutral grey has failed to take the
register.** Take NOTHING else from it…"

An amended provenance comment above the string records why the era's original "palette discipline"
wording was changed. Note the phrasing grants *strength*, not *hue* — "warm or cool" is explicit,
so the tile (which is 83.6% warm) cannot drag an authored-cool scene warm.

### Test assertions updated (part of the change, not an addition)

`test_forge_style_tile.py` holds both changed strings as deliberate drift-locks:
- `ERA_STYLE_ONLY` literal (line ~40) — the §2b drift-lock, updated to the new §2b text.
- `test_the_tiles_role_prose_grants_register_only_and_denies_the_place` — `"PALETTE DISCIPLINE"`
  → `"PALETTE SATURATION"`. Every other required token in that assertion is unchanged, so the
  test still proves the tile grants register-only and still denies the place.

## 5. Probe — ONE cast-free plate, 1K, fix live

Generated as `_staging/L28-r1probe.png` — a name no file used, and `forge.py`'s
`_reserve_staging_output` refuses to overwrite an existing `_staging/*.png` without `--force`, so
`L28-retry1.png` and `L28.png` were structurally untouchable. **Nothing was archived or moved.**

| metric | offender `L28-retry1` | **probe `L28-r1probe`** | era prior `L28` @1K | style tile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean_S | 0.154 | **0.247** | 0.205 | 0.407 |
| **med_S** | 0.089 | **0.189** | 0.181 | 0.409 |
| p90_S | 0.331 | **0.493** | 0.398 | 0.611 |
| **pct_S<0.08** (achromatic) | 44.9% | **18.7%** | 18.5% | 5.6% |
| pct_S<0.15 (neutral mass) | 69.3% | **38.9%** | 46.4% | 16.1% |
| pct_chromatic | 30.7% | **61.1%** | 53.6% | 83.9% |
| pct_warm | 29.4% | 56.9% | 52.7% | 83.6% |
| pct_cool | 1.2% | 3.9% | 0.6% | 0.0% |
| mean_V | 0.647 | 0.667 | 0.633 | 0.689 |

**Verdict: PASS.** The probe is measurably out of grayscale and lands at or slightly above the era
prior on every chroma axis. Median saturation more than doubled (0.089 → 0.189) and the achromatic
share more than halved (44.9% → 18.7%), landing on the prior's 18.5%. Brightness is unchanged
(mean_V 0.647 → 0.667), so this is added chroma, not a darker or a hotter frame.

By eye the probe reads as warm timber benches and tan cartons against **cool-tinted** blue-grey
steel shelving, ceiling and tube light — cool and chromatic at once, which is exactly the state
Daniel's ruling asks for and which the L03 prior (§1c) proved the era could reach. The offender by
contrast is a neutral grey-white floor and walls end to end. Lettering "MINISCRIBE" is spelled
correctly and legible, the frame is cast-free, and the authored place facts (shelving left, roll-up
door rear, tube lights, plank board stage-left) are all present.

**Honest caveats — neither caused by this edit, and not iterated on (one probe, per brief):**
- Both the probe and the offender render the roller door as a fine repeated slat field, which §3
  `line-register` calls a FAIL for micro-pattern fields. **Pre-existing in the offender too**, so it
  is a separate defect from the chroma one and not evidence for or against this fix.
- The probe stages the benches as a rounded-corner counter running centre-to-right rather than two
  long benches receding stage-right; the offender staged that more literally. Framing variance
  between two gens of the same prompt, unrelated to chroma — flagging it for the boss, not fixing it.
