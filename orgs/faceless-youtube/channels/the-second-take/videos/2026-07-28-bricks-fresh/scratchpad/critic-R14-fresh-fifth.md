## BLOCKING

### B-1 · `L24` · Q4/Q1 — the delta unwraps cartons its own parent sealed in film

`L22` reserves exactly ONE carton: *"the clear film is drawn hard round the rear rows but is cut back and
peeled clear of **the front carton** on the top row, which sits loose and unwrapped on the stack."* `L23`
opens that carton — feasible, and the `L22` note is explicit that this reservation is why it was rewritten.
Then `L24` changes *"**every carton along that whole top row** now stands with its flaps folded open"* — on a
parent whose stated film state covers every carton on that row except one. The delta therefore needs TWO
changes (strip film, then open flaps), the first of which the parent's own prose forbids. The feasibility
gate the author installed at `L22` is defeated one shot later, on the chain that carries the video's biggest
reveal.

**Fix direction:** either author the whole top row unwrapped in `L22` (the film "drawn hard round the rear
rows" only), or narrow `L24`'s change to the cartons `L22` actually freed.

## REVISE

### R-1 · `L35` `L36` `L38` `L39` · plan-level — the money block is one image four times
Four shots inside five cuts are all *stacks of flat brown drive cartons on grey concrete apron under a pale
sky*: `L35` four stacks stepping up · `L36` one stack with a number on it · `L38` a pallet beside a giant
crate · `L39` two stacks with numbers on them. Three of the four carry a literally identical palette line
("brown board, grey concrete, pale sky"), and `L36`/`L39` are near-duplicates — a lettered carton stack, then
two lettered carton stacks. The `shot_class` column says the classes vary (crowd-multiplication,
number-glued-to-object, physicalized-imbalance, number-glued-to-object); the **frames** do not. This is
MiniScribe's triumph — 12.6 s of the act — rendered as one idea repeated.
**Fix direction:** re-derive at least two of them into different worlds (the class table's countable mass, a
diegetic dateline/media frame, or the deal's human side); keep the carton-stack vocabulary for one.

### R-2 · `L19` · Q1/Q8 — the money is staged in the wrong party's room
The prompt opens *"A plain back stockroom **behind that display**"* — anchoring the frame to `L17`'s retail
shop window. But the VO is *"the hard drive **manufacturers** were quietly raking it in"*, and the script's
whole point is that the money left the shopfront fight and went to a different company. A back room of the
computer SHOP is the retailer's till, not the drive maker's. Only "a pallet of drive units" argues otherwise,
and it argues weakly. A viewer following the picks-and-shovels thesis reads the wrong party getting paid.
**Fix direction:** cut the "behind that display" anchor and stage the room as the drive maker's own shipping
back-of-house (its own dock, its own product, no retail adjacency).

### R-3 · `L42` · Q1 — an EMPTY carton contradicts the mechanism the video just taught
`L23`/`L24` spend three shots establishing that these boxes contain **one red clay brick filling the box
exactly**. Nineteen shots later `L42` presents *"its lid flaps standing fully open on an interior that is
**entirely empty**, bare board visible inside."* The intended read is "the number is hollow"; the read the
audience actually has been trained for is "the boxes have bricks in them", so an empty one lands as a
continuity error, not a symbol — and it is also factually the wrong fraud (MiniScribe's boxes were never
empty).
**Fix direction:** keep the smug rep and the '600 MILLION' lettering, put a brick in the box (or a stack whose
count visibly does not match the lettered figure).

### R-4 · `L29` `L31` `L32` · Q3/whole-list — the personification reflex
Three of the act's four figure reveals run the same recipe within six cuts: `expr-delighted` + a standing
whole-body action + *"Framing: medium-wide, him [centre] … with the floor deep behind"* + the same
beige/steel/tube-light palette line. `L29` and `L32` are near-identical compositions (same character, same
expression, same scale, same background logic) three cuts apart, and `pc-boxy` at `L11` is a fourth
`expr-delighted`. The register dial reserves strong faces for beats that warrant them; here one face is doing
four different jobs (arrival, founding, boom).
**Fix direction:** vary scale and angle on `L32` (it is the boom — go low/wide or push in), and split the
expressions across the dial's range.

### R-5 · plan-level cadence — the BASE is cut shortest, the DELTA held longest, in 3 of 8 chains
`drive-vault` 1.5 s base → 2.7 s delta · `shopfront-brawl` 2.2 → 3.0 · `backroom-take` 1.6 → 2.5. In each,
the frame carrying a brand-new set (the reading cost) sits at or near the cadence floor while the frame
carrying the smallest change gets the act's longest hold. `L12` is the worst instance: 1.5 s to establish a
records room, a plinth, a drawer wall AND a novel conceit (the drive as a vault). The five chains that get
this right (`store-1983`, `brick-tease`, `inflation-board`, `den-1983`, and the L22 chain) show the shape.
**Fix direction:** move ~0.5–0.8 s from each of `L13`/`L18`/`L20` onto `L12`/`L17`/`L19`.

### R-6 · `L12`–`L15` · Q1 — the drive's scale is unpinned, and the payload depends on it
`L12` gives the unit no scale anchor ("stands alone on a dark plinth", "air above it"). `L14` then fills its
interior with *"upright manila folders, their tabs staggered"* and *"flat boxed program sleeves stood on
their spines"* — a volume far larger than a drive unit implies. Deltas inherit the base's rendered scale, so
whatever `L12` returns is locked for the whole chain; if it returns a desk-object-sized drive, `L14`'s two
nouns (the shot's entire payload — folders for "files", sleeves for "applications") will not be readable at
2.5 s.
**Fix direction:** pin the unit's scale against something in `L12` (the plinth's height, the drawer wall's
courses) at a size where shelved folders read.

### R-7 · `L07` · Q4/cadence — the act's largest transformation, on the emptiest parent, at the cadence floor
The change is *"a queue of eager buyers **now packs the room**"* — a room-filling crowd arriving on a delta
whose parent frame (`L06`) contains no people at all. Forge does seed the crowd exemplar on delta beats, so
this is not a mechanism failure; it is the file's weakest seeding shape, because the dominant input (the
parent image) actively contradicts the payload, and the cap-displacement rationale ("the place frame carries
the rear crowd mass") is false here. It is also given 1.5 s, the shortest hold in the act.
**Fix direction:** re-author as a stage base (a fresh shop-with-queue frame), or lengthen the hold and state
the queue's occupancy zone as a positive arrangement rather than "packs the room".

### R-8 · `L08` · Q4 — the delta deletes the parent's dominant visual mass
The change is *"all three shelf bays now stand bare to the pegboard, every hook empty and every shelf edge
swept down to bare board"* — removing the largest seeded object mass in the parent frame (three bays stacked
with boxed machines, established at `L05` and re-carried through `L06`/`L07`). The completion-state wording
is the correct mitigation and is present; the risk that survives it is that the parent image, which forge
weights heaviest, shows those bays full.
**Fix direction:** consider re-basing this frame off the pre-stock ancestor rather than deleting through
three deltas of accumulated shelf pixels.

### R-9 · `L03` · plan-level — the designated opening peak is under-held and under-scaled
The skeleton (§6) names `L03` the **opening peak**: the plate does the hook's work. As written it gets
**1.8 s**, is framed *"wide from floor level, the lit pallets **small in a large dark room**"*, and closes on
`Palette:`. That asks a first-time viewer to find a small lit thing inside a mostly-black wide, absorb it as
the video's hook, and move on, in under two seconds. Every other tease frame in the act gets more time for
less work (`L22` 2.9 s, `L21` 2.9 s).
**Fix direction:** hold to ~2.5–2.8 s and/or bring the pallets up in frame so the payload reads at speed;
close on the pallets, not the palette.

### R-10 · `L30` · Q3 — a lettering-technique word contradicts the locked register
*"**Chalked** across the end of the nearest crate, a date reads '1980'."* The `global_prompt_suffix` already
injects the marker-capitals register, and the grammar bans lettering words in prompts. This is the exact
defect the author caught and fixed twice elsewhere in this same file (`L36` and `L39` notes: "'stencilled'
replaced with 'lettered'"); `chalked` was missed. Secondary, milder: "a broad **painted** board carries
'MINISCRIBE'" at `L28`/`L29`.
**Fix direction:** state the substrate, not the technique.

### R-11 · `L03` `L04` `L21` · Q3 forced row — the three shots where the palette template costs the payload
Of the 24 unlettered non-delta shots that close on `Palette:`, these three bury a payload that needs the
closing weight: `L03`'s three lit pallets (the hook), `L04`'s *"not one head turned toward the racks"* (the
entire irony of "never heard of"), and `L21`'s *"one beige drive unit standing among them on the counter, out
of place and unremarked"* (the shot's declared joke, sitting mid-prompt with sellers, prospectors, framing
and palette after it).
**Fix direction:** move `Framing:`/`Palette:` ahead of the payload clause on these three, as the lettered
shots already do.

### R-12 · `L18` · Q1 — the payload clause states two incompatible geometries
*"two flat black glass slabs now stand **butted front to front** at the front edge of the platform in a shove
of their own, **edge pressed to edge** and leaning into each other with a matching dent where they meet."*
Face-to-face flat slabs read as one flat object (no shove visible); edge-to-edge leaning reads as a teepee.
The generator gets both instructions in the closing clause of the act's longest hold (3.0 s).
**Fix direction:** pick one contact geometry and let it mirror the cases' lock-up above it.

## What is working (stated so the edit pass does not undo it)

- **Class variety is genuinely wide** — 13 distinct `shot_class` values across 47 shots, no reflex class:
  literal 8 (17%), ironic-counterpoint 6 (the signature move, correctly the second-commonest),
  diegetic-device 5, symbolic-stand-in 5, crowd-multiplication 5, personified-character 4, idiom-pun 4,
  staged-interaction 3, and six classes at 1–2. **Non-literal share 83%**, and every one of the 8 literals is
  a concrete physical object or action — the one place literal is correct.
- **Payload ordering on lettered shots: 10/10 clean**, against 0/9 on 2026-08-04. Deltas 13/13 clean.
- **Disclosure order holds.** The bricks are absent from every frame before `L23`, and land ON "the title
  gives it away"; Singapore is deliberately left unlettered at `L26`; `L30` withholds the founder until the
  words that name him at `L31`. The plate/reveal seam at `L28`→`L29` is the doctrine executed exactly as
  §2 describes it.
- **Cadence band is respected** — every hold 1.5–3.0 s, mean 2.20 s, no hold over 3.0 s, no run of
  conspicuously equal durations, and no cut so fast its payload cannot be read except where flagged (`L07`).
- **No unauthorable mechanism.** Every action beat is staged as a held tableau: the winged box hangs "like a
  museum piece", the cliff is aftermath not a fall, the forklift is explicitly "stopped", the rake lies in the
  heap. Nothing in the act needs in-frame motion to mean what it means.
- **No crowd-rig boilerplate in any prompt**; absence is authored positively throughout ("blank and
  unlettered", "entirely empty, bare board visible inside", "plain and unmarked").
