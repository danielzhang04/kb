### 3 most re-usable motion mechanics (tied to beat type)

1. **Stat beat = handwrite the sentence word-by-word paced to the VO while the literal subject grows in from a frame edge** (E10: "over 80%… public housing" writes on as HDB blocks rise and fill the skyline; E9 variant: the bar physically overtakes on "exceeded"). The number is never popped as a card — it is written at speech pace and the scene *becomes* the statistic.
2. **Section/chapter seam = a recurring "board" set revisited per section: hard cut (or whip-zoom) to a dim pinned item, light it up to color, write its ALL-CAPS label on, with a measured music sting on the cut** (E4 Problem #1, E11 Problem #5). Cheap, structural, and it turns chapter navigation into a progress display.
3. **Held-set evolution by in-world agent, cut only on idea change** (E12: the character erases and redraws the board live over a ~9s hold; E7: thought-bubble icons pop in one per spoken list item on a locked camera). Within a beat, elements move/pop ON the held set — the hard cut is reserved for the next idea, so continuity and cut-energy each do exactly one job.
