## Per-chunk rollup

- **Median hold (measured):** 2.99 s (p25 2.0 / p75 5.0); ad segment slower (3 of 7 longest holds), editorial content faster.
- **% sampled holds with camera motion:** ~55% (7 of 13 held scenes showed deliberate camera: slow push E3/E7/E9-soldier, pull-back E5/E8, pre-cut push E1/E4). Motion is SLOW (1–2%/s) and one arc per scene; several scenes fully locked (E6, E10, E11 map).
- **% sampled holds with element motion:** ~100%. No dead frames observed: idle = character micro-motion (mouths/arms/head turns/typing), smoke/charts morphing, handshake loops, bg spiral drift.
- **Entrance vocabulary counts (sampled):** type-on letter-by-letter ×4 (chapter card, act card, speech bubble, subtitle) · pop fully-formed ×3 (labels, panel contents, caption) · fly-in/tumble ×4 waves (E12 props) · draw-on ×2 (logo wordmark, dashed connection arc) · panel wipe/slide ×5 (E7) · prop-reveal unroll ×1 · color-fill ×1 · staged-at-cut ×2 (character, brand logo). **Cut-to-staged is the default for characters/props; animated entrances are reserved for text and enumeration items.**
- **Transition inventory:** hard cut dominant (127 in 7:39). Non-cut devices found: black title/act card held in a narration gap (2×: 0:24, 3:37) · fade-up-from-black ~0.4 s after a black card (1×) · explosion→whiteout→fade-from-white ~1.5 s (1×) · in-frame panel wipes (1 sequence). No dissolves between ordinary scenes.
- **Charts/maps behavior:** UI slates arrive COMPLETE at the cut; internals then live-morph continuously (area charts re-shape, network graphs re-arrange, maps redraw) — "living data" idle instead of one draw-on ceremony. Maps use color-fill (tan→red) as the emphasis beat, synced to the spoken names.
- **Type observations:** one casual marker/handwriting family carries ~everything: floating labels = CAPS white + dark outline; captions = mixed-case white + outline, type-on synced to VO; list items get a leading hyphen and type on; act/chapter cards = same face, white on black; in-world prop text (nameplate, scroll, passport) replaces floating labels whenever a prop can carry the words; brand wordmarks reproduced in their real style (Palantir, IQT, Google, PayPal).
- **3 most reusable motion mechanics:**
  1. **Live enumeration on a held set** (E12) — each spoken list item = one asset class flying/popping onto the SAME composition, one ping per arrival; serves any inventory/list beat. Direct evidence for **layer-move over cut-to-changed-state**.
  2. **Pull-back reveal with ease-out** (E8) — open tight on the detail, one decelerating zoom-out to reveal the second party/context exactly as the narration completes the thought; serves irony/context-reveal beats.
  3. **Act-boundary ritual** (E2/E11) — music drops → black card, marker type-on, held in a narration GAP → hard cut to a date/map establisher whose color-fill lands on the resumed VO; serves chapter boundaries.
