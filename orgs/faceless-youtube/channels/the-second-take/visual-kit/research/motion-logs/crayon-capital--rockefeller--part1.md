## Per-chunk rollup

- **Median hold 3.0s** (p25 2.0 / p75 4.0); only 5 holds >8s in 10 minutes, and every one is continuously animated (zoom-out diagram, character action, ad screencaps) — the grammar has no dead holds at all.
- **Camera motion during sampled holds:** 5 of 14 distinct sampled holds (~36%) — slow zoom-out (E01), lateral tracking (E02), long diagram zoom-out (E07), fast looping pan (E14), micro zoom-in on insert (E03). The rest are DEAD-STATIC camera. Crayon's default hold = static camera; camera moves are purposeful (establish, travel, diagram-reveal, scale-gag), never idle drift.
- **Element motion during sampled holds:** 12 of 14 (~86%) — character limb/pose animation, pen write-on, money growth, text swaps, ambient life (wagons, smoke, sparkles). Static-camera + animated-elements is THE house idiom. Chapter cards are the only fully static holds (~2s each).
- **Entrance vocabulary counts (editorial only):** pop fully-formed ×6 (dialogue lines, diagram labels/banners, 1-2-3 buttons); typewriter/char-by-char ×4 (PROFIT label, act cards ×2, beach bubble); draw-on ×2 (pen ledger total, doodle chart line); grow-in-place ×3 (coin stacks, money piles ×2); fade-in ×1 (date/place label); swap-in-place ×3 (dialogue phrases, threat lines, bakery customer). **Slide-ins: 0. Character pop-ins: 0** — new characters ONLY arrive via cuts to new compositions.
- **Transition inventory:** hard cut ×100% (176/176 measured cuts; burst-verified at 6 cuts incl. both card boundaries). Black typewriter/title cards act as punctuation between beats. No fade/wipe/dissolve/whip anywhere in range.
- **Chart/diagram behavior:** two modes. (a) Hand-drawn doodle charts BUILD: line draws on, stacks grow stepwise, label types on (~1.25s build). (b) Concept diagrams build by continuous decelerating zoom-out with labels popping at each reveal stage (10–12s). Key numbers are hand-WRITTEN on progressively (ledger). Nothing appears as a finished static chart.
- **Type observations:** 3 editorial text classes — (1) black-card script: white handwritten-marker, static when a chapter TITLE, typewriter when a NARRATION line; (2) dialogue: either floating white script + leader line (dark scenes) or white oval outlined bubble w/ black text (bright scenes), pops whole or types on; (3) diagram labels: black marker CAPS in boxes/banners/signs, always pop whole. Plus the outlined cursive place/date label (fade-in).
- **3 most reusable motion mechanics** (Remotion-implementable):
  1. **Insert-cut + write-on payoff number** (emphasis beat): hard cut from action wide to a detail insert, then the key figure writes on progressively so it completes with the VO — maps to our stat-card/counter device with a draw-on mask.
  2. **Decelerating zoom-out that turns a scene into a labeled diagram** (explainer beat): one continuous ease-out pull, labels popping at fixed reveal stages — maps to a single STAGE-long camera arc + word-anchored overlay pops.
  3. **Black typewriter card as beat punctuation** (act boundary / dramatic-irony beat): snap cut to black, white script types on char-by-char (~10–16 chars/s), ~2–3s, snap cut out — coincides with measured ~0.5s audio dips (below).
- **Observed, NOT adoptable:** full limb/walk-cycle character animation (jump/fall gag, shoveling loop, cart-pushing walk), per-phoneme mouth movement, character swap-on-set as staffed animation. These carry most of Crayon's held-frame life and are outside our engine.
