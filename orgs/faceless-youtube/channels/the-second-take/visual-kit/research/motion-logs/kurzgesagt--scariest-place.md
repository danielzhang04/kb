**Typography observations:**
- One family everywhere: a geometric sans. Two roles: chapter titles = LIGHT weight, large, wide letterspacing; labels/counters = small caps or digits at regular weight, letterspaced.
- ALL CAPS universally; white on dark; no drop shadows, no color fills.
- Containers: thin ~1px white rectangle outline for data labels/counters; rounded capsule + leader line for the focus label in diagrams; chapter titles free-floating (or framed by a PROP, T3).
- Animation-in is ALWAYS per-letter assemble (strokes/letters stagger in with positional jitter, decel settle, ~1.5–2s), never a block fade or slide. Boxes draw their outline on. Text-out: labels leave with their scene (fade/scale with the zoom), not with their own exit move.

**Most re-usable mechanics (each ASPIRATIONAL), tied to the beat type it served:**
1. **Counter that keeps ticking past the spoken number** (F1 — "8,000 voids… and we keep discovering more"): the sustained digit-roll carries the second half of the sentence with zero extra pixels. Beat type: scale/quantity reveal where the point is "and growing". Direct fit for our T2 counter device.
2. **Decel-by-trail character arrival** (E1/E2 — travel beats): speed and stopping are read entirely from a motion trail's length shrinking frame-over-frame while the mover itself barely translates; the brake lands on the cautionary word ("Careful now"). Beat type: journey/arrival beats and any "slow down, look at this" pivot.
3. **Per-letter assemble title over a held prop** (T1/T3 — chapter breaks): scattered letter-strokes resolve into a light, letterspaced all-caps lockup while the background prop keeps idling; the type settle fills the narration pause. Beat type: chapter/act boundaries — the pause is held by type motion instead of a camera move.
