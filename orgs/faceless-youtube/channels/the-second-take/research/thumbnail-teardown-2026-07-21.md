# Thumbnail teardown — reference channels (frame-level pixel pass)

**Status:** filled 2026-07-21 · **Method:** pulled 10–18 recent/top thumbnails per channel via
`yt-dlp --flat-playlist` (weighted by real view_count), downloaded maxres JPGs, and **actually looked at
every image** (vision pass, ~55 read in full detail across 89 downloaded). This is the **packaging half of
the visual terminal** the board flagged still-open (`reference-channels.md` "Still open"). It CONFIRMS,
REFUTES, and DEEPENS the 2026-07-02 directional packaging learnings with real pixels.

> **Headline finding up front (this rewrites the grammar):** the current Second Take thumbnail spec
> (`dna.md` Branding — "one dominant money object as the anomaly, ≥50% negative space, **no face, no
> clutter**, ≤3-word overlay") is **the single weakest configuration in the entire reference set.** Almost
> every high-CTR thumbnail in these 8 channels is **FACE-FORWARD, EMOTIONAL, and packed** — the opposite of
> a quiet money object floating in empty space. The rejected Poyais set failed for structural reasons named
> in §Poyais-gap below. Rules a metadata/visual-prompt agent can apply mechanically are in §Rules.

Each finding tags **[EMOTION]** / **[ABSURD]** / **[CHARACTER]** (Daniel's "personality, animation" ask)
vs **[COMP]** (composition/legibility).

## 1. Crayon Capital — the closest model (style + packaging + persona). 14 thumbs.

The #1 reference is **overwhelmingly face/character-driven** — this is the biggest single correction to our
current spec.

- **[CHARACTER][EMOTION]** The default hero is a **central personified character with ONE loud emotion**,
  usually smug or menacing. *Rockefeller* (1.1M): bowler-hat villain, evil eyebrows, gritted teeth, red
  string-web arcing to oil-company logos. *NVIDIA* (55k): Jensen Huang caricature, leather jacket, smug
  grin, red strings to AMD/MSFT/OpenAI/Google. *amazon* (916k): giant glowing head, evil grin, gold money
  streams pouring from the warehouse. The villain's FACE is the hook, not the money object.
- **[CHARACTER][EMOTION]** When no human fits, **the money object is personified with a face**. *Petrodollar*
  (197k): a dollar bill wearing a crown, bug-eyed evil grin, red strings arcing to national flags. This is
  the money-object-as-anomaly idea done RIGHT — it has a face and an emotion, so it hooks.
- **[EMOTION]** A recurring device is the **red "conspiracy string-web"**: red lines radiating from a
  central power node to everything it controls (*Rockefeller*, *Petrodollar*, *NVIDIA*, *AI Bubble*
  detective-corkboard). Red = the web of control/alarm, and it POINTS.
- **[COMP][EMOTION]** **BEFORE/AFTER time-split** with year labels and an emotional contrast: *Worst time to
  be rich* (423k) 1870 (content, tea) vs 2026 (sweating businessman on phone); *Singapore* (845k) 1950s
  slum vs TODAY skyline with a skeptical face straddling; *526 Years of Slavery* (26k) 1500 grim chained
  miner vs 2026 gleeful shopper. The split + emotion carries it, not detail.
- **[EMOTION]** **Panic/dread faces** work too: *Margin Call* (159k) a glasses character mid-panic, crashing
  red charts on trading monitors behind. *Theranos* (215k) EXTREME close-up of Holmes's face holding a tiny
  vial, red **"FAKE"** label + red arrow pointing to it — the "spot the fake" idea, but with a huge face +
  a pointing arrow + one word (this is exactly what our rejected bond-row lacked).
- **[COMP]** **One GIANT subject-word** is the standard text: `ROCKEFELLER`, `PALANTIR`, `PETRODOLLAR`,
  `amazon`, `NVIDIA` — the name IS the type, huge and bold, or a yellow title band (`THE WOLF OF WALL
  STREET`). Text is the subject, big; the emotion is the image.
- **[COMP]** Frames are **packed, not sparse** — crowds, cityscapes, money floors. Negative space is
  minimal. There is always ONE clear focal face against the busy field. (Two outliers, *Palantir* &
  *Dot-Com Bubble*, drop the cast for a flat-graphic/3D glowing object — these are the minority and read as
  a different, cooler register.)

**Takeaway:** Crayon — the channel we most want to emulate — proves the hook is a **character with a
readable emotion + a red pointing device**, on a filled frame. Our "no face, empty vignette" rule is
directly anti-Crayon.

## 2. How Money Works — packaging exemplar (the sarcastic-text source). 14 thumbs.

Photo-composite, not vector — but the **TEXT grammar is the transferable lesson and it is unambiguous.**

- **[EMOTION][COMP]** Text is **ALWAYS a spoken line / verdict / punchline — NEVER the title or a label.**
  `WELL...WE TRIED...` (Ford CEO), `CANCELLED` (data center), `IT WAS NICE WHILE IT LASTED` (travel
  collage), `THE MATH IS NOT MATHING`, `DON'T TOUCH MY RETIREMENT` (laughing boomer golfer). This is the
  purest confirmation of our "sarcastic-reframe, not the title" learning.
- **[ABSURD][EMOTION]** **DEEPENED — the reframe is frequently a fake FIRST-PERSON QUOTE put in a real
  subject's mouth, in quote marks**: `"I LOVE INFLATION"` (Trump, hands up), `"MY AI COULD BEAT YOURS UP"`
  (Trump whispering to Xi), `I'M NOT F**KING LEAVING!` (Jerome Powell, arms crossed — note the profanity).
  Putting words in a recognizable face's mouth = instant character + emotion + joke. We can do this with our
  personified cast.
- **[COMP]** A recurring **series look**: dark condensed caps on **graph-paper** + a hand-drawn **red marker
  underline** under the key phrase + one hero object or chart (`THIS IS THE ECONOMY NOW` w/ knotted
  stethoscope; `THE MATH IS NOT MATHING`; `POLITICAL INSIDER TRADING`; `ESCAPING THE PERMANENT UNDERCLASS`
  w/ mannequin on a hamster wheel). The red marker underline is their persistent accent — this maps directly
  onto our locked marker-hand + red accent.
- **[COMP]** **Chart-as-image is real but never naked** — `THE MATH IS NOT MATHING` is a hand-drawn
  multi-line chart (RAM/PS5/GAS/RENT/FOOD all soaring over a flat INFLATION line), `POLITICAL INSIDER
  TRADING` is a candlestick chart with a **red marker arrow** slammed on the anomaly. The chart always
  carries a punchline + an annotation; it is never a cold data plot.
- **[ABSURD]** **Absurd literal props** carry the metaphor: boxes on people's heads (`THE JOB MARKET IS
  BOOMING / DON'T ASK QUESTIONS`), a stethoscope tied in a knot, a mannequin on a hamster wheel. The visual
  is a joke, not an illustration.
- **[EMOTION]** Real faces do heavy emotional lifting: smug, defeated (slumped trader under a torn
  `FINANCIAL TIMES` clipping), gloating, arms-crossed defiance.
- Text runs **4–6 words routinely** — our ≤3-word cap is too tight for a punchline.

## 3. Casually Explained — persona (illustrated, comedy). 11 thumbs.

- **[CHARACTER][ABSURD]** The recognizable **deliberately-crude MS-Paint face** is the anchor, and the hook
  is an **absurd photo+drawing mashup**: the drawn deadpan face on a real jacked bodybuilder body =
  `100% Natty` (3.5M); the face perched on a real money-mountain amid an AI-crowd = *Billionaires* (2.9M);
  the face with a cyborg eye between a real paperclip-Clippy and a real Blade-Runner still = *Levels of AI*.
  The joke IS the composition.
- **[EMOTION]** Text is a **deadpan punchline, 1–3 words**: `Fool...`, `100% Natty`, `No` (with a snapping
  broken heart), `Just delete the app`. Minimal, dry, funny.
- **[COMP]** Persistent **`Casually Explained: <Topic>`** corner franchise wordmark (Comic-Sans-ish).
- **Caveat (board's own honesty flag):** this is a human-comedy-writer moat. Take the mechanic — **expressive
  face + absurd real/drawn juxtaposition + dry short punchline** — not the low-fi crudeness (off-brand for
  our clean 2.5D look).

## 4. Half as Interesting — persona/packaging. 10 thumbs.

- **[COMP]** Clean flat-vector, **ONE hero object/map**: the tangled `BREEZEWOOD, PA` road sign, a red 911
  phone, a plane over a map, Bolivia's flag-filled outline.
- **[EMOTION][COMP]** Text = a **short incredulous verdict in bold YELLOW caps inside a dark rounded box**:
  `THIS AIRPORT IS ILLEGAL`, `WHY WOULD THEY NEED A NAVY?`, `HOW 911 DISPATCH WORKS`. The yellow-on-navy
  text box is their series signature.
- **[COMP]** **Red arrow/line annotation points at the anomaly** on the map (red arrow to Bolivia, red flight
  path to the illegal airport). The accent directs the eye.
- **[EMOTION]** The anomaly is sometimes stated flatly as the joke: `ACETAMINOPHEN — HOW IT WORKS? WE DON'T
  KNOW`.
- **[COMP]** Persistent circular **"H" wordmark**, top-right, every thumb.

## 5. Kurzgesagt — illustrated look (aspirational). 10 thumbs.

- **[COMP]** **ONE GIANT single word** dominates: `SUPERVOIDS`, `PERFECT PLANET`, `OZEMPIC`, `GERMANY`,
  `TERRA-NUKING`. Ultra-bold, high contrast, top of frame.
- **[COMP]** **Deep saturated color + one glowing hero object** (a jewel-toned planet, a void, a glowing
  gut). Their brand is the *style*, so they carry **no corner logo** — the look is the wordmark.
- **[COMP][EMOTION]** **Red accent = a pointing arrow or an alarm label**: red arrow to the `PERFECT PLANET`
  (with tiny labeled `EARTH` for scale), a red `UPDATE!` tag on *Ozempic*. Scale-comparison (big vs tiny
  labeled) is a recurring device.
- **[ABSURD]** Pure-type frames when the word is enough: `GERMANY` in flaming blackletter; a tiny `Hello...?`
  speech bubble in a dark void. Emotion via a single scared character face (*Ozempic*).
- Kurzgesagt is a **legibility masterclass**: even at 168px the single word + single glowing shape + one red
  mark reads instantly. This is the composition bar we should hit.

## 6. PolyMatter — annotation-over-media (board correction: NOT flat vector). 10 thumbs.

- **[COMP]** Confirmed 2026-07-02 correction: **real photo/satellite footage + a green-and-white annotation
  layer.** `WHY ONLY CHINA CAN MAKE THE iPHONE` over a before/after satellite split with black label tags
  (`PACKAGING`/`DORMITORIES`/`FINAL ASSEMBLY`); `HOW BANKS WORK` over a photo $100 bill with **red laser
  scan-lines + circled zoom callouts** (1.32.4A/B/C).
- **[COMP]** **One highlight word in green** inside otherwise white text (`ONLY CHINA` green, `SCAMS`/
  `SUDDENLY` white). A second color marks the payoff word — same idea as RLL's yellow.
- **[ABSURD]** Subvert the subject's own branding: a literal Walmart sign photo with a `Big Comeback` banner
  taped on; a retro Windows-XP scam popup for `WHY SCAMS ARE SUDDENLY EVERYWHERE` with a red vertical accent
  bar.
- **[COMP]** Persistent hexagon **"P" wordmark** corner. Reference for annotation/typography-over-media, not
  for our illustrated cast.

## 7. RealLifeLore — packaging/casual. 10 thumbs.

- **[COMP]** **3D relief map** canvas + **bold text in black boxes** (white body, **payoff word in
  YELLOW**): `COUNTDOWN TO COLLAPSE` (COLLAPSE yellow), `GEOGRAPHY'S DEADLIEST GLITCH` (GLITCH yellow),
  `IT'S OVER`.
- **[EMOTION][ABSURD]** The hook is a **single loaded emotional-metaphor ICON overlaid on the map** — this is
  how RLL gets emotion into a faceless map: **scissors cutting Crimea off** Ukraine (`IT'S OVER`); a
  **skull-and-fire** burning across Cuba (`COUNTDOWN TO COLLAPSE`); a **glowing red skull** on New Hampshire
  (`DEADLIEST GLITCH`); **yellow `WAR` arrows radiating** from a blood-red `IRAN`. The metaphor icon =
  instant dread/stakes without a human face.
- **[COMP]** **Red circle + red arrow annotation** marks the anomaly: `OOPS` in a black box with a red
  hand-drawn circle around the Strait of Hormuz. Red = the pointing finger, again.
- Big map + big black-box text + one bright icon = trivially legible at feed size.

## 8. Wendover — packaging rigor. 10 thumbs.

- **[ABSURD][EMOTION]** The signature move: **hijack the subject's OWN branding and subvert it with a
  sarcastic line.** `JUST BLEW IT.` on a Nike shoebox (parody of Just Do It), with a fake `NKE STOCK 52`
  label; `100% CHANCE OF RIPPING YOU OFF` on a car-dealership sign flanked by flailing red tube-men;

- **[COMP]** **Chart embedded in the object**: `FAST FOOD'S DIGITAL REVOLUTION` — McDonald's arches built

- **[COMP]** Bold condensed white caps, often in a **black box**; a **retro sunrise-mountain circle
  wordmark** top-left on every thumb.
- **[EMOTION]** Even a photo-grid (Red Bull 2×2 extreme-sports + logo) leads with energy/motion, not a static

## Cross-channel synthesis — what the high-CTR thumbnails actually DO (ranked)

Ranked by how consistently the top-viewed thumbnails across all 8 channels rely on it:

1. **[EMOTION][CHARACTER] A face (or personified object with a face) showing ONE loud emotion** — smug,
   menacing, panicked, gloating. Crayon, HMW, Casually Explained all lead with this; RLL substitutes a
   loaded metaphor icon (skull/fire/scissors) to get the same emotional jolt on a faceless map. **This is
   the #1 hook mechanism and the one our current spec forbids.**
2. **[EMOTION] Text is a punchline / verdict / fake-quote with attitude** — never the premise, never a
   neutral label. Often 4–6 words; often a first-person quote in quote marks put in the subject's mouth.
3. **[COMP] One big, simple focal thing, legible at 168px** — a giant word (Kurzgesagt), one hero object
   (HAI), one face (Crayon). No fine detail, no rows of near-identical items, no small numbers to read.
4. **[COMP] A red/bright accent that POINTS at the anomaly** — arrow, hand-drawn circle, string-web, marker
   underline, crash line. Red is the director's finger, not just ink on a number.
5. **[ABSURD] An absurd or menacing juxtaposition** — tube-men at the scam dealership, boxes on heads,
   scissors cutting a country, a drawn face on a real bodybuilder. The image itself is the joke/threat.
6. **[COMP] A familiar-brand anchor** — a recognizable logo/CEO/product/map (Nike box, Ford CEO, Amazon
   wordmark, Iran outline). The famous thing leads; the obscure specifics are the payoff (matches our
   `dna.md` familiarity-anchor title rule — apply it to thumbnails too).
7. **[COMP] Poster-weight type; highlight the payoff word in a 2nd color** — yellow `COLLAPSE`/`GLITCH`
   (RLL), green `ONLY CHINA` (PolyMatter), a red marker underline (HMW).
8. **[COMP] A persistent corner wordmark** — HAI's H, PolyMatter's P, Wendover's sunrise, Casual's franchise
   line. (Kurzgesagt is the exception: the art style IS the brand.)

### Where the 2026-07-02 directional learnings were right / wrong

- **"Thumbnail text = sarcastic reframe, not the title" → CONFIRMED, emphatically** (HMW, Wendover). And
  **DEEPENED**: the strongest version is a **fake first-person quote in quote marks** put in a
  recognizable subject's mouth.
- **"The chart IS the image" → PARTLY RIGHT, needs a caveat.** Charts appear often but are **never naked** —
  they're always paired with a face, a personified object, an absurd prop, or a heavy red annotation +
  punchline. A cold data plot alone does not hook. Rewrite as **"the chart can be the CANVAS, but an
  emotion/annotation/punchline must ride on it."**
- **"One red accent for money numbers" → REFINED.** Red's job is to **POINT** (arrow/circle/string/
  underline), not merely to color a number. Keep one-red discipline; change its function from decoration to
  direction.
- **"Persistent corner wordmark" → CONFIRMED.**
- **`dna.md` Branding spec ("no face, ≥50% negative space, ≤3-word overlay, one money object, no clutter") →
  LARGELY REFUTED.** No-face + empty-vignette + money-object-only is the *weakest* configuration in the
  entire set. Negative space is not a driver (Kurzgesagt uses it, but always with a giant word + glowing
  hero); ≤3 words is too tight for a punchline; and "no clutter / no face" strips out exactly the emotion
  and character that the winners live on. **This spec should be rewritten** (see §Rules).

## Why the rejected Poyais set under-hooked (name the gap)

The three rejected candidates — **(a)** ornate banknote in an empty vignette `Population: 0`, **(b)** a row
of bonds `Spot the fake`, **(c)** a brochure split `As advertised` — fail on multiple axes at once, and
every failure maps to a thing the reference winners do:

1. **No emotion, no face, no character.** All three are cold, static objects. Nothing is smug, panicked,
   menacing, or gloating; nothing is personified. The #1 hook mechanism (a face/personified thing with an
   emotion — Crayon's crowned evil-grin dollar, HMW's mouths-of-real-people) is entirely absent. This is the
   biggest gap.
2. **Too subtle / cerebral at feed size — they demand a TASK, not a glance.** `Spot the fake` literally asks
   the viewer to inspect near-identical bonds — the opposite of a half-second read. `Population: 0` requires
   reading a tiny number inside a vignette. `As advertised` is a quiet ironic label. None presents a single
   dominant anomaly visible at 168px. (Contrast: Theranos does "spot the fake" RIGHT — one huge face + a red
   arrow + the single word `FAKE`.)
3. **Too much fine detail that vanishes small.** Ornate banknote engraving and rows of bonds are exactly the
   micro-detail that dissolves at thumbnail scale; winners use ONE big simple shape.
4. **Text is a premise/label, not a punchline.** `Spot the fake` / `As advertised` / `Population: 0` are
   descriptions of the premise. The winners deliver a **verdict with attitude** (`JUST BLEW IT.`, `100%
   CHANCE OF RIPPING YOU OFF`, `"I LOVE INFLATION"`, `IT'S OVER`). No joke lands, no alarm fires.
5. **No pointing accent.** Nothing directs the eye to the anomaly — no red arrow, circle, string, or
   underline. The winners always point.
6. **The brand rule itself produced the problem.** "≥50% negative space + single-color field + no face"
   *manufactures* a quiet, empty, low-energy frame. In this reference set, quiet = invisible. The empty
   vignette is the bug, not the feature.

**One-sentence version:** the Poyais set reads as a tasteful cold *illustration of the premise* — no face,
no emotion, fine detail that dies at 168px, and label-text instead of a punchline — whereas every reference
winner is a **loud, emotional, one-big-thing frame with a punchline and a red finger pointing at the joke.**

## Rules — a Second Take thumbnail MUST follow these (mechanically applicable)

Written so a metadata-writer / visual-prompt-writer agent can apply each without judgment calls. Tags:
**[EMOTION/CHAR/ABSURD]** = Daniel's personality ask; **[COMP]** = composition/legibility.

1. **[CHAR][EMOTION] A hero with ONE readable emotion is mandatory.** Default = a member of our 2.5D cast
   (the con-man, the mark, the personified institution) with a single loud expression: smug, menacing,
   panicked, or gloating. If no human fits, **personify the money object** and give it a face (a crowned
   banknote with an evil grin, a sweating bond). **A faceless object with no emotion is banned as the sole
   subject.** The ONLY no-face exception: a single loaded metaphor-icon that itself carries dread —
   skull, fire, scissors cutting, a crash line (the RLL move).
2. **[EMOTION] The overlay text is a PUNCHLINE / VERDICT / fake-quote, ≤6 words, never the premise or a
   neutral label.** It must land the joke or the alarm in a half-second. Prefer a **first-person quote in
   quote marks** put in the subject's mouth. **Ban task-prompts** ("Spot the fake") and quiet labels ("As
   advertised", "Population: 0").
3. **[COMP] Exactly ONE dominant thing, big and simple, legible at 168px.** No fine engraving, no rows of
   near-identical items, no numbers smaller than ~15% of frame height. If understanding the anomaly requires
   inspection, the thumbnail fails — blow the anomaly up until it reads at feed size.
4. **[COMP] The one red accent must POINT at the anomaly or the punchword** — a hand-drawn marker arrow,
   circle, string-web, or underline (locked accent `#d7402b`, marker hand). Red directs the eye; it is not
   just the color of a money number.
5. **[COMP] Poster-weight hook type + highlight the payoff word.** The HOOK line is bold, thick, high
   contrast — heavy caps in a dark box, or a giant single subject-word — even though body/diegetic text uses
   the marker hand. Put the payoff word in a second colour (our accent red, or a bright secondary) the way
   RLL yellows `COLLAPSE` and PolyMatter greens `ONLY CHINA`.
6. **[ABSURD] Prefer an absurd or menacing juxtaposition over a literal illustration.** Subvert the subject's
   own branding (a con's "brochure" turned against it), stage an impossible/damning scene, or an oversized
   prop. The image should be the joke or the threat, not a neutral depiction.
7. **[COMP] Lead with a familiar anchor.** The recognizable brand/face/place/era the story touches must be
   the visual entry point; the obscure specifics are the payoff (same rule as our titles, `dna.md`).
8. **[COMP] Persistent corner wordmark** on every thumbnail for channel identity.

**Priority when they conflict:** emotion/character/absurdity (rules 1, 2, 6) **beat** composition polish
(3–8). A busy, imperfect frame with a great emotional hook out-clicks a clean, tasteful, empty one — that is
the core lesson of this teardown and the direct fix for the rejected Poyais set.

### Which findings answer Daniel's "personality / animation" note

- **[EMOTION/CHAR/ABSURD] rules 1, 2, 6** and cross-channel ranks **1, 2, 5** are the personality core —
  faces, loud expressions, personified money objects, fake-quotes, absurd staging. This is where the
  reference winners live and where the Poyais set was empty.
- **[COMP] rules 3, 4, 5, 7, 8** and ranks **3, 4, 6, 7, 8** are composition/legibility — necessary, but
  they are the frame, not the hook.
