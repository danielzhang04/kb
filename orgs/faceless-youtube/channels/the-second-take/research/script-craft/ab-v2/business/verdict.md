## 4. vs the reference (Magnates)

**v2 is meaningfully closer to Magnates' watchability than v1 — without copying, and without adopting
their unsourced color.** Magnates' engine is villain-origin → mechanism-assembly → named human cost →
historical rhyme. v1 had *none* of the character/human layer. v2 now runs all four in miniature: the

and substitutes the court-recorded Mountains. Register stays The Second Take's dry sprinkle, and the run
is compressed to ~15 min vs Magnates' 31 (legibility-by-subtraction). This is exactly the §6 guardrail
working as designed.

## 6. Writer's self-flagged soft spot — VERIFIED REAL

The irony cross-cut (§3F — carry tension on a pre-spoiled ending via *cross-cut* dramatic irony) is the
least-delivered fix. Search confirms **no** cross-cut markers ("Meanwhile," "blissfully unaware,"
collectors-buying-while-Warner-knew). v2 *states* the collapse (*"The music was slowing down, and
everyone in the chairs could feel it"*; *"Almost nobody believed the shutdown was ever real"*) rather
than dramatizing an ironic gap. It doesn't hurt the score much because mechanism-assembly + character
carry the tension instead (same as Magnates), but the specific technique is under-delivered. The writer's
self-assessment is honest and accurate.

Minor extra fresh-eyes catch (leash, not scored): the cold-open detail *"he personally brushed their fur
by hand before they shipped"* slightly outruns its source ([F-26] = grooming at trade shows / hovering
after the toys left the factory, per Magnates/Bissonnette). "Before they shipped" implies factory QC.
Small embellishment to tighten.

thinnest part and remains stated-not-dramatized. This is a real, non-cosmetic improvement, not an
over-claim. v2 is publishable; v1 was not.
