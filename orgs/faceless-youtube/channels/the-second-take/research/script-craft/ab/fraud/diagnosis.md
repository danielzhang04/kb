# Self-diagnosis — does research.md hand the writer a STORY or a fact-ledger?

Honest inspection of my own `research.md` against the scaffolding test: cast+motive, stakes,
turning points, ironic/telling detail, a story SPINE, and a second-person "why this matters."

## Verdict (one line)

**It's a strong, disciplined fact-ledger with a bolted-on spine — closer to scaffolding than a raw
ledger, but the story is still *implied by structure* rather than *written down as a story.* A
scriptwriter could build the video from it, but they'd be doing the dramatization themselves; I handed
them lumber and a blueprint, not a framed house.**

## Where it scaffolds well (genuinely present)

- **Cast is named and role-differentiated.** Davis (mechanic), Peh/KIP (purpose-front), Novelli/Terrones
  Godoy (access brokers), Chow (alleged center), Milei (the rented credibility). The div-of-labor is
  explicit in F-12/F-13/F-18. This is real cast-building, not a name dump.
- **A real SPINE exists** — the 8-beat "Narrative spine" section with designated cold-open, mid-video
  re-arm (the $MELANIA turn), and withheld fine-print. It maps cleanly onto the dna's required structure.
  That's more than a ledger requires.
- **Turning points are identified** — the $MELANIA wallet link as the "assembly line, not accident"
  re-arm, and the ethics-clearance-vs-live-criminal-probe as the ironic fallout pivot.
- **The ironic/telling detail is captured** — the antagonist narrating his own mechanism on camera ("it's
  an insider's game"), and the ~30-min-before-the-tweet token creation. Both are gold and both are in the
  ledger.
- **Analogies-as-indictment are pre-built** — the house-with-a-player-badge, the cathedral-stained-glass-
  over-three-card-monte. These teach AND indict, exactly the §5b brief.
- **Defamation discipline is strong** — every hard claim routed through a document/on-record source,
  legality explicitly flagged as unsettled, the $5M doc marked "alleged." This is the part I'm most
  confident about.

## Where it's still a ledger, not a story (the honest gaps)

1. **MOTIVE is thin.** I have *what* each actor did but barely *why* — beyond "money." Why did Milei, an
   economist-president who rails against statism, put his name on a memecoin? Vanity? Genuine (naive)
   belief in the "Argentine tech" pitch? Who inside his circle (Karina? the bank-records unsealing hints
   at it) pushed it? The ledger notes his sister's records were unsealed (F-16) but never asks the *why*
   that turns her into a character. A story needs the actors to *want* things; my ledger has them
   *performing functions.*

2. **STAKES are abstract, not human.** "$251M in investor losses" (F-06) is a number, not a person. There
   is **zero human-cost texture** — no named victim, no "a Buenos Aires taxi driver put in his savings,"
   no scene of the retail buyer who trusted the president. The dna demands a second-person "why this
   matters"; my ledger has none. The whole thing is told from the *operators'* altitude, never the
   *victim's*. That is the single biggest scaffolding miss.

3. **No second-person "why this matters" anywhere.** The brief promised "the casino tells you the odds if
   you read the fine print." That's a *theme*, but I never translated it into *you* — why a viewer who
   will never touch a memecoin should care (answer: this is the template for renting *any* trusted name —
   influencer, politician, institution — to disarm your diligence). It's latent in the analogies but never
   stated as the viewer's stake.

4. **The spine is a section outline, not a dramatized beat sheet.** I labeled beats but didn't hand over
   the *dramatized 2-line exchanges* the dna's persona explicitly wants as the default way to play a beat
   (the meet-and-greet pitch; Davis's on-camera dodge). I gestured at "the confession" but didn't pull the
   actual quotable line for the writer to stage. The writer still has to go find the drama.

5. **Chronology is reconstructable but not *sequenced for tension.*** The facts are atomic and ID'd
   (correctly), but the "peak-before-the-fall" dramatic-irony engine (open at maximum success, then
   rewind) is only named in the spine, not pre-loaded with the beat-by-beat *timing* that makes it land
   (the 45-minute pump, the under-an-hour drain — those two clocks running against each other is the
   whole cold-open, and I under-exploited it).

## Why this happened (the systemic read)

The researcher SKILL + contract optimize hard for **truth and defensibility** (atomic, sourced, dated,
defamation-safe) — and on that axis this dossier is good. But the template's *story* sections ("Narrative
spine," "Analogies," "Myths") are **thin, optional-feeling appendages** after a **heavy, exhaustive fact
ledger.** So the gravity of the format pulls toward *ledger*, and the scaffolding gets a paragraph each.
The result over-serves the compliance/accuracy need and **under-serves the "hand the writer a story"
need** — specifically **motive, human stakes, and second-person "why you care,"** which are the exact
things a fact-ledger structurally *cannot* hold and which the dna's whole entertainment-not-lecture
doctrine lives or dies on.

## Concrete fixes (for improving the researcher skill)

- Add a required **"Cast & motive"** block: for each named actor, one line of *what they wanted*
  (sourced/inferred-and-labeled), not just what they did.
- Add a required **"Human stakes / the victim"** hunt to the chase list — find one named or concrete
  retail loser, or explicitly flag that none is sourceable (so the writer knows to keep it at altitude
  *on purpose*, not by omission).
- Add a required **"Why this matters to you (second person)"** line — the transferable lesson that makes a
  non-participant care.
- Ask the researcher to pull **2–3 actual quotable lines / exchanges** verbatim (defamation-checked) so
  the writer can dramatize, not paraphrase.
- Reframe the spine as a **tension-sequenced beat sheet** (which clocks run against each other), not a
  section list.
