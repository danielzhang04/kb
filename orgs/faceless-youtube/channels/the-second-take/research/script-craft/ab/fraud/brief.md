## The subject (scope-locked)

The **$LIBRA token** launched **February 14, 2025** on Solana, promoted in a single tweet by Argentine
President **Javier Milei** as a project to fund Argentine small business and economic growth. Within
hours it ran to a multi-billion-dollar notional market cap, then collapsed ~80–90% after insiders
pulled a reported **$100M+ out of the liquidity pool**. The engineers around it: **Hayden Davis /
Kelsier Ventures** (launch + market-making), **Julian Peh / KIP Protocol** (the nominal "purpose"
front), and **Tech Forum Argentina** organizers **Mauricio Novelli** and **Manuel Terrones Godoy**
(the access brokers who connected the crypto team to Milei). Fallout: Milei deleted the tweet,
claimed no involvement; the opposition filed fraud complaints and floated impeachment; Argentine and
US legal processes opened. Davis is on record admitting the token was "sniped." The same wallet
cluster is tied to the earlier **$MELANIA** launch.

## Angle (our POV — NOT Coffeezilla's)

Coffeezilla's version is **first-person investigator theater** — "I got the call, I got offered the
$100M, watch me confront him." Our version is the **dry-smart insider autopsy of the machine**: not
"look what I uncovered," but *"here is the assembly line that lets a stranger rent a head of state's
credibility for one tweet and legally walk with nine figures."* The story is the **mechanism of a
'legal' rug** — how selling *access* + insider *sniping* + a 'token with purpose' wrapper combine so
that the fool is the retail buyer and the take is structured to be un-prosecutable. Milei is the
anchor and the cautionary spectacle; **Hayden Davis's launch machine is the real subject.**
