`char-coffeezilla.md` craft taxonomy. Every gap is attributed to **researcher** (material absent from
`research.md`) or **writer** (material present but told flat), with the evidence cited.

**Headline framing for attribution.** The Coffeezilla interview is our source **S4** — cited, but mined
only for *summary facts* (F-08 "admitted sniping, made ~$100M"; F-09 "sniping ≈ front-running"). The
richest craft in the reference — the verbatim swap/sell dodge, the "junkie house" hookah scene, Ben
Chow's on-camera collapse, Dave Portnoy as a named victim — **all live inside S4** and were reduced to
bullets. So most "researcher gaps" below are not *source-access* failures; they are *extraction-depth*
failures: the transcript was flattened into a ledger and the drama fell out.

## B. What OURS did well (not manufactured — genuinely strong)

1. **Landing the vindication on the SYSTEM, elegantly — arguably beats the reference.** Coffeezilla:
   "these meme coins are deeply rigged Financial vehicles… the deck is stacked against you." Ours:
   "The casino didn't cheat, exactly. It posted the odds right there on the wall — on-chain, in
   daylight, in fine print anyone could read. It just built the whole business on one safe bet: that you
   never would." That is a cleaner, more original person→system landing. The "Milei… was this month's
   **ignition key**" pivot is excellent.

2. **Separating the uncontested from the contested — crisp.** Coffeezilla: "it's so far unclear… but
   the sniping itself is uncontested." Ours matches and sharpens it: "The exact figure is genuinely
   contested… What is **not** contested is the direction," and "notice the one thing he did not argue
   with: **the number**." This is the taxonomy's move #9 executed well.

3. **Legal fine print is more rigorous than the reference.** The ethics-clearance-vs-live-criminal-probe
   distinction ("It is an ethics finding. It is not a criminal one — and they are not the same court")
   and "there is no clear law on the books that says launching your own coin and buying it first is a
   crime… deregulated rather than reined in" out-precisions Coffeezilla's looser "probably illegal…
   another matter whether it's prosecuted." Sourced cleanly to F-14/F-15/F-09.

4. **Analogies that teach AND indict** (one-way till, cathedral-window-over-card-table, assembly line) —
   a legitimate faceless strength that fills space the reference fills with live footage. Pre-built in
   research §5b and well-deployed.

5. **Second person is present and lands late** ("you buy in early, you feel clever. Here is what you
   cannot see"; "you are the pot") — correct timing per taxonomy #15.

6. **Defamation discipline is cleaner than the reference** — the $5M deal routed strictly through the
   document ("That is what the document is reported to say. None of it has been proven in a courtroom"),
   exact-figure hedged. This is research F-13's discipline honored faithfully.

## C. Highest-leverage fixes (split by skill)

**RESEARCHER (3 — all extraction-depth, not access):**

1. **Add a required "Verbatim exchanges & scenes" block.** When a primary source is a transcript/interview
   (here, S4 was *cited but flattened*), pull 2–3 verbatim self-incriminating exchanges (the swap/sell
   dodge) and any reported first-person scenes (the hookah "junkie house"), defamation-checked. This one
   change recovers the antagonist-convicts-himself, dramatized-scene, and quotable-dialogue gaps at once —
   the three biggest craft misses, and all sourced from a document we already had.

2. **Mandatory "human stakes / named victim" hunt.** Surface one concrete loser (Portnoy: $5M in, bailed
   out while retail wasn't — a ready injustice beat) or explicitly certify none is sourceable so the writer
   keeps altitude *on purpose*. Fixes the abstract-$251M problem the diagnosis already self-flagged.

3. **Surface + tag corroboration and third-party reactions.** The second in-the-room insider and Ben
   Chow's reaction were in S4 and dropped. Capture them with independence tags so the writer can *count
   sources out loud* and *doubt a favorable clip*.

**WRITER (2 — craft on material already in the ledger):**

4. **Dramatize the verification act, don't just report its result.** F-10 already hands the writer the
   Bubblemaps LIBRA↔MELANIA wallet match; the script states it as a finding. Stage it as an on-screen
   trace ("the wallet that launched LIBRA is the same one that launched Melania's — here is the match")
   *before* the accusation. This is the reference's single biggest portable move and the writer skipped it.

5. **Stage the explicit exoneration beat.** research myths + F-05/F-07/F-08 already say "every insider on
   record says Milei made no money," but the script only *implies* it ("he rented his credibility"). Add
   the crisp Coffeezilla-style clearance ("every insider I could find says Milei himself made nothing —
   believe that or not") — exonerating the obvious villain is what certifies the narrator and earns every
   *other* accusation.

**Net:** ours is already strong on thesis, legal precision, and systemic landing — it loses to the
reference almost entirely on *staged, verbatim, human-level drama*, and the root cause is upstream: the
researcher reduced a rich interview transcript to summary facts. Fix the researcher's extraction depth
first (fixes 1–3); the two writer fixes (4–5) then have material to work with.
