## Fact ledger  ← the scriptwriter may state ONLY what appears here

- **[F-01]** On **Feb 14, 2025, ~7:00 pm Argentina time**, President Javier Milei posted on X promoting a
  Solana token called **$LIBRA**, describing a private project to fund small business and Argentine
  economic growth, linking a site calling it a "token with purpose." — *Src:* S1, S2 · *Conf:* high ·
  *Note:* tweet later deleted (F-05).
- **[F-02]** The $LIBRA token was created by **Kelsier Ventures** (a Delaware-registered company) roughly
  **30 minutes before** Milei's post (~6:30 pm), per the reconstructed timeline. — *Src:* S1 · *Conf:* med
  · *Note:* "~30 min before" per Buenos Aires Herald timeline reconstruction.
- **[F-03]** Within ~45 minutes of the post, $LIBRA spiked to a peak of about **US$5.54** per token before
  collapsing. — *Src:* S1, S2 · *Conf:* med · *Note:* peak paper/notional market cap widely described as
  "billions"; treat the mcap as notional, not realized.
- **[F-04]** Blockchain analysts found roughly **US$99M** was withdrawn from $LIBRA's liquidity pool by
  **~8 wallets** linked to the token's creators, **less than an hour** after launch; other reconstructions
  put the removal in an **US$80M–$100M** range. — *Src:* S2, S3 (Chainalysis-cited) · *Conf:* med ·
  *Note:* exact figure conflicts across sources; hold as "~$80–100M," not a single hard number.
- **[F-05]** Milei **deleted the promotional post** after the crash and stated he "obviously has no
  connection" and "was not aware of the details of the project." — *Src:* S1, S4 · *Conf:* high.
- **[F-06]** Investor losses are commonly reported at about **US$251M** (retail capital wiped out); the
  token lost **>90%+ (reported ~96%)** of value within hours. — *Src:* S3, S5 · *Conf:* med · *Note:*
  aggregate estimate; loss totals vary by methodology (whose cost basis).
- **[F-07]** **Hayden Davis**, CEO of Kelsier Ventures and described as an "adviser" photographed with
  Milei, was the central figure in the launch and market-making. — *Src:* S2, S4, S6 · *Conf:* high.
- **[F-08]** In an on-camera interview with **Stephen Findeisen ("Coffeezilla")**, Davis **admitted his
  team "sniped"** the $LIBRA launch, said he **made ~US$100M**, still **held ~US$11M** in crypto, and
  made **~US$13M in fees**. — *Src:* S4, S6 · *Conf:* high (his own on-record statements) · *Note:* Davis
  framed sniping as protecting the project from *other* snipers, not theft — that framing is his defense,
  not an established fact.
- **[F-09]** "Sniping" = insiders buying a token in the first moments of launch (often via bots with the
  contract address in advance) at the lowest price, then selling into the retail spike; described by
  reporting as a practice **"similar to front-running."** — *Src:* S4, S6 · *Conf:* med · *Note:*
  whether it is *illegal* is contested/unsettled for memecoins — see Open questions; do NOT state "illegal"
  as fact.
- **[F-10]** On-chain firm **Bubblemaps** reported a link between the wallets that launched **$MELANIA**
  (the Melania Trump memecoin) and **$LIBRA**, suggesting the same operation/cluster. — *Src:* S5, S7 ·
  *Conf:* med · *Note:* "linked wallets," per on-chain analysis; presented as forensic inference.
- **[F-11]** Davis was also **publicly tied to the launch of $MELANIA and $TRUMP** memecoins; he admitted
  involvement in $MELANIA but claimed his team **"didn't make any / took no liquidity out"** on it. —
  *Src:* S4, S6, S7 · *Conf:* med · *Note:* his no-profit claim on MELANIA is disputed by insiders in the
  Coffeezilla reporting; hold as claim-vs-claim.
- **[F-12]** Access to Milei was reportedly **brokered via "Tech Forum Argentina,"** an event where crypto
  companies paid to sponsor and get a **meet-and-greet with Milei**; organizers named as **Mauricio
  Novelli** and **Manuel Terrones Godoy**; **KIP Protocol (Julian Peh)** and Kelsier were the paying
  partners. — *Src:* S4, S8 · *Conf:* med · *Note:* roles per reporting; KIP was positioned as the

- **[F-13]** A document recovered from **Novelli's phone**, dated ~3 days before Milei's post, allegedly

## Analogies that teach AND indict (§5b candidates)

- A memecoin launch-and-snipe is like **the house sitting down at its own poker table wearing a player's
  badge** — it dealt the cards, it saw them first, and it's "just another player." (grounds [F-08],[F-09])
- Selling a meet-and-greet with a president is like **renting a cathedral's stained glass to bless a
  three-card-monte table** — you're not buying the con, you're buying the credibility that makes people
  stop checking. (grounds [F-12],[F-18])
- "Token with purpose" is the **charity-gala open bar**: the mission on the banner exists to keep you from
  counting who's actually walking out with the cash. (grounds [F-01],[F-04])
- Single-sided liquidity is like **being the only one who can open the till** — everyone's money goes in
  one side; only the operator can take it out the other. (grounds [F-04])

## Myths / misconceptions to bust

- **Myth:** "Milei launched a coin and rugged his own people." → **Actually:** every insider on record says
  Milei made no money; he sold *credibility*, and a crypto crew rented it — the extraction ran through
  Kelsier's wallets, not the president's. ([F-05],[F-07],[F-08]) — strong vindication payload (the real
  mechanism is more damning *and* more mundane than the tabloid version).
- **Myth:** "A rug pull is obviously a crime; they'll be locked up." → **Actually:** Milei was *cleared* on
  ethics; "sniping" a memecoin sits in an unsettled legal gray zone; in a deregulating environment the
  extraction may never be prosecuted. ([F-09],[F-14],[F-15])
- **Myth:** "This was a one-off scam." → **Actually:** on-chain links tie the same operation to $MELANIA
  (and Davis to $TRUMP) — a repeatable template, not a single event. ([F-10],[F-11])
