## Learnings by dimension (deep-research 2026-07-02 — what The Second Take adopts)

> Cross-channel *craft* lives in `universal.md` (cited inline); *finance-general* facts in
> `business-money.md`. This section = the **channel-specific synthesis** — what WE take, plus board
> Method note: the 2026-07-02 pass was **transcript + packaging-scan only** (the video-vision tool
> couldn't pull full video that session), so its cut-cadence/loudness figures were *directional*. **As of
> 2026-07-04 full-video watching works** (frames + audio + measured scene-cuts/silence/loudness via the
> `claude-video-vision` MCP downloading with yt-dlp) — so a deeper pass can now MEASURE what was inferred.
> The 2026-07-04 script/humor deep-dive (below) uses real frame + audio analysis.

**§1 — Packaging.** Adopt the **mocking-strawman title** as a signature (the title *is* the money myth
we then demolish — HMW `bUt ThAt'S mY NeSt EgG!`); it's the purest fit for our vindication lever. Also:
decline-verb + intensifier ("How X Got So Terrible" — Wendover), definitive-version ("The REAL Reason…"
— J. Harris), and the optional franchise tag ("… Explained Like You're 5" — Crayon; brand + permission
to simplify, not a burned list). Thumbnails: text is a **sarcastic verdict/reframe, never the title**;
**the chart IS the image** (fully faceless, native to finance); one red accent for money/alarm; a
persistent corner wordmark.

**§2 — Casual watchability.** The core engine (Crayon) is **staged micro-scenes** — dramatize each
mechanism as a scene instead of flat narration, but rendered as the **narrator's reported speech, never a
voiced two-hander or a quote** (one-narrator / no-quotes lock; the full staging craft is
`storytelling-grammar.md §4`). Layer Casually Explained's **accurate-runway → terminal-turn** rhythm
and HAI's **sentence-level asides that snap back** ("anyways…") — never multi-minute tangents.

**§3 — Narrator persona.** Locked = a **storytelling-first, witty, razor-smart insider** who **"read the
fine print."** (Crayon literally praises "oddballs, skeptics, troublemakers who read the fine print" —
our persona, verbatim, in a top video.) Hyper-specific and unimpressed by power; **deadpan is a tool, not
the constant** — the register is set by topic gravity (`storytelling-grammar.md §1.4`), not a flat dry
drone. Open on a **mock-sincere thesis**; **close by deflating, not resolving.** Cheap authority via insider
"of course, Softbank…" markers (Boyle).

**§4 — Humor.** Dial = **set by topic gravity, not a fixed rate** (`storytelling-grammar.md §1.4`):
storytelling-first and witty — more comedic than any pure finance/history channel — running hot on
money-absurdity/farce, wry and sparse on villainy, and **OFF entirely on human cost** (grammar §4). The old
"~1 dry line/min" cap is retired; the guardrail is quality, not scarcity — a forced joke that carries no
fact reads as vapor. Locks (universal §1d-V): every joke carries a fact (fact-rider delete-test, §2.4) ·
target = the fool/institution, never the viewer · smart-not-cringe, evergreen-only (§2.6) · the humor bar
(§2.5). Devices: by-X-I-mean-Y (HAI), analogy-as-indictment (Crayon), tonal-modulation-by-act (Crayon), the
full toolbox in §2.3.

**§5 — Pacing/retention.** **Narration-led beats fast cutting** — a new concrete referent every 3–6s in
the VO matters more than cut rate; visuals can breathe at 4–9s (PolyMatter/Crayon/Wendover all run
slower than the 3–8s ceiling). Opening structures to rotate: protagonist-walkthrough (Wendover),
dream-then-turn (RealLifeLore), thesis-first-then-prove (Wendover, an alternative to withholding).
Mid-video **"let's go through the problems one at a time" ladder-announcement** re-hook (RealLifeLore).

**§6 — Explaining a hard concept.** Standardize: **weighable prop** (one reusable object per mechanism —
a bathtub for the money supply), **prediction-then-violation** open, **recursive concrete number** (run
the real dollar chain, kill the formula), **one-sentence recompression** as the click beat, and
**analogy-as-indictment** (the analogy exposes the flaw = payload + vindication in one sentence). Finance
analogy library (fractional reserve, bond seesaw, naked-CDS grandma, ratings food-critic) in
business-money.md.

**§7 — Research depth / credibility (YMYL).** **Dated primary artifact in the first 10s** (ColdFusion's
Forbes cover), **named sources inline**, a **humility hedge on every stat**, and **absurd-decimal
specificity** (Wendover's "$68.75," "0.0013 in"). **Steelman-then-kill** for fairness. Unlike Magnates
(authoritative voice, thin citation), our YMYL gate requires the real sources *behind* the confident
register.

**§8 — Visual style (TWO BOARD CORRECTIONS).**
- **Crayon Capital is NOT literal crayon.** Its art is **clean digital vector cartoon** (South Park /
  OverSimplified family — pale oval heads, thick black outlines, dot eyes, big expressive mouths). The
  "crayon/hand" warmth actually lives in **handwritten-marker title cards + hand-drawn whiteboard
  charts.** → For us: keep character/object art **clean 2.5D vector**; put the hand-feel in **marker-style
  title cards, hand-drawn charts, and one red alarm accent.** This resolves our open "specific
  illustration variant" question and de-risks clone-bait (the literal crayon look is already mass-cloned).
- **PolyMatter is NOT flat vector** in its modern top videos — it's **real drone/stock footage + a
  green-and-white motion-graphic annotation layer** (arrows, call-out numbers, dashed measurement
  lines). Treat it as a reference for **annotation/typography-over-media**, not for an illustrated look.
  Kurzgesagt (aspirational illustration) and RealLifeLore (maps) remain the illustrated-visual refs.

**§9 — Narrative storytelling.** **Three-act, chapter-per-beat**, chapters **titled like fight cards**
("X vs Y" keeps a named antagonist on screen), **each boundary a scored music beat** (Magnates).
**Authority-through-calm** = ~150 wpm + compressed loudness range + **"outsourced alarm"** (splice real
news clips so the narrator never raises his voice — ColdFusion). **Investigation →
primary-source-as-evidence → pause-as-verdict** (Coffeezilla, fully faceless-compatible: highlight the
filing on screen, ask the detective question in VO, then a `[PAUSE]` while the document sits — the
viewer concludes the verdict; never narrate "he's guilty" — see defamation note in business-money.md).

**Reference honesty flags (reinforced).** Crayon Capital's scale is unverifiable (launched ~Apr 2025,
~24 videos, subs unconfirmed) — a packaging/watchability teacher, NOT a durability proof. The humor
channels (Casually Explained, HAI, Sam O'Nella, Internet Historian) are human-comedy-writer moats —
borrow the *mechanism* and the *fact-carrying discipline*, not the density or the edgy content (Sam
O'Nella's slur-adjacent puns, IH's "retardy" pun, profanity — all off-brand). Coffeezilla's opinion
disclaimer failed in court; out-discipline him.
