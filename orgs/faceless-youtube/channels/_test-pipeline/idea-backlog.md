### WHTY-001 — What Happens To You When Your Car Sinks
- **Format:** long-form (~12 min) + shorts bench (5; what-if band 3–6)
- **Hook premise (first 5s):** You are already in the water. The nose of the car is tipping down, the
  dash lights are still on, and the footwell is cold around your ankles. The clock starts now.
- **Angle / POV:** The signature "Countdown" — a real-time second-person survival clock. Not a safety
  tutorial and not a compilation; a minute-by-minute simulation of what the water does to the car and
  to your body, with the escape window treated as a ticking loop, not a lesson.
- **Emotional lever:** morbid awe / dread — personal-scale body-in-extremity (matches channel's
  locked lever). The dread is the closing window, not gore.
- **Talking points / beats:**
  - The float phase: why the car floats for 30–120s and why that time feels like safety but isn't.
  - The pressure trap: why the doors physically will not open once water is above the sill (the
    named antagonist — water pressure, personified as the thing holding the door shut).
  - The window is the only exit; the order of operations under a countdown.
  - The submersion turn: electrical death, disorientation in the dark, cold-shock gasp reflex.
  - Withheld peak (final 20%): the last ten seconds after the cabin fills — the calm that isn't calm.
- **Title options:** 1) What Happens To You When Your Car Sinks  2) You Have 60 Seconds Before the
  Doors Won't Open  3) Your Car Just Went Into the Water. Here's Every Second.
- **Why original:** backlog is empty; distinct from rivals because it runs the sinking-car scenario at
  documentary length as one continuous second-person real-time clock (the channel's "Countdown"
  format), not as a 30s short or a listicle of tips. No rival cloned.
- **Score:** 84/100 (hook 19, lever 19, demand 13, monetization 6, diff 12, fit 10, feasibility 5)
