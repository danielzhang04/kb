## Payload (self-contained, closes in-short)
The door won't open because water pressure presses flat on the whole panel (hundreds of pounds), not
because of the lock. The exit is the side window: the windshield is laminated (won't break in time),
the side glass is tempered (hit a corner and it drops to gravel). Full mechanism + fix, closed in-short.

## VO + cues

[B-ROLL: frame one — POV hand yanking the door handle, door not moving. Repeated pull. Motion on frame
one.]
The door won't open — and it's got nothing to do with the lock.

[B-ROLL: exterior cross-section — waterline outside higher than inside; a wall of hazard-orange arrows
pressing flat on the whole door panel.]
The water outside is higher than the water inside, and it's pressing on the whole door at once. Flat.

[B-ROLL: a shoulder throws against the door — nothing; then two figures shove together — still nothing.]
So you shove, and nothing moves. Physics doesn't care how many of you there are. You're trying to push
a swimming pool out of the way with one arm.

[B-ROLL: a hard object taps the windshield — it webs and holds; then taps the corner of the side window
— the whole pane bursts to gravel.]
Don't fight the door. Go for the side window. [BEAT] The windshield is laminated, layered like safety
glass, and you won't break it in time. The side glass is tempered. Hit a corner, not the middle, and
the whole pane turns to gravel.

[B-ROLL: the cabin floods completely; the door drifts open on its own.]
The door does open, eventually. Right when the cabin is full and it no longer matters. [PAUSE] Take the
window.
