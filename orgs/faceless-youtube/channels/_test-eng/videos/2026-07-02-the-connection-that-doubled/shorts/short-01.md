[B-ROLL: extreme close-up of a single steel rod-and-nut connection under strain — first frame, on-screen text "This joint held twice its design load"] This one connection held up two walkways in a hotel atrium. [PAUSE] The original design ran a single rod from the ceiling through both. Then someone cut the rod in two to make it easier to build — and that doubled the load on this exact spot. [B-ROLL: force diagram, the load number ticking from W to 2W] Nobody re-ran the math. The seam let go, and both walkways came down.
