[B-ROLL: macro shot of one nut bearing on a welded box-beam seam — first frame, on-screen text "The whole walkway hung from this"] The entire walkway hung from a single nut. [PAUSE] It bore directly on a box beam made of two steel channels welded toe-to-toe — and the weakest point was the seam where they met. [B-ROLL: annotated diagram, red circle on the welded seam, load arrow pushing up through the nut] Put twice the load on it, and the nut pulls straight through the steel. That is exactly what happened.
