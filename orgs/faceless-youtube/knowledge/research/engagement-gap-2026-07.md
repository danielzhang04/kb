## Ranked gaps → doctrine recommendation (N2 fold order)

1. **Dead-frame share / within-shot motion (8–14×, THE gap):** revoke the 2026-07-10 dead-still
   locked-look. Normal held tableaux carry subtle life; an explicit line-specific still remains valid.
   Elements/charts populate LIVE timed to the spoken noun, not pre-complete.
2. **SFX coverage:** inspect every entrance, draw-on, reveal, number, pivot, and gravity turn, then
   explicitly cover or abstain. The 3.81/min result and reference rates diagnose; they do not impose a
   floor, and a deliberate hold does not automatically earn a sting.
3. **VO macro-dynamics:** per-beat delivery direction (eleven_v3 tags): lift/accelerate on absurdity
   buttons, then turn restrained and direct for concise human consequence without automatically slowing
   the whole passage. Register locked; dynamics vary within it. This converts the
   already-written humor into landed humor ("delivery + visual-punctuation gap, not a writing gap").
4. **Cut rate (paired lever):** this channel has limited meaningful animation, so faster cutting is the
   practical default. Start new stills-based plans around 2–5 seconds per shot; holds over roughly 6 seconds
   need a real progressive reveal or a legibility/gravity reason. Ambient drift/bob keeps life in a frame
   but does not cancel the need for more cuts.
5. **Beat-typed texture (structural):** winners vary texture by beat type. Absurdity and mechanism
   passages get denser cuts and motion; concise consequence stays respectful and less comic while
   retaining forward narration, restrained music, and visual life. Uniform grade is the error.
