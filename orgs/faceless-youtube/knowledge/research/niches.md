# Research — niches (consolidated, single list)

Medium-depth research, updated 2026-07-01. Two earlier passes + a YouTube-performance pass, now
**collapsed into ONE ranked list.** Optimized for the user's goal: **make the most money, from a
single niche whose ONE idea works as both a Short and a long-form, is fully AI-generatable (script +
visuals + voice), and is actually performing on YouTube now.** RPM = US, after YouTube's 45% cut.
Sources are creator blogs + named channel data; treat figures as directional. No niche committed yet.

## Why dual-format is the right structure

The money is in **long-form ad revenue** (Shorts RPM ~$0.03–0.10 is negligible). Dual-format wins
because **one AI pipeline feeds both**: Shorts do free discovery and funnel viewers into the high-RPM
long-form. Target = **highest RPM × works as both × AI-generatable × proven.**

## The 3-part dual-format screen

A niche qualifies only with all three: **(1) atomic hook** (<60s standalone) · **(2) narrative spine**
(8–20 min at ≥40% retention) · **(3) asset reuse** (Short = trailer cut from the long-form).

## Recommendation

**Maximum money → #1 Business & Money documentaries** — best blend of top RPM, original narrative
(policy-safe), clean dual-format ("the one decision that killed X" → full doc), and a fully
AI-generatable pipeline; proven by multiple multi-million-sub faceless channels. **Viral reach →
#2 what-if / engineered curiosity** (lean animated). **Horror (#5)** is a legitimate volume bet if you
value watch-time over per-view rate.

**Shorts are a first-class criterion, not just a funnel.** Viral Shorts grow the channel ~3× faster and
build the subscriber base that monetizes the long-form (plus modest direct Shorts income). So weight
Shorts virality alongside RPM — which lifts #2 (extreme), #5 (very high), #6 relative to #1.
**The cheat code that maximizes BOTH axes:** run a viral curiosity FORMAT (what-if / "why you…" hooks)
on HIGH-CPM TOPICS (money, tech, health) — e.g. *"What happens if you never pay your credit card?"* =
scroll-stopping Short + personal-finance long-form RPM. This is the strongest single strategy for the
"max money AND Shorts perform" goal.

## Virality psychology (hook levers)

High-arousal emotion (surprise/fear/awe) · curiosity gap (the hook IS the open question) · identity/
relatability · status/self-interest (also the highest-CPM motives) · simplicity (one idea per video).

## Avoid (overplayed / policy-fragile 2026)

Generic Reddit-story narration, true-crime gore (monetization-restricted + crowded), generic top-10 /
"amazing facts", sleep/meditation & study ambience (saturation + reuse-policy fragility), no-facecam
gaming, celebrity news, movie/TV recaps (copyright). The low-effort era is dead — survivors win on
story depth or a unique angle.
