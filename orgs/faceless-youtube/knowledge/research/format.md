# Research — Shorts vs long-form & video length

Medium-depth research, 2026-07-01. **Status:** general verdict below is settled; the *exact* mix for a
given channel is REVISIT-WHEN-NICHE-IS-CHOSEN (user wants "whichever has more impact" decided per
niche). RPM = US, after the 45% cut.

## Verdict

**Long-form is where the money is** — Shorts pay just 3–14% of long-form RPM and have a *harder*
monetization bar. Run a **hybrid funnel**: Shorts for reach, long-form for revenue. Length is
**per-niche** — the old universal 8–15 min sweet spot was retired 2026-07-01 after the doctrine
research pass; see the per-niche band table below (and the niche playbook files) for the current
bands.

- "More impact" depends on the metric: **revenue impact → long-form.** **Growth impact → run both**
  (combined grows subs ~3× faster / ~41% faster overall).
- **Long ↔ Short are algorithmically decoupled since late 2025** — Shorts no longer passively lift
  long-form recommendations. The funnel has to be engineered explicitly (pinned comment, description
  link, end-screen). Publish long-form first, then stagger shorts every 2–3 days.

## Shorts vs long-form

| | Shorts | Long-form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RPM | ~$0.03–0.10 | ~$1–30 (avg ~$3; finance far higher) |
| The math | 10,000,000 views ≈ $700 | 100,000 views ≈ $700 |
| Monetization bar | 1k subs + **10M Shorts views/90d** (brutal) | 1k subs + **4,000 watch hrs/12mo** (easier) |
| Revenue model | Shared regional ad pool, music cost deducted | Pre / mid / post-roll ads on your video |
| Role | Discovery funnel | The earner |

## Optimal length (per niche — updated 2026-07-01)

- **8-minute line is non-negotiable** — under 8 min = no mid-roll ads. Crossing it lifts RPM 30–100%
  (~+50% typical).
- **The old universal "8–15 min sweet spot" is retired.** After the 2026-07-01 doctrine research
  pass, length bands are validated per-niche against 2025-2026 retention data (Retention Rabbit
  + AIR Media-Tech). Full band + rationale + retention benchmarks live in each niche playbook
  under `knowledge/research/niche-playbooks/<niche>.md` §Length band.
- **Retention still beats length** — go longer only while holding the length band's benchmark AVD.

| Niche | Target length (long-form) | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business & Money docs** | 10–15 min OR 25–45 min | Two sweet spots (collapse spine vs. deep-case autopsy). **16–24 min is a "death zone"** — too long for tight-cut audience, too short for deep-case density. |
| **What-if scenarios** | 12–20 min primary; 20–90 min blockbuster | RealLifeLore's proven 8→90 arc. Blockbuster hypothetical earns 40%+ AVD if withheld peak + macro-re-hooks land. |
| **AI tools** | 10–14 min | Tool half-life is short; format-heavy content (workflows) ages slower than tool-heavy (model reviews). Weekly news roundups 12–18. |
| **Engineering disasters** | 18–28 min | Native long-form niche. Cold-start ceiling ~30 min for new AI-gen entrants; below 15 min can't fit the STP × 5–7 cycles the format needs. |
| **Horror / internet lore** | 22–35 min; 60–90 min prestige | Under 15 min signals "list channel" and lowers CPM. Appointment-viewing behavior lets 60–120+ min work once trust is built (Wendigoon). |
| **Micro-health** | 8–12 min (case-walkthrough up to 15) | YMYL trust-vs-length tradeoff: every extra min = more claims = more misinformation surface. |

## The funnel (why not either/or)

- Both formats → subs grow ~3× faster, ~41% faster overall, than single-format channels. Running
  both = **~2.5× total channel watch time** in year one vs single-format.
- **Late-2025 change (nuanced):** Shorts and long-form are now **algorithmically decoupled at the
  discovery layer** — a viral Short no longer *passively* lifts long-form recommendations. What
  died is the automatic spillover, NOT the funnel itself. An engineered Short → long-form
  click-through is still a high-value signal — it feeds long-form session watch time, which the
  algorithm still weights heavily. The change: you have to earn the click, not receive it.
- **Funnel mechanics that still work** (data from Phase 1 research):
  - Pinned-comment link on Shorts → **+20% total channel watch time**.
  - Description link on Shorts → **+12% conversions among non-subscribers**.
  - Shorts with a link element → **~4.5% CTR** industry average.
  - Reverse-repurpose (viral Short → long-form expansion) → **+40% long-form watch time** vs.
    cold-launching on the same topic. Promote a short when it exceeds ~5× your channel baseline.
- **Per-niche shorts cadence per long-form** (universal.md §10): business 2–4 / what-if 3–6 / AI
  2–3 / engineering 1–3 / horror-lore 4–8 / health 3–5.
- **Publish long-form first**, then stagger shorts every 2–3 days across 2–3 weeks. Bursting
  shorts on release day cannibalizes the seed audience.
- **CTA to long-form goes in the pinned comment, not on-screen** — because CTR to another video
  is NOT a *Shorts-specific* ranking signal (only replay rate, swipe-through, engagement are).
  The click-through still matters for your channel's overall session watch time; it just doesn't
  help THAT Short rank better.
