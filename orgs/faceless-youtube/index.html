3 · Niche research

Verdict: One niche, optimized for max money from a dual-format idea (viral Short + high-RPM long-form), fully AI-generatable, proven on YouTube now. Money lands in long-form ad revenue; viral Shorts are the growth engine that builds the audience (and add some direct income). Top pick: Business & Money documentaries for pure RPM; What-if / engineered curiosity if weighting Shorts virality & growth.

4 · Shorts vs long-form

Verdict: Long-form is where the money is — Shorts pay just 3–14% of long-form RPM and have a harder monetization bar. Run a hybrid funnel: Shorts for reach, long-form for revenue. The funnel (why not either/or)

  • Channels running both grow subs 3× faster, ~41% faster overall, than single-format channels.
  • What changed late 2025: Shorts and long-form are now algorithmically decoupled at the discovery layer — a viral Short no longer passively lifts your long-form recommendations. What died is the automatic spillover, not the funnel itself.
  • Engineered click-through IS still a high-value signal. Data (Phase 1 research): pinned-comment link on Shorts = +20% total channel watch time; description link = +12% conversions among non-subscribers; Shorts with a link element average ~4.5% CTR; creators running both formats see ~2.5× total watch time in year one vs. single-format. The click itself feeds long-form session watch time — the algorithm still weights that. The change is that you have to earn the click, not receive it.
  • Per-niche shorts cadence (universal.md §10): business 2–4 / what-if 3–6 / AI 2–3 / engineering 1–3 / horror-lore 4–8 / health 3–5. Publish long-form first, then stagger shorts every 2–3 days.
  • CTA to long-form goes in the pinned comment, not on-screen — because CTR to another video is not a Shorts-specific ranking signal (only replay rate, swipe-through, engagement are). The click-through still matters for your channel's overall session watch time; it just doesn't help THAT Short rank better.

5 · Virality psychology (rewritten 2026-07-01)

Anchor: Berger & Milkman, Journal of Marketing Research 2012 — arousal, not valence, predicts virality. Awe, anger, anxiety, amusement spread. Low-arousal sadness doesn't, even when equally intense. Every content decision downstream of this.

Doctrine: the video is a story that happens to teach — not a lesson that happens to have visuals. Frontiers in Communication 2020 (200+ science videos): high-story-status videos were 3.16× more likely to be popular; moderately-to-highly emotionally-activating videos ~3× more likely to hit high view counts.

The 10-lever emotional taxonomy

A viable channel picks ONE dominant lever and defends it. Cross-lever channels get flagged as templated slop — the channels that survived the July-2025 policy all lock one. Full mechanism + faceless-fit + examples in knowledge/research/niche-playbooks/universal.md §1a.

  • Curiosity gap (Loewenstein) — the carrier for every other lever; it packages them for the click.
  • Morbid curiosity (Scrivner 2021 scale — 4 dimensions: dangerous minds / paranormal danger / bodily injuries / interpersonal violence). Native to horror/lore + micro-health.
  • Awe (Keltner & Haidt 2003 — vastness + schema accommodation). Native to what-if + Kurzgesagt-tier science.
  • Morbid awe / dread — vicarious body-in-extremity. Zack D. Films' whole lever.
  • Righteous anger / moral outrage (Crockett; Brady & Van Bavel). Coffeezilla's lane; post-2024 needs evidence pairing.
  • Vindication / forbidden knowledge / "you were lied to" — epistemic superiority. Reward is being right, not being told.
  • Schadenfreude / status-collapse spectatorship. Business-money niche primary.
  • Hope-porn / aspirational envy — MrBeast lever. Requires a visible face at the moment of the win — hard for faceless.
  • Tribal identity signaling (PNAS 2024). Lets viewers assert group identity via the comments.
  • Wonder + smart-feeling (puzzle/riddle) — Bright Side lever, lower arousal but broadly shareable.

Faceless-friendly levers (no face needed): morbid curiosity, awe, dread, wonder, outrage, forbidden knowledge, schadenfreude, tribal identity. Hope-porn is off-limits without a face — mirror-neuron mimicry requires visible facial reaction.

Doctrine hits enforced by every skill

  • Entertainment, not education. No "and that's why…" or "the takeaway is…" closers. Emotional payoff, not the lesson.
  • CCN rule (Paddy Galloway): every video must serve Core fans + Casual returners + New viewers.
  • Retention cadence 2025-2026: 55% of viewers exit inside 60s (Retention Rabbit 2025); value must land in 7s. Micro-interrupt every 30–45s (tightened from 60–90s). Macro re-hook every 2–3 min. Mandatory mid-video re-arm at 55–65% runtime — the documented exodus zone. Withheld peak in final 20% is a hard rule.
  • Long ↔ Short algorithmically decoupled late 2025. No passive lift; the funnel is engineered explicitly. Shorts cadence per niche: business 2–4, what-if 3–6, AI 2–3, engineering 1–3, horror/lore 4–8, health 3–5.

6 · Pipeline architecture

Two layers talking through a shared markdown folder. Strategy layer (interactive: niche research, channel DNA, weekly review) + autonomous production layer in Claude Code. Every skill reads a file and writes a file — debuggable, resumable, provider-swappable. Channels are data (copy a config folder), not separate pipelines.

ONE-TIME PER CHANNEL (then frozen) A Niche + sellability [BUILD: idea-generator] B Name + branding prompts [BUILD] + [CONNECT: image gen] + manual YouTube/WIPO check C Voice identity (lock 1 ID) [CONNECT: ElevenLabs] D Avatar (SKIP by default) [CONNECT: image/video gen] E Channel DNA file written [BUILD -> channels/<name>/dna.md] PER-VIDEO LOOP (scheduled) 1 Idea + topic/title research [BUILD: idea-generator] (live topic-scouting on research channels) ★ PICK + EDIT IDEA === HUMAN GATE (the only one before a draft) === 2 Deep research (deep path) [BUILD: researcher] -> wraps [native deep-research] -> research.md (fact-ledger) 3a Long-form script [BUILD: long-form-writer] staged writers-room, leashed to the ledger 3b Shorts (self-contained) [BUILD: shorts-writer] derived from the finished long-form 4 Metadata (title/desc/tags) [BUILD: metadata-writer] 5 Visual prompts / shot list [BUILD: visual-prompt-writer] (dense first 60s) 6 Voiceover } parallel [CONNECT: ElevenLabs TTS] 7 Visuals } parallel [CONNECT: image/animation gen] 8 Render + assemble (JSON) [BUILD: render-builder] -> [CONNECT: JSON2Video] 9 Compliance + QA gate [BUILD: compliance-check] === HUMAN GATE === 10 Publish [BUILD: publish-queue] -> [CONNECT: YouTube Data API v3] 11 Analytics feedback [BUILD: analytics-reporter] -> writes back to dna.md Orchestrated by [BUILD: content-manager] on a scheduler

Schedulers grouped by cadence, never per channel: produce-and-queue (daily), publish-approved (daily), analytics-digest (daily/weekly), strategy-refresh (weekly). Human gate before publish for at least the first few months.

8 · Policy & hard constraints

Inauthentic-content policy (2025-07-15, enforced)

Templated near-duplicate video = whole-channel demonetization. Enforcement is real — reporting cites ~$10M creator revenue and 35M subs wiped. YouTube explicitly allows AI-assisted content; it kills the interchangeable template. Every video needs materially different substance. Excluded entirely: scraped TikTok/IG reposts, movie/TV-clip channels (Content ID / strikes / termination). idea-generator is forbidden from "clone this rival."

Quota (corrected & verified Jun 2026)

The old ~6 uploads/day figure is obsolete. As of 4 Dec 2025, videos.insert dropped from ~1,600 → ~100 units, so the free 10k/day tier now allows ~100 uploads/day. Still: pace uploads slowly (bursts read as spam), and never spin up extra Cloud projects to multiply quota — violates Terms, suspends all projects. ✔ confirmed

Audit gate (day-one gotcha)

An unaudited OAuth project uploads every video locked to private and the API can't make it public. Submit the API client for YouTube's compliance audit early; keep the human gate on while pending.
