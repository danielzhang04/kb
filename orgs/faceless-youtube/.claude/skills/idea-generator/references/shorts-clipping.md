# Deriving short-form from a long-form idea

How `idea-generator` derives a shorts bench from one long-form idea. The bench is now **sized to
the niche's cadence band, not a fixed cap** (updated 2026-07-01 — the old 10-archetype ceiling is
retired).

## Bench sizing (per-niche, from universal.md §10)

The AIR Media-Tech 18K-channel study + per-niche analysis established that shorts-per-long-form
varies wildly by niche. Generate the full bench at ideation time; the scriptwriter cuts weak ones
rather than pads to a number.

| Niche | Shorts per long-form | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Business/money docs | 2–4 | Search-driven audience; carved doc-shorts feel like ads; standalone stat/reveal shorts outperform |
| What-if | 3–6 | Scenarios carve cleanly into standalone hooks; each short can stand alone |
| AI tools | 2–3 | Search-driven; tools change monthly so shorts fatigue faster |
| Engineering disasters | 1–3 | Narrative is the value; disaster carves risk feeling exploitative |
| Horror / internet lore | 4–8 | Highest tolerance for shorts volume; each tier/mystery/beat can be a standalone short |
| Micro-health | 3–5 | Native short-form; long-form is the depth product |

**"As many as have integrity, not a fixed count."** If a long-form only supports 3 strong shorts,
generate 3. If it supports 8, generate 8. Cut weak angles rather than pad.

## Why a bench + why publish only a subset

One long video can technically yield 10+ clip angles, but the sweet spot is generating the
*niche-band* count and posting most or all of them at the niche's cadence. Post 2–3 for
business/AI/engineering; 4–8 for horror/lore.

**Publishing cadence (from universal.md §10):**
- **Long-form ships first** (technical requirement + audience seeding).
- **Then stagger shorts every 2–3 days** across 2–3 weeks. Bursting all shorts on release day
  cannibalizes the seed audience and starves the long-form.
- Optionally: one teaser short 12–24 hr BEFORE the long-form (links to nothing, acts as a promise).
- Pin the long-form URL in each short's comments. Since late 2025, Shorts are algorithmically
  decoupled from long-form at the discovery layer (no passive recommendation-lift), so the pinned
  link is the primary funnel signal you control — but the click-through it produces still
  materially boosts channel session watch time (+20% from pinned-comment link per Phase 1
  research). Engineer the click; don't expect it to happen automatically.

## The engagement rules every short angle must follow

A short is not a random slice of the long video, and it is **not a teaser for it** — it's a
**self-contained closed loop** that delivers AND closes one concrete payload on its own (universal.md
§11-0). If the payoff needs the long-form to land, it's a long-form idea in disguise, not a short. The
2025-2026 sweet spot is:

- **Length: 15–30 seconds** (80%+ retention possible). Up to 45s if the payoff is visual. 3-min
  Shorts (unlocked Oct 2024) are almost never optimal — they compete with long-form retention
  thresholds without the RPM benefit.
- **Hook window: 1.3–1.8 seconds** — the actual swipe-decision window per 2025 measurements. 50–60%
  of drop-off happens in the first 3 seconds. Frame ONE is the hook — motion + on-screen text +
  a declarative sentence starting.
- **Structure: `hook → context → payoff → loop`, all inside the short.** Front-load the surprising
  concrete fact in second one, build it across ~3–5 escalating beats, **close the loop** (answer what
  you opened), and write the last line to flow back into the first for a seamless replay. Never end on
  "watch the full video to find out" — that withholding is the #1 way shorts flop.
- **Burned-in captions mandatory** — +15–25% retention. Highest-leverage formatting choice.
- **On-frame text at frame one** — 3–7 words. 85% of Shorts watchers start silent.
- **B-roll density weighted to the first seconds** — 72% of viral Shorts >1M views use fast-paced
  editing with minimal dead air.
- **9:16 aspect ratio mandatory** (95%+ mobile viewing).
- **CTA to long-form goes in the pinned comment, not on-screen.** CTR to another video is NOT a
  Shorts ranking signal (only replay rate, swipe-through, engagement).
- **Retention targets:** ≥70% AVD is good; viral shorts average ~76%; ≥75% gets 3× algorithmic
  push to new audiences. Under 30% 3-second retention effectively kills distribution.

## The clip archetype library

Draw short angles from these distinct shapes (variety is the point — don't make N variations of one
shape). Each angle = {archetype, the 0–1.5s hook line, the **self-contained payload it closes**}. See
universal.md §4d for the full short-form hook archetype library. **Every shape must resolve inside the
short** — the pinned long-form link is a bonus for the minority who want depth, never the payoff:

1. **Shocking stat** — cold-open on the single most surprising number, then say what it *means* in-short.
2. **The reveal / twist** — set up the turn and **deliver it fully** in the short; the payoff is the twist itself, not a promise of it.
3. **Counterintuitive claim** — "everything you think about X is backwards," then the one proof, stated and closed.
4. **Single-mechanism explainer** — isolate one beat/mechanism from the long video and explain it completely, standalone.
5. **Curiosity-gap question** — pose the exact question, **then answer it** in-short (the gap you open, you close).
6. **What most people miss** — the second- or third-order consequence nobody mentions, spelled out.
7. **Before / after (or comparison)** — two states side by side; the contrast is the hook and the payoff.
8. **Myth-bust** — state the common belief, puncture it, and give the real mechanism in one line.
9. **Relatable stakes** — "this is why your paycheck / your rent / your savings…"; make it personal, then explain it.
10. **Micro-list** — "the 3 reasons X" delivered *completely and fast*, or the single strongest one fully — not "here's #1, watch for the rest."

**More archetypes available in universal.md §4d** (Pattern-interrupt visual / On-frame text
promise / "Imagine if…" hypothetical / POV framing / Confession mid-thought / Number-anchored
first frame / "This shouldn't exist" / Direct second-person accusation / Micro-story cold open /
Comparison-frame split-screen). Each names its psychological lever so a writer can invent variants.

**Don't clone all 10 archetypes for every idea.** Pick the ones that fit the specific idea's beat
structure and lever. A business-money collapse video probably wants: shocking stat, boast-vs-
collapse split, forbidden-mechanism reveal, one damning quote. A horror/lore case-file probably
wants: found-footage cold open, artifact reveal, still-unsolved cliffhanger, comment-section
discovery. Match the archetype to the niche's dominant hook flavor (in the niche playbook file).

## Reverse-repurpose signal (Short → Long)

Track shorts performance. **When a short exceeds 5× your channel baseline views**, it becomes a
long-form candidate for future ideation runs. Reverse-repurpose data: +40% long-form watch time
vs. cold-launching a new long-form on the same topic. Only expand shorts that have 12+ min of
actual substance to unfold — padded expansion tanks retention.

Signals to promote a short to long-form:
- View ratio 5–10× your channel-average short views over 2 weeks.
- Comment cluster of "wait, tell me more" / "when's the full video."
- Save-to-share ratio elevated (audience wants to come back).
- Non-subscriber share >70% (new audience discovered).
- Replay rate >10% (exceptional engagement).

## Output shape (per long-form idea)

List the short bench compactly in the idea's brief — archetype · hook line · publish|bench.
Example (business-money niche, band = 2–4 shorts):

```
Shorts bench (4):
1. [Shocking stat · publish]        "40% less than 2019" — the paycheck number
2. [Reveal · publish]               the one chart that proves the squeeze
3. [Counterintuitive · publish]     you didn't get poorer — the dollar did
4. [Curiosity-gap · bench]          why do raises feel like pay cuts now?
```

Example (horror/lore niche, band = 4–8 shorts):

```
Shorts bench (6):
1. [Found-footage cold open · publish]  15s of the deleted recording, no context
2. [Artifact reveal · publish]          the waveform anomaly, one line of analysis
3. [Still-unsolved · publish]           "they never explained what this was"
4. [Second-person walkthrough · publish] "you're tuning through empty bands at 3 AM"
5. [Named entity reveal · bench]        the folklore reference, one image
6. [Comment discovery · bench]          "a viewer sent me this last week"
```
