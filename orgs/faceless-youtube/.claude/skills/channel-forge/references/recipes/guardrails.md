# Recipe: guardrails stage

The channel-specific compliance guardrails (the Enforcement Contract's deferred compliance layer, made concrete
per channel). Playbook-authored. **Gather** the niche's real policy exposure + playbook compliance. **Reuse-first:**
the playbook rules + an exemplar's block. **Generate** the risk->mitigation set. **Critics:** covers real risks,
differentiation-compliant, discloses AI, keeps the audit gate. **Gate:** human approves → dna.md Guardrails.
