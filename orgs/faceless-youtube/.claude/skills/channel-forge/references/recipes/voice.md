# Recipe: voice stage

The narrator voice (or voices). **Gather** niche + doctrine + register. **Reuse-first:** the voice-lab method +
library, not a specific voice. **Generate** an auditioned shortlist (N voices for multi-voice channels) on a
board. **Critics:** register-fit, distinct, low-drift; the human ear-gates feel. **Gate:** human locks the
voice(s) + settings → dna.md Voiceover config.
