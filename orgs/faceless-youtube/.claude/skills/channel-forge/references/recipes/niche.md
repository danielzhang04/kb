# Recipe: niche stage

Proves the convergence engine cleanly (full loop, no image-gen weight; stage 1).

**Gather** the human's channel intent + `knowledge/research/niches.md`. **Reuse-first:** lean on
`idea-generator` and existing niche research rather than inventing a niche framework. **Generate** N candidate
lanes, each with its one-lever hook and a why-it-can-win. **Critics** (beyond the Contract): each lane must be
differentiated from named rivals, payload-first, in a monetizable RPM band, have durable idea-supply, carry
**strong short-form AND long-form virality**, be **faceless / AI-generatable** — and rather than veto a lane
our *current* pipeline can't render, FLAG whether it reuses our existing pipeline (cheap/fast) or needs a NEW
pipeline build (a cost carried forward to the `production-pipeline` stage, held to a no-slop bar) — and land
a **high awe/curiosity hook**. On a bare "more options" request, re-run the whole loop with these baked in and
generate a FRESH batch (exclude lanes already shown) — the human re-specifies nothing.
**Converge** to a ranked shortlist; **present** an Artifact board. **Gate:** the human picks + edits one lane;
on approval it seeds `dna.md` Identity and the walk advances.
