# Recipe: channel-page stage

The YouTube channel PAGE — everything a viewer sees that is not a video: About description, links,
business email, channel keywords, avatar/banner, unsubscribed-trailer policy, home shelves. **Gather**
the locked identity + brand assets + the measured genre skeleton (harvest: one-line positioning first;
bimodal length; faceless no-product channels carry 0-4 links; trailer = a representative banger or
empty until one exists; keywords optional). **Reuse-first:** The Second Take's `channel-page.md` as
the template. **Generate** the copy options. **Critics:** per the frontmatter checks. **Gate:** human
approves, then applies in Studio — the file is the source of truth the Studio state must match.
