# Doctrine reconciliation plan

## Decision frame

This is the section-level execution plan for all 60 rows in `era-map.md`. It follows the evidence rather
than trying to manufacture a majority of rollback labels: 31 rows have direct present-positive evidence
and 11 evidence-none rows are bound to default to KEEP-PRESENT. Of the 16 rows that do change, 12 are
reversion-bearing (three REVERTs and nine HYBRIDs) and four are deletion/relocation cleanups. Thus the
changed doctrine is mostly reversion plus selected modern protection, while the stack as a whole does not
discard working control machinery.

Verdict totals: **KEEP-PRESENT 44 · REVERT 3 · HYBRID 9 · DELETE 4**.

The seven protected wins are invariants of this plan: seeded cast + STEP-1 cards; three-state review;
HARD no-op delta lint; closed-world poses; wave parallelization; suffix byte-lock; and single-writer
stamping. No row below weakens any of them. “Layer move” always means delete at the source after the
canonical destination is confirmed; it never means copying the same doctrine into two homes.

The pending Bricks findings are consequences, not new rules:

- **Blue-orange:** neutralize the existing suffix phrase and retain the already-written beat-local palette
  wording.
- **Character collapse:** put the existing true-subject/occupancy clause before the non-literal move menu;
  do not restore anonymous/everyman foregrounds.
- **Prop-text bake:** keep the current supplied/verbatim text law and test prose-named props live. No cited
  era demonstrably prevented engine-invented lettering on a prose-named prop, so this remains OPEN rather
  than becoming a speculative rule or lint function.
