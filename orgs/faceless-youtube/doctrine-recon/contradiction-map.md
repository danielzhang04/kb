## Ten worst live conflicts / overlaps and observable symptom

1. **Suffix warm bias vs per-scene palette freedom** — Grammar suffix requires warm bias; Bible permits cool/desaturated beats. **Symptom:** blue-orange/temperature collapse and repeated cream/charcoal scenes.
2. **`No text` descriptor vs baked-text contract** — Bible head tells the engine not to draw what VPW/Schema require it to draw. **Symptom:** prop-text bake/garble, caught only in pixel review.
3. **Payload-last vs suffix-last** — authored payload must close, yet Forge appends the style tail after it. **Symptom:** authored payload loses salience; suffix-tail/template convergence.
4. **Two-voice claim vs multi-input runtime** — Grammar says two style voices only; Forge additionally injects crowd/rig/policy and a style-tile seed affects images. **Symptom:** template convergence and authored-scene dilution.
5. **Payload-led openness vs universal “filled edge-to-edge” review** — Grammar permits air; Bible/IG turn density into a pass condition. **Symptom:** openness loss, foreground cropping, and fewer small/distant figures.
6. **Vantage unlock vs injected plate framing override** — Grammar asks for class/payload-driven vantage; Forge imposes frontal/mild-oblique, eye-level plate geometry. **Symptom:** centered/frontal template convergence; depth asserted rather than staged.
7. **Figure-tier doctrine duplicated across seven homes** — one policy is simultaneously prose, schema, generator, style, and data documentation. **Symptom:** any future tier edit can reintroduce figure collapse (base/anonymous/crowd rule mismatch) even while individual lint checks pass.
8. **Crowd emotion split between prose and rigid template** — Grammar/VPW require beat-fit group attitude; Bible constrains the facial menu. **Symptom:** neutral/dead crowd mass, comic beat loses its actor; this is a latent rule-shape collision rather than a proven new render defect.
9. **Example bar duplicates class law** — Examples and Grammar each establish the accepted depiction moves while VPW repeats them procedurally. **Symptom:** named examples become a phrasebook/template instead of the stated “match thinking” calibration.
10. **Taste criteria split across critic, Grammar, Bible, and IG review** — pre-pixel critic avoids style-policing but post-pixel IG review repeats subjective style/depth tests. **Symptom:** defect discovery moves downstream into expensive retries/parking rather than one pre-gen taste decision.
