# Daniel's canonical visual goal-state

**Use.** This is the evidence-consolidated target for doctrine diffs and each
12-frame generation board. It is not a new prompt recipe, a quota sheet, or a
claim that an untested mechanism has already recovered the look. **STRONG** =
direct Daniel feedback or a measured liked-set fact; **MEDIUM** = a repeated,
audited inference; **WEAK** = a useful but unconfirmed extrapolation. A number
below is a reference measurement unless explicitly called a pass condition.

## 1. The target

### Scale and composition — **STRONG**

- Ordinary figure-in-world shots put the figure at roughly **10-30% of frame
   height** and retain roughly **30-50% open air/space**. The world is visibly
   structured and deep (at least fore/mid/back, such as a winding river,
   corridor, or receding shelves). A deliberate close character beat is allowed;
   it is not the ordinary scale rule. [taste-ground-truth, "Decoded";
   threeway-visual, L01-25 and §2]
- The image must read as **a world with people in it**, never a person pasted in
  front of a backdrop. The subject-to-space ratio and depth must be visible in
  the output, not merely asserted in the prompt. [taste-ground-truth,
  "Decoded"; threeway-visual, L21 and §2]
- Empty-world and object-hero shots may supply real breathing space. Negative
  space is payload-led, not a universal density failure. [poyais-visual-audit,
  synthesis; contradiction-map, row 9] **MEDIUM** for the no-density rule.

### Palette — **STRONG**

- Choose and visibly deliver a **beat-local 2-3-colour palette**; warm, cool,
  mixed, neutral, and desaturated passages are all legitimate when their story
  turn calls for them. No global temperature or fixed complementary pair may
  overrule the beat. [palette-forensics, §§3-4; poyais-visual-audit, synthesis]
- The liked saturation reference is mean HSV-S **0.326** (Daniel's decoded
  preference: about **0.33**): balanced-warm, not pushed saturation. This is a
  calibration reference, not a per-frame cap. [taste-ground-truth, "Decoded";
  threeway-metrics-summary, saturation]
- Palette diversity means more than warm surfaces plus teal light: the Poyais
  reference contains warm passages, cool/desaturated low points, mixed notes,
  and neutrals; the fresh failure converged to blue/orange in 50.0% of prompts
  and orange/teal in 45.8% of its verified slice. [poyais-visual-audit,
  "Coloration"; palette-forensics, §§1-2]
- Red is semantic when used (a measured historical convention, **MEDIUM**).
  Accent pop, especially on an object-hero, is a per-beat option, never a quota
  or a demand for more red. [taste-ground-truth, "Decoded";
  palette-forensics, §§3-4]

### Characters and occupancy — **STRONG**

- The true story-bearer keeps the frame: a person whose decision, action,
  relationship, reaction, or consequence is the beat must be a seeded named
  performer; do not replace that person with an object just to make a
  non-literal shot. [character-presence-audit, §§1-3]
- A crowd is for a genuine mass-story beat. It is rearward/smaller through
   depth/overlap and stays on the simplified crowd rig; no foreground
  individuation creep (hair/facial-detail/smiling-face wall). Its body design
  remains the squat ~2.7-face-only crowd-rig, not adult-proportioned heads at
  camera. [threeway-visual, §4; poyais-visual-audit, "People";
  composition-crowd, §§2-4] **MEDIUM** for the exact ~2.7 proportion.
- Current named/costumed performers are right; do not restore anonymous
   everyman foregrounds. Closed-world poses, expressions, and interactions are
   control safeguards, not an aesthetic substitute. [taste-ground-truth,
   "Decoded"; era-map, §1] **MEDIUM** for the final distinction because the
   control has no direct taste proof.
- A personified device/company may carry a beat when it is that beat's true
  subject, but it cannot stand in for the human whose causal work, decision, or
  consequence the line needs to stage. Character tokens are not human
  occupancy. [character-presence-audit, §§1-3]
- There is deliberately **no human-presence percentage target**. Fail a run of
  figureless frames only when it hides the person doing the narrated work or
  decision; pass a cast-free map/object/aftermath when it is the true subject.
  [character-presence-audit, §§1-3] **STRONG**.

### Depiction mix — **STRONG**

- Literal re-enactment is earned by concrete physical action or object facts;
  it is not the default transcription of narration. Use the full non-literal
  vocabulary: symbolic stand-ins/objects, number-object, map/plan, reaction,
  aftermath/palette turn, personification, physicalized imbalance, interaction,
  irony, idiom, and empty-world beats. [poyais-register-audit, §2;
  poyais-visual-audit, synthesis]
- The benchmark is directional, not a quota: Poyais had 3/117 literal shots,
  28 symbolic stand-ins, and 48 cast-free-capable beats; fresh had 43/245
  literal and four symbolic. A board must show a story-led mix rather than
  literal/crowd substitution. [poyais-register-audit, §2]

### Chains — **STRONG**

- A chain exists only for a genuine progressive reveal/enumeration. Its delta
  must visibly alter the story argument; otherwise hard-cut or re-base. The
  frame change is inspectable, not inferred from a `changed_elements` string.
  [era-map, §1; poyais-register-audit, §3]
- Historical reference: Poyais had 0/22 no-op deltas; fresh had 26/109 (23.9%).
  This is evidence of the defect, not a request to maximize or minimize chain
  count. [poyais-register-audit, §3]

### Render register — **MEDIUM; still unclosed**

- The liked frames visibly carry paper/canvas grain, painterly/gradient material
  shading, atmospheric haze or diffuse beams where the beat calls for it, and
  material sheen; fresh and v2 were uniformly flat-cel. In the direct 13-versus-
  24 visual audit, this was the remaining all-frame difference after scale had
  substantially improved. [threeway-visual, §2]
- Therefore no variant may claim recovery merely because it has small figures,
  correct rig, or palette variety. It must expose this axis to pixel review.
  The evidence does **not** yet prove a single mandatory render recipe or that
  every frame needs grain/beams. [taste-ground-truth, 2026-08-19 update]

### Lettering — **STRONG**

- No unrequested text, logo, label, or invented sentence. Requested text is
   supplied, diegetic, short, and reviewed verbatim; prose naming an object is
   not permission for the engine to invent glyphs. [poyais-end-problems, P9;
   recon-adversarial-verdict, text-contract finding]
- The prose-named-prop mechanism remains **OPEN**: no doctrine diff may claim
  that a static guard prevents it. Inventory every visible glyph on frames with
  a prose-named prop and park any frame that invents lettering. [reconciliation-
  plan, §§14, 53 and 5; recon-adversarial-verdict, M3]
- When text is requested, review its lettering register against the declared
  crude-marker exemplar as well as its literal spelling; a polished/digital
  treatment is not a pass merely because the characters are correct. **MEDIUM**
  for the style-family check. [rig-script-lettering, L04; residual-forensics-
  codex, §L04 lettering family]

### Scope boundary — **STRONG**

- Resolution is not a taste factor. A 1K/2K choice neither passes nor fails a
  doctrine variant or frame board under this yardstick; evaluate it only in a
  separate operational/cost decision. [taste-ground-truth, "Decoded";
  era-map, §1]

## 2. Keep / don't-keep registry

**KEEP (6).** These are protections or positive vocabulary, not extra taste
formulae.

1. Seeded-cast promotion for a story-bearing individual; crowds only for actual
   mass beats. [character-presence-audit, §3]
2. Closed-world pose/expression/interaction and the named rig discipline.
   [era-map, §1]
3. Honest review machinery: Pass-1/seed provenance, verified/parked/unreviewed,
   and only verified frames shipping. [poyais-end-problems, P7-P8]
4. Reveal-only chains with a material visible delta. [era-map, §1]
5. The non-literal vocabulary and cast-free shots where the true subject is not
   a person. [poyais-register-audit, §2]
6. Disjoint parallel waves and independent review; retain as process machinery,
   without claiming they cause taste recovery. [era-map, §1;
   recon-adversarial-verdict, L1]

**DON'T KEEP (7).** These are ruled out because they conflict with the target,
are reversed/churned, or lack a demonstrated benefit.

1. Anonymous/unseeded foreground tier or casting a generic everyman instead of
   using a real named story-bearer/crowd decision. [taste-ground-truth,
   "Decoded"; contradiction-map, row 1]
2. Quotas, caps, cadence numerology, and hyper-specific visual parameters as a
   substitute for a story-led decision. [poyais-end-problems, P1-P2;
   drift-ledger, counts]
3. Invented sentences or any unrequested on-image lettering. [poyais-end-
   problems, P9]
4. A single recurring palette combination, especially blue/orange convergence.
   [palette-forensics, §§1-2]
5. A warm-biased global tail or warm-only neutral rule. [palette-forensics,
   §§3-4; contradiction-map, row 2]
6. Mandatory staging formulas (edge-to-edge fill, fixed eye-level/frontal
   plates, forced foreground props, or a chain by default). [contradiction-map,
   rows 3, 5, 9; drift-ledger, churn chains]
7. Function/rule layering: duplicate normative prose across skill, grammar,
   bible, schema, and forge. [contradiction-map, §§1-2 and condensation map]

## 3. File-editing law

When a doctrine variant changes this target, the edit must:

1. change the live logic **in place**, including every exact mirror/equality
   fixture affected by it; do not add a compensating side document;
2. be a net reduction where it removes a contradicted or superseded rule—state
   the before/after line ledger rather than calling a larger patch a cleanup;
3. preserve cross-file consistency between authored rule, dispatch order,
   schema/validation, and review; a rule the engine receives differently is not
   consistent;
4. condense: delete superseded prose rather than append a dated exception; and
5. give each normative rule one home. Registry and forge may retain factual
   data/executable enforcement, but not competing doctrine prose.

Evidence: the stack reached six conflict/shadow topics and six overlaps; the
reconciliation record explicitly defines a single rule home and rejects copying
the same doctrine into two homes. [contradiction-map, §§"Then vs now" and
"Condensation map"; recon-adversarial-verdict, line-budget finding]

## 4. Per-variant adversarial review rubric

Review the **doctrine diff first**, then the same 12 selected frames. Each item
is PASS, FAIL, or N/A (only when the board contains no applicable beat). A
variant passes only when every applicable item passes; a parked/unreviewed frame
is a FAIL for the board, not an omission. Record the frame IDs and the concrete
failed condition—never an unlocated "vibe."

1. **Scale/composition.** Diff: contains no compulsory fill/frontal/foreground
   formula and preserves payload-led air. Frames: ordinary figure-world shots
   record the tallest ordinary figure height and open-space share as percentages
   of the full canvas, then apply the 10-30% and 30-50% reference bands. For
   repeatability, trace and total the visible air/space regions that contain no
   figure, crowd, or payload object; close beats are labelled close.
   Figure-world and environmental world shots have readable fore/mid/back
   structure. A flat map/graphic or isolated object hero is N/A for that
   structure test only when its stated payload uses the air. [STRONG]
2. **Palette.** Diff: no warm-biased/global-temperature tail and no preferred
   pair. Frames: each planned palette is visibly delivered; any planned
   cool/desaturated/neutral turn is not warm-dominant; the 12-frame hue-mode
   table records the story rationale for a repeated pair across distinct beats.
   Fail a board-wide/default recurring pair, not a justified recurrence or a
   numerical count. [STRONG]
3. **Characters/crowds.** Diff: a true story-bearer cannot be object-replaced;
   cast and crowd routes remain valid. Frames: each causal human story-bearer
   has its named performer; a personified device cannot satisfy that human
   occupancy; each crowd is a mass subject, rearward/smaller and simplified at
   the ~2.7-face-only crowd proportion; no foreground crowd face is
   individuated. [STRONG]
4. **Depiction mix.** Diff: literal is reserved and the non-literal class menu
   remains available. Frames: label each frame's class and true subject; fail
   a literal frame unless its review record names the concrete physical action
   or object fact that earns literal depiction, or any object-only frame that
   suppresses its story-bearer. [STRONG]
5. **Chains.** Diff: chains are reveal-only and require a material delta.
   Frames: overlay each child against its parent; pass only if the changed state
   is immediately legible and advances the narrated argument. [STRONG]
6. **Render register.** Diff: does not force flat-cel or assert an unproved
   recipe. Frames: identify grain/texture, gradient/material modelling, and
   atmospheric light/haze where the chosen beat asks for them; fail an
   all-uniform-flat board. Report this axis separately as OPEN until Daniel
   accepts it. [MEDIUM]
7. **Lettering.** Diff: preserves supplied/verbatim text enforcement. Frames:
   inventory every visible glyph; pass only supplied, exact requested lettering
   and no logos/extra labels/sentences; prose-named-prop frames remain OPEN and
   are parked if they invent glyphs. Compare requested lettering to its declared
   exemplar as a separate MEDIUM register finding. [STRONG]
8. **Editing law and review integrity.** Diff: one rule home, deleted
   superseded prose, cross-file dispatch consistency, and an honest line ledger.
   Board: all 12 have review states and seed/lineage evidence; no mechanical
   green result is presented as a taste PASS. [STRONG for integrity; MEDIUM for
   condensation's taste effect]

### Decision rule

Do not average axes. A single FAIL on scale, palette, story-bearer, material
delta, lettering, or review integrity rejects the variant. Render-register is
an explicit unresolved finding: it cannot grant promotion by itself, but it can
block any claim of visual recovery. Daniel's final comparative eye gate remains
the only authority that can close that axis.
