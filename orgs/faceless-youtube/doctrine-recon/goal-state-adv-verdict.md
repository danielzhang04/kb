## Findings and exact edits applied

### HIGH — rubric would reject valid, evidenced literal beats

**Original location:** per-variant rubric, depiction mix.

“Fail any literal transcription that has an available story-led non-literal
move” was an invented test. A non-literal alternative is nearly always
imaginable, but the source expressly earns literal depiction for a concrete
physical action or object fact. It would therefore reject an otherwise valid
literal beat and turn the directional comparison into a literal quota by
another name.

**Applied edit:** a literal frame now passes when its review record names the
concrete action or object fact that earns literal depiction; the true-subject
and object-replacement checks remain. This preserves the source rule without
requiring a reviewer to prove that no imaginable alternative exists.

### HIGH — personified PC/company figures could falsely satisfy human occupancy

**Original location:** characters/crowds rubric.

The prior “each narrated person/action/decision has its named performer” test
was both broader than the true-story-bearer source and silent on the documented
failure where L01–L25 had personified character tokens but no human bodies.
That permits a personified PC/company to mask a missing human whose decision,
labour, reaction, or consequence makes the beat true.

**Applied edit:** the target and rubric now distinguish a permitted
personified-subject beat from human occupancy: personified devices cannot
satisfy a causal human story-bearer. No personification quota or blanket ban
was added.

### MEDIUM — crowd-rig proportion was omitted

**Original location:** characters and occupancy; characters/crowds rubric.

The document preserved simplified/rearward crowds but omitted the audited
squat ~2.7-face-only crowd-rig proportion, leaving “simplified” too loose to
catch the observed adult-proportioned wall-of-heads failure.

**Applied edit:** added the ~2.7 proportion and the adult-proportioned
at-camera failure condition, explicitly marked MEDIUM because the exact ratio
is an audited standard rather than direct owner wording.

### MEDIUM — prop-text mechanism and lettering-family failure were under-specified

**Original location:** lettering target and rubric.

The no-unrequested-text rule was correct but did not retain the OPEN status of
prose-named-prop text generation, so a variant could claim a static guard had
closed an unproven mechanism. It also tested spelling but not the documented
polished/digital lettering-family false-pass.

**Applied edit:** prose-named-prop frames now require glyph inventory and
parking on invention, without claiming prevention; requested lettering is
separately compared against its declared exemplar as a MEDIUM register check.

### MEDIUM — standing boundaries and real gains were absent

**Original location:** palette/history.

The target did not state that resolution is irrelevant to taste, did not state
accent-pop as an optional per-beat choice, and said later improvements were not
a closed verdict without making their non-regression status explicit. Those
gaps risked reintroducing resolution as a false axis, treating accent as a red
quota, or discarding actual fresh-board gains.

**Applied edit:** added a resolution scope boundary; clarified semantic red
versus optional accent-pop; and retained structured scale/depth, spacious
non-character, and simplified crowd/rig gains as floors, not proof of recovery.

### MEDIUM — scale rubric was not fully applicable to its own allowed classes

**Original location:** scale/composition rubric.

“Every world shot has readable fore/mid/back” contradicted the document’s own
permitted flat maps, graphics, and isolated object heroes. The percentage test
also gave no repeatable recording instruction.

**Applied edit:** reviewers now record figure height and traced open-space
share against the full canvas; flat maps/graphics/isolated object heroes are
N/A only for the layer test when their stated payload uses the air.

### MEDIUM — repeated-pair test smuggled in an undefined palette quota

**Original location:** palette rubric.

“No unexplained repeated pair” supplied neither a recurrence threshold nor a
definition of explanation, while the owner’s standing ruling expressly rejects
quota numerology. The source supports rejecting a board-wide default pair, not
penalising a justified recurrence.

**Applied edit:** each recurrence across distinct beats must carry its
story rationale; failure is a board-wide/default pair, never a numerical count
or an explained reuse.
