# DRAFT — Asset-first automated income research dossier

_Research date: 2026-07-21. Supersedes the earlier B2B intelligence portfolio as the recommended direction. Estimates are planning hypotheses, not forecasts._

## The corrected objective

Build cash-flow assets, not a company that depends on repeatedly selling and servicing a product or client.

Allowed monetization:

- display and in-app advertising;
- affiliate and referral commissions;
- sponsorship marketplaces;
- royalties and licensing;
- platform revenue sharing;
- automated lead routing;
- eventual acquisition or resale of a cash-flowing asset.

Operating constraints:

- no sales calls, custom fulfillment, account onboarding, or recurring client support;
- no SaaS sales motion or paid product unless the opportunity becomes exceptional;
- the asset should earn while unattended and remain transferable;
- after stabilization, recurring support should stay below roughly two hours a week;
- agents and deterministic tools should handle collection, production, publishing, QA, analytics, and iteration.

## Executive recommendation

Launch two projects and hold a third behind a validation gate:

1. **A free high-intent decision utility and changing-data property** — the primary bet.
2. **A multi-marketplace commercial media licensing catalog** — the low-support diversifier.
3. **One ad-supported utility app** — build only after one of the first two passes its 90-day gate.

The underlying strategy is not “publish a lot with AI.” It is to own useful things that AI summaries cannot replace easily: calculations based on user inputs, current prices and availability, historical changes, alerts, specialized reference data, or commercially useful media assets.

## Why this direction is stronger

- Google answer pages are becoming less dependable as a traffic source. Pew observed traditional-result clicks on 8% of searches with an AI summary versus 15% without one; only 1% clicked a source inside the summary. [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)
- Fresh prices, inventories, status changes, personalized calculations, alerts, and interactive tools still require a destination. They are less substitutable than generic articles.
- Google explicitly treats mass-generated pages with little added value as scaled-content abuse. The advantage must come from the data model or utility, not publishing volume. [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
- Platform and marketplace earnings are highly power-law distributed. Roblox reports a $1,550 median annual payout among its 35,000-plus DevEx creators in 2025 even while total creator earnings approached $1B. This is why marketplace reach is not enough without a sharp buyer need. [Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/monetize)
- Asset catalogs can be multi-homed: Adobe pays 33% on non-video assets and 35% on video, while Pond5 pays 30% for non-exclusive media and 40% for exclusive video. [Adobe royalties](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/royalty-details.html), [Pond5 payouts](https://contributor.pond5.com/getting-started/payout-overview/)

## Project 1 — Free decision utility and changing-data property

### What it is

One coherent web property containing three to five tools around a single expensive or recurring decision. It is free to the user. Revenue is attached to completed decisions through referrals, affiliates, ads, sponsorship marketplaces, and eventually automated lead routing.

The asset should combine at least three of these:

- user-specific calculations;
- current prices, availability, or eligibility;
- normalized historical data;
- saved scenarios or email alerts;
- verified directory/reference records;
- change detection;
- a transactional next step with multiple monetization partners.

### Strong niche shapes to test

Do not select a niche from intuition alone. Run a one-week data and monetization tournament across:

1. **RV/mobile connectivity decision engine** — route and usage-based plan comparison, equipment compatibility, current plan pricing, coverage tools, affiliate hardware and connectivity referrals.
2. **Refurbished equipment total-cost tracker** — price and warranty history, model normalization, expected maintenance, availability, financing/referral partners.
3. **Shipping/3PL cost engine** — dimensional-weight, fee, zone, packaging, and break-even calculators with software/referral partners.
4. **Trade-equipment rent-versus-buy engine** — inventory, financing, utilization, depreciation, maintenance, and auction comparisons.
5. **Home-energy decision engine** — appliance/upgrade operating cost, incentives and rebates by location, tariff changes, replacement timing, retailer/installer referrals. Avoid personalized financial or engineering advice.
6. **Cloud storage/GPU/backup total-cost engine** — real usage scenarios, egress and retention costs, price-change alerts, affiliate or referral programs.

### Monetization order

1. High-intent referral or affiliate links from completed results.
2. Email alerts and saved scenarios to create direct return traffic.
3. Display ads after repeat usage and page depth appear.
4. Secondary partner programs so no merchant controls the economics.
5. Acquisition as terminal upside, never as the base-case justification.

Amazon should not be the primary rail; common categories often pay only 3–4.5%. [Amazon commission schedule](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ)

### What makes it defensible

- canonical entities with stable identifiers;
- source timestamps and provenance;
- normalized data and stored history;
- calculations the raw sources do not provide;
- user inputs and repeat alerts;
- a correction and exception workflow;
- direct and email traffic, not only Google.

The precedent is much larger than a solo target, but shows the value of free utility plus data: Ziff Davis's Connectivity segment, including Speedtest and Downdetector, generated $230.7M in 2025 and was sold to Accenture for $1.2B in 2026. [Ziff Davis filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1084048/000108404826000021/annualreport2025.htm), [transaction filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1084048/000119312526089778/d80883d8k.htm)

### 90-day gate

- Maximum initial build: 40 focused hours.
- Week 1: confirm lawful, stable data and at least two usable monetization partners.
- Week 6: three genuinely useful tools live, instrumented, and tested.
- Day 90: continue only if at least one is true:
  - 1,000 qualified monthly users;
  - 100 monthly repeat users;
  - 100 qualified email subscribers and 25 saved alerts;
  - five verified monetizable actions or conversions.
- Kill if exception handling exceeds one hour weekly per 100 active entities.
- Kill if data freshness falls below 98%, entity matching below 97%, or corrections take over 48 hours.
- At day 180, freeze or sell unless it reaches $100/month gross or maintains at least 25% monthly traffic growth for three consecutive months.

## Project 2 — Multi-marketplace commercial media licensing catalog

### What it is

Create commercially specific, reusable assets and distribute them non-exclusively across Adobe Stock and Pond5 first. Add a third marketplace only after metadata and upload syndication are automated.

The strongest formats are:

- hard-to-source real-world regional or procedural B-roll;
- clean ambient and mechanical sound effects;
- motion backgrounds, overlays, transitions, explainers, and data-visualization components;
- shot families that serve distinct commercial uses rather than cosmetic variations;
- media that can be produced alongside other real work but has standalone licensing value.

### What not to make

- generic AI people at laptops;
- prompt variations of the same composition;
- trademarked, celebrity, or imitation-style material;
- unverified “editorial” footage;
- commodity decorative backgrounds with no buyer-defined purpose.

Adobe accepts labeled generative content but emphasizes distinct concepts, correct rights, and selective curation; it may reject near-duplicate variations. [Adobe guidance](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/distinct-generative-ai-submission-best-practices.html)

### Why it belongs in the portfolio

- nearly no customer interaction;
- marketplace-native discovery and payment;
- assets can be multi-homed;
- revenue failure is different from web traffic and affiliate failure;
- source files, metadata, and upload automation form a transferable catalog;
- production tooling can be reused without depending on YouTube's audience or ad account.

### 90/180-day gate

- Maximum initial production: 40 focused hours.
- Day 90: 100 approved assets across two marketplaces with at least 70% approval.
- Kill a concept cluster after 25 assets and 120 days with zero licenses.
- Six months after reaching 100 assets, stop new production unless the catalog has 15 paid licenses or $50 cumulative revenue plus an improving three-month trend.
- Metadata, tagging, and upload work must fall below 20% of production time.
- Never produce cosmetic duplicates merely to reach the asset-count target.

## Project 3 — One free ad-supported utility app

### What it is

One coherent, privacy-light, account-free app bundling repeat-use utilities in a niche. Revenue comes from ads and transparent referrals, not subscriptions or feature sales.

Good app characteristics:

- works offline or with a small permissioned data feed;
- no persistent accounts;
- no UGC or moderation;
- not aimed at children;
- no sensitive financial, health, location-history, or identity data;
- repeated real-world use rather than novelty;
- cross-platform codebase where practical.

Examples:

- trade measurement, cut/yield, load, or materials calculator bundle;
- RV trip connectivity and power-planning utilities;
- equipment maintenance interval and total-cost tools;
- shipping/package dimensional optimization tools;
- sound-level, lighting, shot, and production utilities for media creators.

Do not make twenty cloned calculator apps. Google Play rejects repetitive or low-quality apps and recommends aggregation into one stronger app. [Google Play spam policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9899034)

### Launch gate

Do not begin until Project 1 or 2 passes its day-90 signal gate.

- Maximum build: 80 focused hours.
- Day 45: polished MVP submitted.
- Day 90 post-release: require 500 non-incentivized installs, 100 MAU, 10% D30 retention, and 99.5% crash-free users.
- Miss three of those four: kill.
- Day 180: require $50/month ad revenue or 20% or better three-month MAU growth.
- Any recurring privacy, SDK, review, or support burden above two hours/week triggers redesign or shutdown.

## The newsletter's proper role

A newsletter is a distribution layer for Project 1, not one of the three primary projects.

Use it for:

- meaningful price or status changes;
- new data and comparisons;
- personalized alerts;
- summaries that send users into tools;
- sponsor or ad-network inventory after an audience exists.

Do not build a generic prose newsletter and hope automated ads make it a business. beehiiv reported 4,728 newsletters using its Ad Network in 2024 and $3.73M in total network earnings—roughly $788 each if divided evenly. Large outcomes exist, but automatic network ads are usually supplemental. [beehiiv report](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/2025-state-of-email-newsletters-by-beehiiv)

## Acquisition option

Buying a small proven asset is the fastest route to existing revenue, but it is not a substitute for understanding the model.

Buy only something resembling Projects 1–3 with:

- 12–18 months of analytics and payment evidence;
- transferable data and content rights;
- at least two monetization rails;
- at least 40% direct/email traffic if paying over 24 times monthly owner profit;
- documented support below five hours weekly;
- no merchant, ad platform, or query producing over 35% of revenue;
- no dependence on a disappearing SEO article archive.

Empire Flippers reports that standalone display-ad sites represented only 13.3% of its 2025 marketplace deals and describes content sites as riskier amid Google volatility. Treat acquisition as a later accelerator, not a launch project. [Empire Flippers 2026 report](https://info.empireflippers.com/hubfs/2026%20Lead%20Magnets/2026%20State%20of%20the%20Industry%20Report.pdf)

## Portfolio operating rules

- Launch Project 1 and Project 2 now; Project 3 stays gated.
- Do not count resale value in the base-case ROI.
- Never use paid traffic before organic, direct, or marketplace economics are demonstrated.
- By month 12, no intermediary should produce over 50% of the new portfolio's revenue.
- Preserve domains, source files, code, schemas, metadata, analytics, and SOPs so assets remain transferable.
- Shut down any project that requires over two support hours weekly for four consecutive weeks after stabilization.
- A project survives day 180 only with cash flow or an objectively improving leading indicator.

## Explicit rejects

- generic “best X” affiliate articles;
- AI-generated informational page farms;
- dozens of near-identical utility apps;
- passive affiliate-cookie replacement extensions;
- generic AI stock-image batches;
- automated KDP/POD/template volume;
- synthetic influencer portfolios;
- scraped coupon or product feeds without permission and transformation;
- any property whose correction burden grows linearly with its record count;
- any opportunity whose operator mainly makes money teaching the opportunity.

## Final selection

The recommended next two projects are:

1. **A free high-intent decision utility around a changing dataset.** Run the six-niche tournament before selecting the vertical.
2. **A commercially specific footage/motion/SFX licensing catalog.** Use two marketplaces and hard asset-level kill rules.

The third project is **one free ad-supported utility app**, activated only after a first project demonstrates real demand.

This portfolio earns through referrals, ads, royalties, licensing, and potential resale without turning the operator into a salesperson, product-support desk, or service provider.

### Category notes

#### 1. Decision and changing-data assets

An owned property helps a user decide or alerts them when something changes. Strong forms include price and availability trackers, total-cost engines, verified specialist directories, compatibility tools, and rebate or opportunity databases. Revenue can combine referrals, lead routing, sponsorship placement, advertising, data licensing, and eventual asset resale.

The moat is accumulated history, normalization, saved preferences, direct traffic, and alerts—not prose volume.

#### 2. Automated content and attention assets

Repeatable media formats earn from platform distribution, subscriptions, advertising pools, referrals, or sponsorship infrastructure. Faceless YouTube remains approximately **78/100** as a standalone model. Facebook native publishing, TikTok serial content, Spotify video podcasts, and data-led newsletters are adjacent forms.

AI can automate research collection, drafts, storyboards, narration, assembly, localization, publishing, and analytics. It does not remove the need for original editorial framing, factual review, packaging judgment, or rights control.

#### 3. Reusable creative-IP licensing

Assets earn repeatedly when other people license or use them. This includes commercial footage, motion elements, sound effects, functional audio, 3D/CAD files, professional templates, fonts, shaders, and verified voice clones.

The strongest assets save a buyer measurable production or integration time. Generic AI output is not a durable catalog thesis.

#### 4. Ecosystem extensions and UGC primitives

These assets live inside platforms that already provide users, discovery, installation, measurement, and payment. The strongest newly found examples are Minecraft mods and plugins, TikTok Effect House effects, CurseForge/Modrinth add-ons, Poe bots, Fortnite islands, Roblox experiences, and creator-development tools.

CurseForge reports 20 million monthly users, 1.2 billion monthly downloads, $20.4 million paid to authors, and a 70% creator allocation. [CurseForge authors](https://authors.curseforge.com/welcome/)

TikTok Effect House pays for qualified public-video uses of effects. Program admission requires five published effects and three used in 1,000 qualified videos each; admitted AI-generated, game, and randomizer effects begin earning at 2,000 unique public-video uses within 30 days. [Effect Creator Rewards](https://effecthouse.tiktok.com/learn/guides/support/faq-effect-creator-rewards)

Poe allows eligible creators to set a price per message and pays when a user messages a bot and it responds. [Poe monetization](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/21921312368020-Poe-Creator-Monetization-FAQs)

#### 5. Free utility software properties

One substantial free application, extension, plugin, or local-first tool solves a recurring task. Monetization can come from in-app advertising, referrals, marketplace revenue, optional one-time upgrades, or acquisition. The recurring error is building cloned utilities without a distribution thesis.

#### 6. Digital cash-flow asset acquisition

Acquisition is an entry strategy rather than an output type, but it changes the economics enough to rank separately. Suitable targets resemble categories 1–5 and have 12–18 months of verifiable payments and analytics, transferable rights, multiple monetization rails, and low support.

Do not pay for revenue derived from a decaying search archive, nontransferable account privileges, or an undocumented operator relationship.

#### 7. Machine-consumed assets

Software and AI systems—not human audiences—consume APIs, normalized data feeds, datasets, model packages, retrieval corpora, deployment repositories, and licensed source content.

RapidAPI handles authentication, metering, and billing for providers while retaining a platform share. [RapidAPI API monetization](https://docs.rapidapi.com/do/docs/monetizing-your-api-on-rapidapicom)

AWS Data Exchange provides marketplace discovery, subscriptions, billing, and payment distribution, but qualification requires a defined support and update process. [AWS provider onboarding](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/data-exchange/latest/userguide/provider-getting-started.html)

Really Simple Licensing now defines machine-readable pay-per-crawl, pay-per-inference, training, subscription, and attribution terms for web content and other digital assets. This is an emerging rail, not dependable present revenue. [RSL overview](https://rslstandard.org/guide/what-is-rsl)

#### 8. Marketplace-native content catalogs

Finished content products are consumed through a platform transaction or subscription system. Examples include Kindle Unlimited titles, Amazon onsite videos, 3D-printable catalogs, educational resources, audiobooks, and craft-machine files.

This category crosses the operator's preference against product selling more directly than licensing catalogs do. Only specialized, functional, and low-support catalogs merit testing.

#### 9. Outcome and bounty markets

The operator is paid for a validated result: a vulnerability, merged contribution, or competition result. AI can assist reconnaissance, code analysis, experiments, reproduction, and report preparation.

Bug bounties are the strongest subcategory at approximately **71/100**. HackerOne explicitly recognizes AI-specific security findings, but requires human investigation and validation; fully autonomous hacking is not permitted. [HackerOne AI bug bounties](https://docs.hackerone.com/en/articles/12570435-ai-bug-bounty) [HackerOne AI-tool rules](https://docs.hackerone.com/en/articles/10908081-hai-security-trust)

Open-source bounties and ML competitions are useful experiments or paid skill building, not dependable cash-flow assets.

#### 10. Automated physical infrastructure

Hardware supplies GPU compute, storage, bandwidth, mapping imagery, weather data, or wireless coverage. Operation can be automated, but economics depend on utilization, electricity, cooling, hardware depreciation, isolation, and sometimes token value.

Vast.ai is a real GPU marketplace where hosts set GPU-hour, storage, and bandwidth prices. It is only attractive as an initial test on already-owned idle hardware. [Vast hosting overview](https://docs.vast.ai/host/hosting-overview)

Storage, residential bandwidth, and DePIN hardware generally fail when equipment is purchased solely for the projected rewards.

## Newly identified low-cost asymmetric experiment

ElevenLabs pays verified speakers when paid subscribers generate audio with their Professional Voice Clone. ElevenLabs reported more than 10,400 earning creators and over $22 million in cumulative creator earnings by July 2026. Only the speaker's verified voice qualifies; this is not a synthetic-character farm. [ElevenLabs voice marketplace](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/22-million-earned-by-voice-creators-on-elevenlabs)

This is nearly passive after a professional recording and metadata setup, but it requires an explicit decision about licensing one's vocal likeness for novel generated speech.

## Influencer-idea filter

Influencer and operator reports were used for idea discovery, not as forecast evidence. Earnings screenshots commonly omit failed accounts, prior audiences, invitation status, disqualified views, editing time, tool costs, withheld payouts, and revenue from selling the method.

Reject or heavily discount:

- copied-clip Facebook and TikTok farms;
- generic AI influencer portfolios;
- fully AI-generated Medium publications;
- AI Canva-template factories;
- passive Snapchat Spotlight claims based on the discontinued rewards program;
- raw text-to-3D upload farms;
- generic prompt-wrapper bots;
- residential bandwidth as meaningful income;
- purchased GPU rigs modeled at optimistic utilization;
- token-denominated DePIN projections that require appreciation;
- prediction-market trading presented as automated income;
- any opportunity whose operator primarily earns by teaching it.
