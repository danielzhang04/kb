# Figment trial-04 casting brief — candid-register probe

## Scope and non-negotiables

This is a fresh six-person casting pool for the Z-Image Base and FLUX.2 klein 4B Base arms. No prior face is an identity reference. Every subject is fictional, visually distinct, fully clothed, and unambiguously an adult woman. The intended apparent age is about twenty-one, reinforced by adult facial structure, adult proportions, and age-consistent hands and neck. Any frame that reads below the adult floor is discarded rather than relabelled. Do not use any real-person name, reference, or likeness.

The probe covers S1 only: six candidates by two fixed seeds, for twelve jobs in each arm. S2 and S3 are retained here for the later full recast. Prompts use plain prose for the shared Qwen3 text encoder. The candidate descriptions and S1 core remain the same across both arms; only the documented Z-Image phone-sensor clause differs.

## Candidate look-specs

All six are fictional Asian-American women. Five have black-to-dark-brown hair; c06 is the acknowledged lighter-haired outlier. Each description uses a different face shape, eye area, skin tone, hair length and texture, and identifying mark.

### c01 — slim

A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, with an adult woman's face and a slim adult woman's frame. Her jawline is set, and her hands and neck read the same age as her face. Her face is round with full, softly rounded cheeks; low-crease almond eyes turn slightly downward at the outer corners. Her skin is cool-light, with pores across the nose and a faint freckle beneath her left eye. Jet-black hair reaches mid-back, centre-parted, mostly straight, air-dried, and separated at the ends. She wears almost no makeup: one coat of mascara, a thin brown line close to the upper lash, her own straight brows brushed up and not drawn in, and balm over her natural lip colour.

### c02 — slim

A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, with an adult woman's face and a long-limbed slim adult woman's frame. Her jawline is set, and her hands and neck read the same age as her face. Her face is a soft heart shape with a broader brow and rounded chin; wide-set hooded almond eyes sit beneath softly curved brows. Her skin is warm golden-olive, with visible pores and a fine pale scar crossing her right brow. Dark-brown hair ends at the jaw in a tousled, air-dried bob with bent ends. She wears almost no makeup: one coat of mascara, nothing else on the eye, her own brows only tidied, and balm over her natural lip colour.

### c03 — lean-curvy

A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, with an adult woman's face and a lean-curvy adult woman's frame. Her jawline is set beneath a soft square face with rounded corners, and her hands and neck read the same age as her face. Her monolid eyes have a slight rise at the outer corners. Her skin is medium tan with an even neutral warmth, visible pores, and a single mole near the left jaw hinge. Dark-brown hair falls below her shoulders in loose, rope-like curls, air-dried with uneven ends. She wears almost no makeup: a thin brown line close to the upper lash, one coat of mascara, her own softly angled brows brushed up, and muted rose balm over her natural lip colour.

### c04 — lean-curvy

A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, with an adult woman's face and a lean-curvy adult woman's frame. Her jawline is set beneath a long soft-oval face with moderate cheek fullness, and her hands and neck read the same age as her face. Her narrow almond eyes are deep-set beneath low, straight brows. Her skin is deep brown with a neutral undertone, visible texture across the cheeks, and a faint crescent-shaped patch at the right temple. Black hair ends at the collarbone in dense, air-dried waves with an uneven fringe. She wears almost no makeup: one coat of mascara, her own brows left soft-edged, and clear balm over her natural lip colour.

### c05 — slim-thick

A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, with an adult woman's face and a slim-thick adult woman's frame. Her jawline is set beneath a wide oval face with full cheeks, and her hands and neck read the same age as her face. Her open almond eyes have a visible crease and a slight asymmetry between the lids. Her skin is warm medium-amber, with visible pores and a faint scatter of freckles across the bridge of her nose. Espresso-brown hair reaches her shoulder blades in loose air-dried waves, with a subtle ash-brown gradient confined to the lower lengths. She wears almost no makeup: a thin brown line close to the upper lash, one coat of mascara, her own broad brows brushed up, and soft berry balm over her natural lip colour.

### c06 — slim-thick, lighter-haired outlier

A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, with an adult woman's face and a slim-thick adult woman's frame. Her jawline is set beneath a round-heart face with full lower cheeks, and her hands and neck read the same age as her face. Her low-set hooded almond eyes sit beneath softly arched brows. Her skin is fair peach with visible pores, light unevenness at the forehead, and a single mole above the left side of her mouth. Wheat-blonde hair over natural dark roots ends at her shoulders in loose, air-dried bends. She wears almost no makeup: one coat of mascara, her own brows only tidied, and pale pink balm over her natural lip colour.

### S1 — bed-edge window probe

A candid phone photograph of {look} She is seated on the edge of a bed beside a window in soft daylight, wearing a fully opaque black cotton camisole and one thin silver chain. A friend holds the phone at sitting distance while she slouches, her air-dried hair falling as it settles, her mouth relaxed, looking slightly past the lens as if caught mid-thought; her hands rest naturally in her lap. ### S2 — lamp-lit night close view

A close candid phone photograph of {look} She sits on the floor beside her bed at night in a fully opaque oversized charcoal cotton tee and one thin silver chain. A single lamp across the room is the only light; a friend holds the phone nearby while she leans against the bed, mouth relaxed, eyes caught mid-thought just past the lens, and air-dried hair loose around her face. Her skin remains visibly textured and the frame feels like an unplanned moment in her apartment. Asian-American woman.

### S3 — full-body standing view

A full-body candid phone photograph of {look} She stands against a plain apartment wall in a fully opaque black cotton camisole, relaxed dark jeans, one thin silver chain, and clean low-profile sneakers, with both shoes fully visible. Her weight rests on one hip without a display pose; her shoulders are easy, mouth relaxed, and gaze slightly past the lens while a friend holds the phone. Window light is the only source, her air-dried hair sits loosely, and the frame feels unplanned. Asian-American woman.

## Model-specific levers

The current research file numbers the relevant sections as §8 for the Z-Image surface-render problem and §9 for adult age read.

For Z-Image Base, the workflow uses `cfg: 4`, `steps: 22`, `sampler_name: res_multistep`, and `scheduler: beta`. CFG 4 remains within the official Base range; the twenty-two-step `res_multistep` plus beta schedule is the cited community recipe worth probing at fixed seeds. Each Z prompt adds: “The ordinary phone image has mild lens softness, sensor grain, light JPEG artifacts, ordinary background focus, no computational sharpening, and no HDR halos.” These words are the lowest-cost evidence-backed camera-language probe. Resolution remains 1024 by 1280. No second sampling pass, adapter, post-process, custom node, or new asset is introduced because those would change the copied graph or require a separate controlled test.

For klein, there is no evidence-backed klein-only age sampler or adapter to add. The compatible age levers are in the shared prompt core: the early-twenties adult life stage, set adult jaw and proportions, age-consistent hands and neck, casual adult apartment context, restrained self-applied makeup, and visible skin texture. The same core is retained in Z-Image for safety and comparability.

## QA checklist for the grader

- Adult-read: cull any face or body that could plausibly read underage, regardless of stated age.
- Clothing: cull any missing, sheer, broken, or incomplete garment; the body must be fully clothed in the stated Instagram register.
- Hands and anatomy: cull extra, fused, missing, malformed, or implausibly posed hands; cull material body distortion.
- Real-likeness: cull any output that appears recognizably similar to a real person or carries a specific-person tell.
- Register: cull synthetic-smooth skin, loss of pores, overly drawn brows, sharply carved cheeks, controlled commercial-set backgrounds, direct burst lighting, or magazine-finish drift.
- Cell compliance: cull any output that loses the required shot framing, light, wardrobe, or candidate traits. For the later full recast, cull S3 outputs without both shoes visible or without readable hip-weighted balance.
- Candidness — SOFT flag, record only and do not cull: “cull any frame that reads as performed/composed-for-camera rather than candid.” Record the flag verbatim in grading, but route the frame to operator review instead of quarantine.

Do not repair a hard cull with captioning or prompt interpretation. Quarantine the output and regenerate only that same candidate-shot-seed job under the approved workflow. A soft candidness flag is recorded separately and is not a hard cull.
