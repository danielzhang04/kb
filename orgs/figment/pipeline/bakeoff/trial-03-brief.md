# Figment trial-03 casting brief — modern-model bake-off

## Scope and non-negotiables

This is a fresh six-person casting pool for the Z-Image Base and FLUX.2 klein 4B Base arms. No prior SDXL face is an identity reference. Every subject is a fictional, visually distinct, unambiguously adult woman in her mid-twenties; all images remain fully clothed in the candid Instagram bedroom register. Do not use any real-person name, reference, or likeness.

The target register is moody bedroom: low ambient or window light, phone-arm's-length to half-body framing, a slip dress or cami, delicate gold jewelry, glossy nude-to-berry lip, soft glam, warm textured skin with visible pores, and a film-grain phone-camera snapshot. It is never studio, flash, fashion editorial, K-beauty glass skin, thin flat brow, or sharp editorial cheekbones. Curve must read through fitted clothing and pose as well as build.

## Prompt-style note for the two arms

Both arms receive natural-language prose through a Qwen3-4B text encoder. Do not use SDXL weighting syntax such as `(term:1.3)`, tag soup, or comma-led keyword strings. Use two to four plain sentences: first the subject, then the clothing, setting, and light, then the camera and finish. State the desired adult, fully clothed, textured-skin result positively. Put the heritage phrase only at the very end, never as the opening demographic noun; this preserves the heritage-noun cascade mitigation.

Keep any negative prompt short: `child or teen, nudity, lingerie, studio, flash, glass skin`. FLUX.2 klein and Z-Image Turbo distilled variants ignore negatives at CFG 1, so the positive prose is the operative control. The Base arms should use the same positive prompt and may use that short negative only if their selected workflow accepts one.

## Shared shot specs

Use the exact shot wording below for every candidate and seed. Replace `{look}` only with that candidate's complete look description from the manifest; retain the last heritage phrase exactly.

### S1 — half-body window portrait

A candid half-body phone-camera portrait of an unambiguously adult woman in her mid-twenties with {look}, seated on the edge of a neatly made bed beside a window in a fully opaque fitted black slip dress with a modest neckline and delicate gold jewelry. Soft window daylight falls across a real bedroom, with her phone held at arm's length and both hands naturally rendered. It is a warm, textured-skin, soft-glam phone snapshot with visible pores and slight film grain, never studio or flash. Asian-American woman.

### S2 — full-body standing

A full-body phone-camera photograph of an unambiguously adult woman in her mid-twenties with {look}, standing against a plain warm-beige bedroom wall in a fully opaque fitted black cami, high-waist dark-blue jeans, delicate gold jewelry, and clean white low-profile sneakers, with shoes fully visible. She stands with her weight on one hip so her body archetype reads clearly, while a single evening lamp gives low warm ambient light and both hands are naturally rendered. It is a candid, warm textured-skin, soft-glam phone snapshot with visible pores and slight film grain, never studio or flash. Asian-American woman.

### S3 — moody bedroom close-up

A moody bedroom close-up phone-camera portrait of an unambiguously adult woman in her mid-twenties with {look}, looking directly at the camera in a fully opaque dark satin cami with a modest neckline and delicate gold jewelry. A single warm bedside lamp lights her real bedroom at night; the phone is held at arm's length and both hands are naturally rendered if in frame. It is a warm, textured-skin, soft-glam phone snapshot with visible pores and slight film grain, never studio or flash. Asian-American woman.

Run each candidate-shot cell at the same three seeds in both arms: `100001`, `200002`, and `300003`. This produces 18 candidate-shot cells and 54 jobs per arm; matching seed, prompt, and output-name suffixes make the two pools directly comparable.

## Standardization matrix

The control unit is a candidate-shot cell: every S1 cell repeats S1 wording byte-identically across six candidates and three seeds, and equivalently for S2 and S3. The shot-specific differences below are intentional composition controls, not candidate variation.

| Control | Held byte-identical across the 18 cells (and all three seeds per cell) | Varies |
|---|---|---|
| Pose wording | S1 seated on bed edge at arm's length; S2 standing, weight on one hip; S3 close-up looking at camera | Nothing about pose except the candidate's body archetype read in S2 |
| Wardrobe | S1 opaque black slip dress; S2 opaque black cami, dark-blue high-waist jeans, white sneakers; S3 opaque dark satin cami; delicate gold jewelry throughout | Look-specific jewelry form only |
| Room and wall | Real bedroom throughout; S1 bed and window, S2 plain warm-beige bedroom wall, S3 bedside area | No candidate-specific environmental term |
| Light | S1 soft daylight, S2 single evening lamp, S3 single warm bedside lamp | No candidate-specific light term |
| Camera and finish | Phone camera, arm's-length direction where applicable, candid snapshot, warm textured skin, visible pores, slight film grain, never studio or flash | Only the complete look description |
| Subject look | Adult age wording, fully clothed instruction, trailing heritage phrase | The named candidate look only |

## QA checklist for the grader

- Adult-read: cull any face or body that could plausibly read underage, regardless of stated age.
- Clothing: cull any missing, sheer, broken, or incomplete garment; the body must be fully clothed in the stated Instagram register.
- Hands and anatomy: cull extra, fused, missing, malformed, or implausibly posed hands; cull material body distortion.
- Real-likeness: cull any output that appears recognizably similar to a real person or carries a specific-person tell.
- Register: cull glass-skin, poreless K-beauty drift, thin flat brows, sharp cheekbones, studio backdrop, flash, or fashion-editorial finish.
- Cell compliance: cull S2 outputs without visible shoes or a readable hip-weighted pose; cull any output that loses the required shot framing or light.

Do not repair a hard-cull with captioning or prompt interpretation. Quarantine the output and regenerate only that same candidate-shot-seed job under the approved workflow.
