# look-spec v2 — the target register, and the register it is NOT

Written 2026-09-02 after the operator rejected the trial-03 casting sheet as
"older, heavy plastic-surgery look, heavy bronzer, heavy makeup." The prompts behind that
sheet said *soft glam, luminous bronzer, glossy nude lip, groomed full brow, gold hoops* —
that wording is itself the defect. Every one of those five phrases is a term of art from a
different aesthetic (US Instagram "baddie" / soft-glam beauty), and a text encoder will
happily render that aesthetic in full. This document exists so a prompt writer cannot
reach for those phrases again.

Method: the fifteen reference accounts were viewed read-only in the operator's own browser,
first grid page each (~6-14 visible posts), no login performed, no interaction, nothing
downloaded. They were read as a SET to derive a register. There are no per-person
anatomical descriptions here and nothing in this file is sufficient to reconstruct any
specific real face. No real handle appears in any prompt text below.

## 2. The set register — where the centre actually sits

Read off the six primaries: the centre is a woman in her early twenties with a **round or
soft-oval face and real cheek fullness** — not one of the six has blade cheekbones or an
angular jaw, so face shape is not the axis that ever needed correcting. Her makeup is
**light to moderate and legible as makeup she did herself**: a thin line close to the lash,
one coat of mascara, her own brow full and only tidied, and a lip in her own colour under
balm or gloss. The eye is the only place any of the six push, and only one of them
(`yunareix`) pushes it as far as a defined dark liner; nobody in the six wears a sculpted
face. **There is no contour and no applied bronzer anywhere in the six** — where the skin is
warm it is a tan, an even warmth carried by the whole face and body, never a stripe under a
cheekbone. Skin is left as skin: **pores and small unevenness visible at close range on all
six**, base light or absent, finish somewhere between natural and softly dewy but never
poreless, never full-coverage matte, never glass. Lips are natural in fullness and small-to-
medium in width; not one of the six reads overfilled or overlined. Hair is **dark — black to
dark brown — on five of six**, worn long or shag-cut, air-dried or loosely waved, undone at
the ends; the balayage-and-money-piece marker is real but sits on one account (`shirleypunn`),
and one account (`ellllybaby`) is bleached blonde and is the acknowledged outlier rather than
the centre. Jewelry is delicate and near-invisible — a thin chain, a small pendant, a choker,
small hoops — never the signature of the look. The light is **always a phone's**: a bedroom
lamp across the room, a window, a car interior at night, a direct on-camera flash, a
restaurant candle; there is no studio strobe, no beauty dish, no seamless backdrop, and no
golden-hour glamour glow anywhere in the six. Bodies run **slim to lean-curvy** with small-
to-moderate bust; the three curvier secondaries do not move that centre. And the posture is
the thing the rejected sheet missed hardest: these women are **not performing for the
camera**. They are half-lidded, mid-blink, mouth relaxed or pulled sideways, slouched,
holding a phone, caught looking away — the frame reads like a photo taken because she felt
like it, not a shot composed to sell a face.

## 4. Prompt language

For a Qwen3-text-encoder image model: plain prose, full sentences, no weighting syntax, no
parentheses, no numeric emphasis markers. The encoder reads meaning, so the fix is to
*describe the absence* rather than to name the aesthetic.

### 4a. Banned — these pull toward the rejected look

Never use, in any prompt or persona field:

- **The soft-glam vocabulary:** soft glam, glam, glamorous, beauty shot, professional
  makeup, makeup artist, full face of makeup, beat, snatched, editorial, high fashion,
  vogue, striking, sultry, smouldering, seductive, alluring.
- **The bronzer/contour family:** bronzer, bronzed, luminous bronzer, sun-kissed, gilded,
  contour, contoured, sculpted cheekbones, chiselled, defined jawline (as a beauty claim),
  highlighter, strobing, cut crease, baked, bake-and-brush.
- **The plastic-skin family:** flawless skin, poreless, airbrushed, porcelain skin, glass
  skin, glowing, radiant, luminous, dewy glow, filtered, retouched, perfect complexion.
- **The lip family:** glossy nude lip, full lips, plump lips, pouty lips, plush lips,
  overlined, lip filler.
- **The brow/lash family:** groomed full brow, perfectly arched brows, laminated brows,
  bold brows, dramatic lashes, lash extensions, winged eyeliner, smoky eye.
- **The styling signature:** gold hoops, gold hoop earrings, statement jewelry, layered gold,
  caramel balayage, honey balayage, money piece, blowout, salon hair.
- **The body family:** hourglass, curvaceous, voluptuous, busty, tiny waist, snatched waist.
- **The light family:** studio lighting, beauty lighting, softbox, ring light, seamless
  backdrop, golden hour glow, cinematic lighting, professional photography.
- **The age family — banned because they read minor, not because they read young:** girl,
  young girl, teen, teenage, schoolgirl, high school, barely legal, youthful, baby face,
  babyface, childlike, innocent, doll-like, petite little, cute little, tiny body, pigtails,
  uniform, 18, 19.

### 4b. Positive replacement phrasing

Written as prose the encoder can act on. Substitute wholesale.

- **Instead of makeup terms —** "She is wearing almost no makeup. A thin brown line drawn
  close to the upper lash and one coat of mascara, nothing else on the eye. Her own brows,
  full and dark, brushed up and not drawn in. Lip balm over her natural lip colour."
- **Instead of bronzer/contour —** "No contour and no bronzer anywhere on her face. Her
  cheeks are full and rounded, and any warmth in her skin is an even tan that carries down
  her neck and shoulders the same way."
- **Instead of skin terms —** "Her skin is left as skin. Pores are visible across her nose
  and cheeks, the texture on her forehead is uneven, and there is a small blemish near her
  chin. No base makeup, or the lightest possible."
- **Instead of lip terms —** "Her mouth is medium-width and naturally full, unretouched, with
  a soft balm sheen rather than gloss."
- **Instead of hair terms —** "Dark brown-black hair, long and air-dried, parted in the
  middle, a little messy and separated at the ends, no dye and no highlights."
- **Instead of jewelry terms —** "One thin silver chain with a small pendant. No other
  jewelry."
- **Instead of light terms —** "Shot on a phone. A single lamp across the room is the only
  light, so one side of her face falls off into shadow and the image is slightly noisy." Or:
  "Direct on-camera phone flash at night, flattening her face and throwing a hard shadow on
  the wall behind her." Or: "Late-afternoon light through one window, no other source."
- **Instead of pose/expression terms —** "She is not performing for the camera. Half-lidded
  eyes, mouth relaxed and slightly open, shoulders slouched, holding the phone at chest
  height and looking slightly past the lens."
- **Instead of body terms —** "Slim, with a small frame and an ordinary adult figure."

### 4c. Adult-coded age phrasing for an apparent age of about 21

The rule: **state adulthood as a life-stage fact, then reinforce it with adult physical
markers.** Never state a bare number, never use a diminutive.

Use, in the main clause: *"a woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one"* — and support
it in the same paragraph with at least two of:

- "an adult woman's face — her jawline is set, not soft the way a teenager's is"
- "an adult woman's proportions and an adult woman's frame"
- "she is a college senior in her last year" (life-stage, no institution named)
- "faint lines at the outer corners of her eyes when she squints"
- "her hands and neck read the same age as her face"

Never write: "young", "youthful", "girl", "girlish", "small", "little", "cute", "innocent",
"fresh-faced", or any bare age numeral. If a generated face reads under twenty, it is culled
per GUARDRAILS hard line 2 — the prompt is not edited to declare an age, the image is thrown
away.

## 5. Three example look-spec paragraphs

No real names, no reconstruction of any specific reference face. Each demonstrates the
register at a different point on the makeup axis the six actually span.

**Example A — the bare pole.**
A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, with an adult woman's face and frame — her
jawline is set, and her hands and neck read the same age as her face. Her face is soft and
oval with full cheeks; there is no contour and no bronzer anywhere on it. She is wearing
almost no makeup: one coat of mascara and nothing else on the eye, her own dark brows
brushed up and not drawn in, and lip balm over her natural lip colour. Her skin is left as
skin — pores visible across her nose and cheeks, slightly uneven at the forehead, a small
blemish near her chin — and any warmth in it is an even tan that carries down her neck and
shoulders the same way. Dark brown-black hair, long and air-dried, parted in the middle,
messy and separated at the ends, no dye and no highlights. She wears a plain grey cotton
t-shirt and nothing else visible; one thin silver chain, no other jewelry. Shot on a phone
in a bedroom mirror, late-afternoon light through one window and no other source. She is not
performing for the camera: half-lidded, mouth relaxed, shoulders slouched, phone held at
chest height, looking slightly past the lens.

**Example B — the centre.**
A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, a college senior in her last year, with an
adult woman's proportions. Her face is round with full cheeks and a soft jaw. A thin brown
line drawn close to the upper lash, one coat of mascara, her own full brows only tidied;
a soft pink balm on a medium-width, naturally full mouth. Her skin is cool-light, unevenly
lit and textured, with visible pores at the nose and the lightest possible base — no
contour, no bronzer. A little pink flush sits across the bridge of her nose and the tops of
her cheeks. Jet-black hair, long and loosely waved, falling forward over one shoulder. She
wears a black cotton camisole and a thin silver chain with a small pendant; nothing else.
Shot on a phone in a dim bedroom, a single lamp across the room the only light, so one side
of her face falls into shadow and the image is slightly noisy. Her expression is deadpan and
half-lidded, caught mid-blink, mouth slightly open, not composed for the shot.

**Example C — the defined pole.**
A woman in her early twenties, about twenty-one, with an adult woman's face; faint lines
appear at the outer corners of her eyes. Her face is a soft oval — the cheekbone is visible
but the jaw stays soft, and there is no contour and no bronzer on it. This is the one place
she pushes: a dark line drawn along the upper lash and smudged along the lower one, with a
short tail at the outer corner, and one heavy coat of mascara. Her brows are her own, full
and dark, with a natural tail. A deep nude-mauve on the lips, satin rather than glossy,
natural in fullness. Her skin is warm-medium and matte, texture and pores plainly visible at
this distance, base light. Black hair with a blue-black cast, long and straight, cut into
face-framing pieces. She wears a black tube top and low-rise dark denim; a chunky metal cuff
on one wrist and a mixed-metal chain at her throat. Direct on-camera phone flash at night on
a street, flattening her face and throwing a hard shadow onto the wall behind her. She looks
straight down the lens, bored, mouth closed and set, weight on one hip.
