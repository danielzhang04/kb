## 1. Named operators

### Aitana López — The Clueless (Barcelona, founded 2023 by Rubén Cruz; cofounder Diana Núñez)
- Origin story: agency founder was short on modeling clients, built his own AI model to shoot for brand work instead.
- Scale: ~343,000 Instagram followers (as of late 2024/2025 coverage); reported $11,000 in a single month (Fortune/UNILAD, 2023); average cited around €3,000/month, up to €10,000/month; ~€1,000+ per branded post; became face of sports-supplement brand "Big."

- Disclosure: openly acknowledged as AI by the agency; Núñez says followers stayed loyal after the reveal.
- Agency roster (same shop, same business model reused across personas): **Maia Lima** (Argentine persona), **Alba Renai** (European fashion persona, 100K+ followers, positioned as a complementary "sister" model to Aitana for coordinated campaigns) — evidence the agency's model is a persona factory, not a one-off.

### Fanvue — platform-level disclosure program, not a single operator
- Explicit "AI Creator" designation with a signup-time checkbox declaring AI involvement; policy requires AI status stated in bio, and creators/personas may not claim to be a real human in posts or DMs.
- Provides its own in-house generative tooling ("Fanvue AI" tools) for creators to build/run AI personas rather than requiring an external stack.
- Platform economics (2026, self-published/press-sourced): ~$200M annualized run rate (doubled in 5 months as of July 2026 per company announcement); ~17M monthly active users; ~325,000 creators; AI-generated content ≈15% of total platform revenue; 93% of creators use at least one of Fanvue's proprietary AI tools, and the 72% of earners who use AI tooling earn 3–6x more than those who don't (Fanvue's own figures — credibility caveat: vendor-published, not independently audited).
- Stated realistic AI-creator earnings curve per Fanvue's own guidance: $0–500/mo (months 1–3) → $500–2,000/mo (months 4–6) → $2,000–10,000/mo (months 6–12, at 200–500 active subscribers).
- Sources: [Fanvue Help Centre](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538752-what-is-an-ai-generated-model), [Yahoo Finance/H1 announcement](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/h1-fanvue-doubles-200m-run-100800432.html), [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanvue/), [Fanvue blog](https://www.fanvue.com/blog/how-much-do-content-creators-make)

## 4. Published stack patterns — what recurs

Across every operator and tool researched, the same shape keeps repeating:

1. **Image model**: Stable Diffusion (still the base for most solo/DIY operators) or Flux (newer projects, cited heavily in 2026 ComfyUI/consistency guides) — either via CivitAI-sourced checkpoints or a platform's proprietary model.
2. **Consistency method**: two competing approaches, both widely documented —
   - **LoRA training** on the base model (the "classic" DIY approach: 20+ reference images → trained LoRA → trigger word yields consistent character across prompts/scenes). Small file (50–200MB), portable across base-model updates.
   - **Face-swap post-processing** (ReActor / FaceDetailer in ComfyUI): generate a scene with any tool, then swap in a stored reference face — guarantees facial accuracy independent of the base generation.
   - Platform-native black-box equivalents (Higgsfield's **Soul ID**) package the same idea (train once on reference photos, reuse identity token everywhere) as a no-prompt, GUI-driven product.
3. **Video generation**: increasingly separated from image generation as its own model/step (Higgsfield, Eromify, Sora/Midjourney cited per third-party Fanvue coverage) — script-to-video is now a first-class product feature, not an afterthought.
4. **Monetization platform**: Fanvue is the dominant named destination for the paid/NSFW-adjacent tier across nearly every operator in this research (Aitana, Emily Pellegrini, Hailey Lopez); Instagram/TikTok/X serve as the free top-of-funnel audience-building layer, with brand deals monetizing the SFW public feed and Fanvue monetizing the gated/intimate tier.
5. **Disclosure stance splits cleanly two ways**: (a) lead-with-it personas (Milla Sofia, Fanvue's platform-level AI Creator badge) that make transparency part of the brand story, vs. (b) reveal-after-the-fact personas (Aitana, most Fanvue solo operators) where the creator stays anonymous/"secretive" (a word used repeatedly in press coverage) until press or virality forces the reveal — and the reveal itself has not visibly hurt engagement (Aitana's cofounder specifically notes followers stayed loyal).
6. **Agency-vs-solo scale pattern**: agencies (The Clueless) build a *roster* of complementary personas (Aitana + Maia Lima + Alba Renai) to spread brand-deal surface area and de-risk any single persona's virality, whereas solo operators (Milla Sofia, Hailey Lopez) run one persona end-to-end themselves. Fanvue's own numbers suggest the solo/small-operator base ($500–$10K/mo trajectory) is the volume tier, while the headline $20K+/mo outliers are the visible top of a much larger unremarkable middle.
7. **Tooling is bifurcating**: dedicated all-in-one SaaS (Glambase, Foxy, Eromify, Higgsfield) is absorbing the DIY ComfyUI/LoRA workflow into a no-prompt GUI product, while the monetization platform itself (Fanvue) is now building/acquiring the generation layer in-house rather than leaving it to third parties — vertical integration is the visible trend line into 2026.

## 5. Concrete lift candidates for figment

1. **Higgsfield Soul ID** (https://higgsfield.ai/ai-influencer) — most directly relevant consistency product to study/benchmark against for a disclosed-persona pipeline; GUI-driven identity token is a faster iteration loop than manual LoRA training if figment needs non-technical persona designers.
2. **CivitAI** (https://civitai.com) + its "Build an AI Influencer" guide (https://education.civitai.com/build-an-ai-influencer-with-civitai-com/) — the open-source substrate (Flux/SD checkpoints + LoRAs) worth adopting directly for full pipeline control instead of a closed SaaS; also the best free source of prior art on character LoRAs.
3. **ComfyUI + ReActor/FaceDetailer face-swap workflow** (per MyAIForce guides, e.g. https://myaiforce.com/face-swapping-in-comfyui-with-lora/) — a complementary consistency technique to LoRA training worth having in the toolkit for salvaging otherwise-good generations.
4. **Fanvue's AI Creator disclosure model** (https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538752-what-is-an-ai-generated-model) — closest existing precedent for a disclosed-AI monetization/compliance pattern (bio disclosure + no-real-person-claims policy); worth mirroring the disclosure copy/UX pattern even if figment doesn't use Fanvue as the platform.
5. **The Clueless multi-persona roster model** (Aitana/Maia/Alba Renai) — a business-model pattern worth lifting: build 2–3 complementary personas under one production pipeline rather than betting everything on one account, per https://www.midiaresearch.com/case-studies/the-clueless-agency-accelerating-virtual-influencer-growth.
6. **Eromify's credit-based pricing model** (https://www.eromify.in/pricing) — a concrete, working monetization structure (pay-per-generation credits, non-expiring) worth benchmarking if figment considers productizing its own pipeline for other operators later.
7. **Glambase's bundled lifecycle** (generation → autonomous posting → monetization dashboard, https://unrealspeech.com/ai-apps/glambase) — a reference architecture for what an "agency in a box" product needs to bundle if figment ever externalizes tooling, not just personas.
8. **Milla Sofia's transparency-first brand playbook** (https://millasofia.eth.limo/) — concrete copy/positioning example for a persona that discloses AI status on first contact and was praised for it, directly relevant to figment's disclosed-AI mandate.
9. **Fiverr "profit-ready AI influencer" gig category** (search: "AI OnlyFans model Fiverr") — worth a scan as a cheap outsourcing/benchmarking option for image-pack production if figment needs to spot-check its own pipeline's cost/quality against market rate (~$495 seen for a full persona build).
10. **BlackHatWorld AI-influencer subforum threads** (e.g. thread 1604877, blocked from direct fetch here — revisit via a non-restricted fetch path) — likely the single richest source of unfiltered operator numbers/tactics (SEO/growth-hacker culture over-shares stack details and revenue); worth a dedicated follow-up pull.
