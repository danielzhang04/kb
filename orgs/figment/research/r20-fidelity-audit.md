# R20 — fidelity audit: package vs. our port, stage by stage

Read-only. Ties `research/10sorlabs-package/` (modules 02–11, JSON+`.bat`+`transcript.txt`), the
claim-checked `r15/r15b/r16` reports, and our port (`pipeline/expand/`, `pipeline/train/`,
`personas/creator-001/`) together into one gap list against the operator's tester-board verdict
("kind of close, glossy, reads a lot older, some inconsistent"). No recommendations beyond the
ranked list at the end, per brief.

**Anchor correction to the brief:** `identity-spec.md`:1–4 and `train/runs/creator-001-expansion-01-README.md`:3
both call g01/g02/g07 "**fictional, operator-owned** face anchors" from `personas/anchors/gemini-batch-01/`
— AI-generated (Gemini/Nano-Banana), not photographs of a real person. This matters below: the
package's own anchor is *also* synthetic, but generated by their own pipeline (§1).

## Ranked divergences most likely to explain the symptoms

1. **No FaceDetailer/SAM face-repair pass in generation (stage 7).** The package's only per-face,
   low-denoise (0.15) detail-recovery mechanism, dropped for a real licence reason (`.pt` pickle,
   no clean non-pickle Apache/MIT replacement found — r15 §2d, `TENSOR-TRAINING.md`:23-32) and not
   yet backfilled with `r16`'s own recommended substitute. **Best explanation for "glossy."**
2. **Anchor identity has none of the package's texture/skin LoRA lineage (stage 1).** The package
   bakes `realistic_snapshot_lora` into its own anchor and reinforces it with two more style LoRAs
   at generation; our anchor comes from an unrelated generator (Gemini/Nano-Banana) with no
   equivalent texture prior anywhere in the chain. **Best explanation for "reads a lot older"** (a
   flatter/smoother AI-default skin prior reading as an older, more "done" look, compounded by the
   missing FaceDetailer's fine-grain recovery).
3. **Full-body dataset cells with known mask-like face-swap risk went into training uncurated
   (stage 3).** Our own `identity-spec.md` predicts exactly this failure and prescribes a
   mitigation (half-body-first framing, second-pass face-crop edit for full-body) that was never
   applied to this dataset; all 31/31 raw images were used unfiltered. **Best explanation for
   "some inconsistent."**
4. **Checkpoint choice was not screened against the tester's own numbers (stage 6).** Own-anchor
   cosine peaks at step 1500 (0.894) and is already declining by step 2000 (0.880), the step
   actually used — the same eye/metric ranking method the package teaches (and we replicated) was
   not applied to select the checkpoint before generation.
5. **Single-word ("woman") captions instead of module 11's current descriptive VLM captions
   (stage 4)** — a documented, deliberate regression to a method the package itself retired.
6. **Both style LoRAs dropped at the dataset stage too (stage 2)**, so no stage in the whole
   pipeline ever reintroduces the package's texture look — same root cause as #2, compounding it.
7. **33% fewer training steps (2000 vs. 3000, stage 5)** — a budget-driven deviation; weak
   standalone explanatory power given #4 shows quality was already past peak before step 2000.
