## Local runtime contracts for reference-conditioned inference

The local OmniGen2 branch reuses the pair engine rather than adding a second executor. The engine accepts a closed set of extra Comfy launch flags, reference-staging and output-decoding hooks, a resource observer, and tighter readiness and row deadlines, while its default command and receipt keys stay unchanged. The engine is the single teardown owner; the observer only samples and reports. A read-only Windows observer supplies process commit, system commit headroom and free VRAM at a cooperative one-second cadence, retains a bounded sample set, and fails closed on invalid telemetry or capacity exhaustion. Launch and dispatch re-check deadlines immediately before acting, and late observer, stderr or evidence errors cannot become successful receipts. Teardown targets a 30-second acceptance window but is cooperative, not a guaranteed timer.

Above the engine sits a fixed, read-only admission validator and a thin controller. The validator reconstructs the admission canonically, loads the planner and preparer only from hash-checked bytes, and streams every weight in 1 MiB chunks.
