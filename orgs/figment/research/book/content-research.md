# Content research

Content research should answer which ideas deserve a controlled production test. Begin with a dated evidence packet: topic, audience assumption, source links, observed format, rights posture, and the decision it informs. Classify concepts by visual hook, narrative promise, production cost, and review risk. Separate an idea's popularity from its suitability for this persona.

The two growth SOP PDFs were subsequently read and summarized in [r15, section 5](../r15-10sorlabs-artefacts.md#5-module-15--the-two-growth-sops-as-derived-rules), superseding r14's earlier unavailable-source status. Their format and hook suggestions are hypotheses; no account analytics accompanied them. Device resets, proxy rotation, account farms and engagement manipulation are excluded from Figment. Instagram integration is deferred. The hub can record hypotheses and outcomes from permitted sources, but it must never invent views, retention, shares, or conversion. Metrics need a denominator, observation window, platform context, and source receipt.

Use a research queue with one hypothesis per card: “This hook improves completion among the defined audience under this format.” Pre-register the asset, caption family, posting window, and comparison. Record null results and confounders such as distribution, novelty, and account history. Keep adult, clothed presentation and disclosure requirements in the content brief before production begins.
